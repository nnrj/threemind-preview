## Conditions and observations

After receiving relation stimuli such as “A is pine-nut” and “B is bell,” the network was probed with shorter inputs such as “A” and “B.”

1. “A” repeatedly reactivated an anonymous response set close to the earlier “A is pine-nut” response. A local route was therefore revisitable while it remained retained.
2. After the second relation was introduced, “B” sometimes produced a mixed label projection containing elements of both relations. This is first evidence of **cross-history spillover or over-association**, not relational inference.
3. Repeated structures appeared during intervals without manual text input. Because the environmental sensory adapter may continuously provide external background stimulation, this must be described as background-driven reactivation rather than input-free autonomous activity.
4. Strict deduplication of single-frame outputs would discard evidence about persistence, re-entry, and decay. Repetition is itself a dynamical measurement.

## Corrected interpretation under the current implementation

ThreeMind does not locate nodes by matching plaintext strings. Labels are external annotations; direct contact, propagation over existing directed edges, plasticity, forgetting, and optional local inhibition determine the response. The result is compatible with history-dependent local reactivation, but it does not establish syntax nodes, a predeclared attention module, a semantic knowledge graph, or subjective thought.

## Required follow-up

- From one snapshot, repeat target, unrelated, and character-frequency-matched shuffled probes; compare anonymous-set Jaccard similarity, activation integral, duration, and convergence latency.
- Repeat with background stimulation disabled to separate residual internal propagation from ongoing external input.
- Compare one-relation, two-relation, swapped-binding, and label-permutation conditions to quantify interference.
- Preserve raw per-tick records; display deduplication must never delete persisted evidence.

## Interpretation boundary

The defensible result is that an established local response could be revisited by a related probe and showed measurable cross-relation spillover. This is an experimental object for association specificity and interference, not proof of semantic understanding.
