# 005 — Plastic and Repeatable Convergence Mapping under V3 Probe Stimulation

## Experimental purpose

Controlled probe stimuli were used to test whether the network could form a plastic and repeatable mapping from stimulus, through a dynamical response, to a final converged state. The experiment also examined whether local inhibition allowed activity to converge through propagation and competition within a finite number of ticks rather than expanding indefinitely or saturating the network immediately.

## Observations

1. The current model formed a repeatable stimulus-to-convergence mapping. Reapplying a related probe produced a corresponding internal response and eventually a converged state that remained stably associated with that input under the tested conditions.
2. After inhibition was introduced, activation did not present its final result instantaneously. It underwent local propagation, competition, decay, and reordering across a finite interval before converging.
3. Under the tested conditions, inputs of the same kind remained stably associated with their final convergence outcomes. Consecutive ticks formed a continuous temporal evolution rather than unrelated cross-sections.
4. The temporal-convergence readout repeatedly detected this process and preserved external observations such as the principal nodes, observation window, and convergence classification.

## Instrument boundary of plaintext labels

Plaintext labels served as an observation instrument, not as computational content:

- the large characters in passive and temporal readouts are external display labels associated with anonymous nodes, used to make experimental regularities visible to a human observer;
- the anonymous node identities in the smaller grey text, together with their per-tick activation, inhibition, contact, and connectivity states, are the actual objects of computation and convergence analysis;
- labels do not participate in Core propagation, association, inhibition, forgetting, consolidation, or edge formation;
- readable text is therefore a diagnostic projection of anonymous dynamics and cannot replace the anonymous record as primary scientific data.

## Relation to the working hypothesis

The observation supports a weaker, testable proposition: a continuously plastic network can form history-dependent internal structure under stimulation; a later related stimulus can revisit that structure; and local propagation plus inhibition can drive the response through temporal evolution into a stable and repeatable state. This is compatible with the ThreeMind working hypothesis concerning continuity, association, consolidation, and emergence from local rules.

Within the present evidence, these dynamics may be described as having formal features reminiscent of recall, association, and a thought-like process. They do not establish sentence comprehension, subjective experience, or artificial consciousness.

## Falsification criteria and required controls

To elevate the observation into evidence suitable for a paper, the following are required:

1. From the same initial snapshot, repeat target stimuli and matched unrelated stimuli, then compare converged node sets, anonymous signatures, latency, duration, and activation integrals.
2. Compare paired inhibition-off and inhibition-on conditions for propagation range, convergence rate, oscillation rate, and final response specificity.
3. Replicate across fresh consciousnesses, sensory seeds, and label permutations. Conclusions must follow anonymous node trajectories rather than readable text.
4. Preserve stimulus envelopes, model parameters, adapter registry state, per-tick dynamics, and readout configuration for independent audit.
5. If related inputs do not significantly increase recurrence of the same anonymous response, or unrelated inputs enter that state equally often, the stimulus-specific convergence interpretation must be rejected or narrowed.

## Interpretation boundary

This entry records a plastic and repeatable stimulus–convergence correspondence under the current configuration and instrumentation. It is a dynamical result that warrants strict replication, not proof of semantic understanding or subjective consciousness.
