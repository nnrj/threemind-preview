# 007 — From Snapshot Readout to Temporal Evidence

[中文](../zh/007_2026-08-02_1246_从截面读出到时间区间证据.md) | [English](007_2026-08-02_1246_from-snapshot-readout-to-temporal-evidence.md)

**Observed:** 2026-08-02 12:46–15:49  
**Model and instruments:** V3; single-tick passive readout and temporal-window convergence readout

## Observation

Probes corresponding to “A is,” “who is A,” and “pine-nut” produced different principal node sets and rankings in a single-tick cross-section versus a continuous time window. “Who is A” did not necessarily converge reliably to “pine-nut” in this run. The earlier relation-local response therefore cannot be equated with question answering or relational inference.

The temporal readout began measuring active duration, longest consecutive run, peak, mean, activation integral above baseline, inhibition, direct/associative contact, tail persistence, and set stability. The observation target consequently changed from “what does one frame resemble?” to “how does the network evolve after a stimulus, and does it converge?”

## Methodological significance

A high value in one frame may be a transient peak. A node ranked highly for several ticks may reflect a stable response, slow residual decay, or periodic oscillation. Raw trajectories are required to distinguish them. Plaintext labels remain display instrumentation; anonymous identities, edges, and numerical trajectories constitute auditable evidence.

## Interpretation boundary

This entry is not a new proof of semantic ability. It records a measurement correction: future claims about recall, response, or convergence must specify the observation window, baseline, persistence, stability, and controls against oscillation and residual decay.
