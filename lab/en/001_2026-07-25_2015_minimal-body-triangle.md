# 001 — Minimal Body Adapter and a Repeatable Three-Node Structure

[中文](../zh/001_2026-07-25_2015_最小躯体三角结构.md) | [English](001_2026-07-25_2015_minimal-body-triangle.md)

**Date:** 2026-07-25  
**Status:** preliminary observation; controls pending

## Conditions

A newly created anonymous spatial consciousness received only continuous weak
background stimulation. The body adapter was enabled. At this time its
background sensor projected the outside field into three persistent anonymous
channels: slow, fast, and drift. It applied sampling, thresholding, adaptation,
and small amplitude noise before publishing a `SensoryFrame` to the input bus.

## Observation

The visual projection rapidly showed three activated nodes with a stable
triangle-like local arrangement. The passive output observer later reported a
repeatable anonymous structural signature. Across consciousness instances, the
screen-space geometry can differ because each instance has a different sensory
seed and spatial projection, while the constrained channel count remains the
same.

## Interpretation

This is consistent with the working hypothesis that a persistent sensory body
helps determine which internal mappings are likely to form: a narrow, stable
sensory projection repeatedly co-activates a narrow set of receptors, and the
associative runtime can consolidate a matching local structure.

The observation is **not** evidence by itself of a message, a concept, a
semantic triangle, or consciousness. The triangle is a visualization of a
small topology; visual geometry must be distinguished from graph topology.

## Important implementation fact

The observed structure is not a Core rule that creates triangles. It follows
from the then-current minimal body condition: three recurring sensory channels
can create at most three background receptor identities. The body adapter is
therefore an experimental constraint, not a neutral background generator.

## Required controls

1. Compare the minimal three-channel body with a dynamic multi-channel ambient
   adapter under otherwise identical Core parameters.
2. Sweep channel count, sampling interval, threshold, adaptation, and noise.
3. Repeat across independently generated seeds and record receptor count,
   graph topology, recurrence, persistence, and decay after source removal.
4. Disable the body adapter or route the same environment through an alternate
   adapter, rather than treating one body as the universal baseline.

## Next action

Introduce an adapter registry and adapter-management UI. This will make the
body condition explicit, configurable, switchable, and reproducible without
putting sensory semantics into the Core or the input bus.
