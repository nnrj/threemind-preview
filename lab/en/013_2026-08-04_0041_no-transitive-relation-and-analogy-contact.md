# 013 — No Transitive Relation Emerged; Candidate Analogy Contact

[中文](../zh/013_2026-08-04_0041_传递关系未涌现与类比触达.md) | [English](013_2026-08-04_0041_no-transitive-relation-and-analogy-contact.md)

**Observed:** 2026-08-04 00:41  
**Question:** after learning one father–grandfather example, would the network form an unstated grandfather relation for a second pair of father relations?

## Conditions

One stimulus set contained “A is B's father,” “B is C's father,” and the explicit “A is C's grandfather.” A second set contained “D is E's father” and “E is F's father,” but never a D–F grandfather statement.

## Observation

D/F probes stably recovered mainly the trained father relations; no stable “grandfather” response appeared. A complete D-set father sentence briefly contacted nodes previously associated with A/B/C/grandfather, but those nodes exited during temporal competition and did not enter the final convergence set.

## Conclusion

This is an important negative result. There is no evidence that the network derived the D–F grandfather relation. The transient cross-set contact is better treated as structural-similarity or analogy contact between a father pattern and a previous grandfather template, not transitive inference.

## Stronger success criterion

Candidate transitive inference would require blinded repeated untrained D/F relation probes from the same snapshot to produce significant, stable joint convergence of the relevant anonymous structures, exceeding character-frequency, swapped-binding, unrelated-kinship, and untrained baselines.

## Value

The negative result separates associative contact from relational inference and shows how a temporal readout can distinguish transient analogy contact from the final stable response.
