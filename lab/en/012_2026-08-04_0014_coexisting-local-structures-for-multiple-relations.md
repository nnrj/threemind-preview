## Observation

Different entity and relation probes reactivated different but partially overlapping local anonymous sets. Relation-word probes sometimes recovered labels of entities experienced with them. Labels from earlier history also appeared as cross-history spillover; the network did not generate those characters from nowhere.

## Corrected interpretation

The phenomenon is compatible with multiple history-dependent, relation-local subgraph candidates coexisting and being revisited through different entry points. It is not a database knowledge graph: Core has no explicit triples, predicate semantics, or query engine, and labels do not participate in dynamics. The readable projections therefore do not establish understanding of multiple relations.

## Follow-up

Compare exact anonymous subgraph identities, edge direction, role reversal, relation-label permutation, untrained combinations, matched unrelated relations, and replication across fresh consciousnesses. If local sets follow character frequency or position rather than changed bindings, relation specificity must be rejected.

## Interpretation boundary

This entry records coexistence and interference among several revisitable local responses. Structured relational representation remains a hypothesis.
