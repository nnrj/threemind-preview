# R51m: locating the cause of the changed response to inertial input

On 13 November 2026, the author requested this study immediately after the current R51k fixed-personal-readout experiment and before further revalidation. R51k's curriculum, readout conditions and stopping rules remain unchanged. The prepared 48-source R51l transfer confirmation is deferred; completed batches are not rerun.

Historical E18 used `continuous-memory/2` and found reverse process responses in 144 tests with inertial input. In the corresponding R51h tests, version 6.1 responded in the taught direction in all 144. The author proposed that slower decay prevents transient connections from disappearing promptly, changing the effect of inertial information. The author authorized a higher-deletion-threshold condition calibrated to approximate historical deletion speed, and investigation of other candidate causes.

The study will first align genuine kernel versions, anonymous stimuli, teaching order, bodily recordings, measurement windows and blank references, then freeze a bounded comparison. Higher deletion thresholds apply only to newly registered experimental subjects or causal copies; production source and version 6.1 defaults remain unchanged. Threshold selection uses independent structural calibration rather than formal response outcomes. Taught, reverse and unmeasurable responses are counted separately: eliminating responses by deleting all connections does not reproduce reversal.

This records authorization and scheduling, not results. The complete protocol, apparatus checks, originals and conclusions will be registered separately. If R51k triggers the campaign stopping rule, preserve and report that outcome before starting another batch.
