# Research motivations and references [Public edition: manuscript editing instructions omitted.]

14 November 2026. The author requested citations at the points where prior research actually motivated a hypothesis, experimental contrast or interpretation. Biological findings are not measurements of ThreeMind.

- Rao SG, Williams GV, Goldman-Rakic PS. Destruction and creation of spatial tuning by disinhibition: GABA_A blockade of prefrontal cortical neurons engaged by working memory. *J Neurosci*. 2000;20:485–494. [doi:10.1523/JNEUROSCI.20-01-00485.2000](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6774140/). GABA_A blockade predominantly removed existing spatial tuning, while creating tuning in some previously untuned cells. This motivates joint measurement of duration, selectivity and decisions, rather than equating more activity with better function. Cite in the persistent-activity rationale and discussion.
- Brooks JX, Cullen KE. The primate cerebellum selectively encodes unexpected self-motion. *Curr Biol*. 2013;23:947–955. [doi:10.1016/j.cub.2013.04.029](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6100740/). Different responses to self-generated and externally applied motion motivate the closed-loop body contrasts. Cite when introducing inertial reversal and active/passive perturbations.
- Compte A, Brunel N, Goldman-Rakic PS, Wang X-J. Synaptic mechanisms and network dynamics underlying spatial working memory in a cortical network model. *Cereb Cortex*. 2000;10:910–923. [doi:10.1093/cercor/10.9.910](https://www.cns.nyu.edu/wanglab/publications/pdf/compte2000.pdf). Joint excitatory and inhibitory organization of selective persistent activity motivates preserving inhibition and testing existing settings first. Cite in the design and mechanistic interpretation.
