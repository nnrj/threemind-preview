# R51t3: response inversion from one taught state and its closed-loop consequences

Preregistered on 14 November 2026. Four E18 coding/material blocks (930101–930104) use the ordinary configuration. Complete 6.1 references from R51m are retained. Conditions are nested causal copies, not new independent sources.

First, five taught/reference modes per block—full senses, joint-only, dose-matched, shuffled inertia and untrained—provide the same complete state at 20.2 seconds. Two test copies use transmitted-signal thresholds 0.0004 and 0.004. Both call the same public reconfiguration interface, controlling its same-time settlement. Post-intervention connections, node state and queued events must match apart from the threshold before claiming a same-state contrast. Each has positive-posture, negative-posture and blank diagnostics with the original 0.4-second stimulus and 0.6–5.6-second process score: 120 stages. Activation, inhibition, direct and associative activity are saved. At least three of four blocks must show taught responses at 0.0004, reversed responses at 0.004, and preserved taught joint-only responses. Failure narrows the earlier finding to a configuration effect including teaching.

Second, declared research copies receive the E18 MuJoCo pendulum with its original sensors and a new joint motor limited to 2 N m. This is an explicit body attachment, not continuation of a historical physical body. Initial angles are ±0.7 radians. Each run is continuous for eight seconds, with a 0.5 N m external torque in the initial-angle direction from 4.0 to 4.2 seconds. Objectives are reduced angle and speed; reversal is not designated correct.

A fixed output rule reads the preceding 0.5 seconds of the two taught-marker activation traces every 20 ms. Integral differences above ±10^-6 choose opposite motor directions; otherwise torque is zero. Both actuator polarities are tested and held identical across threshold comparisons. Only Core activity enters this controller. Zero actuation and a position/velocity feedback controller are apparatus references, not ThreeMind outputs. All four activity quantities and physical/action trajectories are retained.

Online sensory modes are full, joint-only, full scaled to joint-only per-frame dose, randomly permuted inertial values among the 12 inertial code positions each frame, and full for the untrained reference. Permutation preserves instantaneous inertial dose and saves RNG state. It is distinguished from the temporal shuffle used in historical teaching. Sensory intensity is 0.3 at 20-ms intervals, with independent background every 0.5 seconds. Test stimuli never include answer-marker channels.

The closed-loop comparison comprises 4×5×2×2×2=160 stages plus 16 apparatus references. Complete Core, input queue, observer, body, actions, readout history and RNG are saved. Each registered causal stage is internally continuous.

The primary measure is eight-second angle RMS; speed RMS, final-second error, absolute torque and switches are secondary. A threshold change qualifies as a benefit across actuator polarities only if it reduces angle RMS by at least 10% in at least three blocks without a corresponding >=10% worsening under the other polarity. A reversal of benefit with polarity establishes dependence on output interpretation. Improvement is not attributed specifically to inertial information unless real inertia outperforms dose-matched and shuffled controls. Stabilization and damping are reported separately. No newly trained controller is fitted to conceal a reversal.

Total: 296 stages, originals <=2 GiB, delivery allowance <=6 GiB. Separate apparatus material verifies complete physical restoration, observer invariance, action-to-sensation feedback and answer isolation. Resource and evidence rules follow the overall plan. Defaults and the main campaign remain unchanged.
