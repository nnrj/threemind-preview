# 14 November 2026: novelty timing and prefix discrimination

The initial contrasts show persistent activity in authentic kernel /2 but failure to distinguish both prefixes sharing B; historical /1 records distinguish them. Kernel /2 introduced `novelty_mode=arrival-window`, whereas `immediate` retains the earlier calculation timing. Both are existing public birth configurations. This motivates one bounded follow-up rather than a parameter search.

Two conditions are added: authentic /2 with immediate novelty and 6.1 with immediate novelty. Each uses the same two material blocks, with one continuous sequence-teaching stage and two diagnostic copies (AB and DB) per block: 12 scientific stages in total. They are compared with the same-version default conditions in the initial batch. Authentic /1 is supplied by the preceding 80-stage supplement. All other configurations, inputs, doses, timing, process measurements and thresholds remain unchanged. Both taught-ending margins must exceed 1e-6 in each block, and both blocks must pass for restoration.

If changing novelty timing alone restores /1-like prefix discrimination in /2, that change is sufficient to explain this early-version difference. Otherwise the single-factor restoration hypothesis fails. The 6.1 contrast independently tests whether the same change restores discrimination with the current remaining mechanisms. A benefit in /2 is not assumed to transfer to 6.1.

This follow-up is registered after inspecting the initial results and source code. It is a subsequent causal exploration, not an original preregistered hypothesis or an independent confirmation. Execution follows successful reproduction of authentic /1. An independent-code apparatus first checks continuous teaching, observation and full restoration for /2 immediate; the 12 scientific stages then run once. Material blocks and the overall 2-GiB originals budget are unchanged. The campaign stays paused and production defaults stay unchanged. No further novelty-parameter search follows this contrast.
