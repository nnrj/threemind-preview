# Preparation for E21 timing and reference experiments

14 November 2026. R51o state exchange is still running. This directory prepares the next component of the approved plan; it has not been registered or run and has no new scientific results.

Historical E21 compared dense repetition, sparse repetition and separate references in four anonymous material blocks. Dense teaching separated AX and BX by 50 ms and the start of successive pairs by 550 ms. Sparse teaching separated pair starts by 2.5 seconds, with within-pair intervals of 50, 500 or 2,000 ms. Shifting all teaching inputs by 0, 20, 40, 60 or 80 ms tests whether a course works only at a particular timing. Lesson count, dose, course endpoint and probe time remain matched within each course comparison.

The proposed replication retains 80 dense, 240 sparse and 32 separate-reference teaching courses: 352 continuous teaching references and 1,472 registered single-test diagnostic copies covering 1- and 30-second delays, cues and blanks. The independent material count remains four. The separate-reference course replaces AX/BX with AX/BZ and tests X and Z separately. It matches element count and total dose, while per-reference repetition and topology differ and will be described accordingly.

The materials tool matched all 88,720 input frames from 1,824 historical stages for codes, intensity, enqueue times, ordering and endpoint. Related historical files occupy 193,772,483 logical bytes. The prepared measurement uses five-second activation integrals and the historical bidirectional threshold of 1e−6 activation·seconds, with a 1e−9 numerical check and raw responses retained. It has not been applied to new individuals.

The formal protocol will be frozen after R51o delivery and its stopping decision. Apparatus equivalence under observation, save/restore and host pause, and a complete storage budget, precede one serial run. These preparation files do not constitute evidence of execution.
