# R51r: Formation and expression of bodily experience

14 November 2026. This preregistered E21C replication belongs to comprehensive revalidation plan v2 and follows verified R51q delivery without a stop condition. It retains four historical material blocks, 930101–930104, and two sensory conditions: complete bodily input and joint input without inertia. The genuine 6.1 implementation retains its default signal threshold of 0.0004, deletion threshold of 0.008, inverse-logarithmic decay and other rules.

## Questions and predictions

Historical E21C separated local mechanisms operating during teaching from those operating during testing. In the historical kernel, teaching with synaptic resource depletion and testing with depletion plus local input competition produced reversed responses. R51m has established that default 6.1 instead responds in the taught direction; raising the transmission signal threshold restored reversal. This batch uses default 6.1 to measure how teaching and test mechanisms affect response direction and magnitude.

There are two primary targets. First, with both mechanisms enabled throughout, recover the taught-direction responses found in R51m: both physical directions must pass in at least three of four blocks for each sensory condition. Second, with the other three switches enabled, separately disable teaching depletion, teaching competition, test depletion or test competition. These give eight primary comparisons across the two sensory conditions. At least one is expected to show a consistent effect, and every comparison receives its own conclusion. The remaining 56 simple effects and 16 interactions are also prespecified and reported separately.

For each comparison, average the two directional response margins within each block. An effect is detected when the enabled-minus-disabled difference has the same sign and exceeds 1e−6 activation-seconds in magnitude in at least three of four blocks. Otherwise that expectation is not met in this batch. Publish all four differences and their mean. The 256 cued responses are nested observations, not 256 independent replicates. Taught, reversed and unresolved directions remain distinct.

Failure to recover the default reference, or failure of all eight primary mechanism comparisons, requires completed verification and reporting followed by a campaign pause. An individual negative comparison is a normal result. Recreating the historical reversal cells is not a pass requirement: that directional change was already localized by R51m. Defaults will not be changed to obtain the old direction.

## Materials, subjects and interventions

Four blocks, two sensory conditions and four teaching switch combinations give 32 complete teaching references. Each continuously receives 32 bodily episodes of 0.6 seconds and then waits one second, reaching internal time 20.2 seconds. Historical bodily recordings, anonymous encodings, doses, simultaneous ordering, enqueue times and delivery times are preserved. All 32,544 inputs are checked before running.

The bodily signals are fixed replays of saved physical simulations. This batch introduces neither online bodily feedback nor a trainable motor adapter. The complete subject includes Core, the input bus, observer state and the executed environmental input record. Teaching sources and diagnostic copies are registered separately.

Each teaching reference supplies 16 registered diagnostic copies: four test switch combinations, two physical directions and either bodily input or a time-matched blank. Every copy undergoes one 5.6-second diagnostic, without A/B target markers in the physical cue. The batch contains 512 diagnostics, comprising 256 cued and 256 matched blank trials, and 544 stages overall. There are four material blocks. This is a causal comparison of teaching and test conditions, not an ordinary continuous ability examination.

`depletion_enabled` controls synaptic resource depletion. When disabled, available resource is restored to one before and after release; utilization and facilitation retain their implemented rules. `context_enabled` controls local competition when receiving signals. Neither switch denotes global energy supply or all short-term state. Teaching switches are set at birth. Test switches are set through the public API at the unchanged current internal time. Every diagnostic receives the same configuration call, including unchanged references. Complete before/after states, configurations and field differences are saved. The API also settles existing state to the current time and updates the associated timers; this behavior is part of the declared intervention.

## Process measurements and validation

A passive observer records every node's activation, inhibition, direct activation and associative activation every 20 milliseconds. The primary E21C measure is the trapezoidal integral over the full 5.6 seconds. Subtract the blank from the same parent, test configuration and physical direction, then subtract the other target from the taught target. Report the 0.6–5.6-second post-input window separately for comparison with R51m. Margins above 1e−6 indicate the taught direction; margins below −1e−6 indicate reversal; other cases are unresolved. Raw integrals, peaks, terminal activity and the other three activity fields are retained, so equal strong responses are not described as absent activity.

Independent apparatus checks use encoding seed 930195 and historical 930101 bodily recordings transformed by the original encoding rule. These checks are excluded from scientific scores. Each sensory condition receives an all-enabled teaching course. All four test configurations are then compared under normal, absent and sparse observation, complete save/restore and host pause; final state hashes must match. An unchanged configuration call is also compared with no call: activity differences at common sample times must remain below 1e−9. Blank trials compare the historical E21 enqueue schedule with R51m prequeueing and report differences. Formal trials always use the original E21 schedule.

Save teaching initial/final states, the state at 19.2 seconds, and diagnostic states before intervention, after intervention and at completion: 1,632 complete Core states. Input bus and observer states, stimuli, receipts, activities and interventions are also saved. Analysis is read-only and independently verifies integration with 45-digit decimal arithmetic. All-enabled formal cases are compared with corresponding R51m samples, with observation frequency and same-time settlement explicitly recorded. There is no trainable adapter here; adding correction or spaced review would change the controlled teaching course.
