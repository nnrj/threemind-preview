# E22 bodily state exchange and waiting: material preparation

14 November 2026. This work belongs to comprehensive revalidation plan v2. R51r bodily formation is still running. This directory only prepares subsequent materials and research tools; no new experiment has been registered or executed.

Original E22A reused four material blocks, two sensory conditions and two teaching-depletion settings, giving 16 references one second after teaching. Test configuration enabled depletion and local input competition. There were 128 persistent-state exchange conditions, each tested in two directions with corresponding no-cue controls: 512 diagnostics. Waiting to 10, 30 and 60 s added 48 holding copies and 192 diagnostics. All 752 stages and 17,888 inputs have been checked against the originals, including enqueue times, delivery times, dose and absence of target labels in probes. Related historical originals occupy 175,206,760 logical bytes. The material sample size remains four.

The 6.1 references will come from the corresponding complete R51r teaching states, brought to the common test configuration by the declared same-time configuration operation. Historical /2 states supply materials and historical outcomes only; they are not loaded into 6.1. The two inspected full-sensory teaching conditions for block 930101 have the same 12 nodes, 60 directed connections and configuration at 20.2 s, with 12 and five in-flight events respectively. Exchange must therefore preserve all recipient signals; the R51o tool requiring empty queues is unsuitable.

The prepared `intervention_active.py` supports only weights W, connection history S, node history N and their eight combinations. It retains recipient activation, inhibition, fatigue, synaptic resources, short-term state, input queue, transport records and emission schedules. Changes to persistent values update only the associated local deletion schedules under the existing 6.1 formula, with every change recorded. This is an explicit intervention on a research copy, not an observation tool or a production Core modification. References must have matching configuration, time, node addresses and directed topology. A mismatch requires investigation rather than silently adding connections.

Before formal execution, work remains on compatibility across all references, independent restoration/observation checks, field-level verification of all eight combinations, scientific targets and criteria, budgets, protocol and registration. The new study should determine how persistent connections and current state influence bodily responses, and how those responses change while waiting. Default 6.1 already differs from the old version in inertial response direction. Neither the old reversal nor historical scores may be substituted for new observations. Original scale and negative outcomes are retained.
