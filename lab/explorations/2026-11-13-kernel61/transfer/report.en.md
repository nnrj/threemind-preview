# R51j: local connection transfer changes selected door choices while preserving unrelated responses

13 November 2026. **All eight independent sources met the registered transfer target. Transplanting target connections changed 48/48 target choices to the donor's learned direction while preserving 48/48 unrelated choices. Replacing the original connections restored whole states and question-level responses.** This confirms selective causal control of actual choices by local experience-dependent structure in 6.1.

## Purpose and expectation

The study retained the eight-source scale of historical X1. It tested whether transferring local connections formed through experience makes a recipient adopt a donor's specific response, with consequences for behavior. Target transfer was expected to work; matched transfer was expected to leave target questions unchanged; sham intervention was expected to preserve responses; restoring original connections was expected to restore original responses. Persistent and fast states were also exchanged separately. The [preregistered protocol](protocol.en.md) fixed all conditions, thresholds and stopping rules.

## Method

Each source supplied two continuously taught parents with opposite correspondences. Channel roles and cross-copy alignment were defined before teaching. The 6.1 parents were saved at 18 internal seconds after 16 teaching rounds. Targets were the two directed edges from X/Y to answer channels A/B; matched connections ran from Z/W to A/B. Counts and degrees had to match, with weight and teaching-activity residuals within 0.02. Final-test answers were not used to select connections.

Each teaching history supplied seven research conditions: intact, sham, target bundle, matched bundle, target persistent package, target fast package and restoration. Bundles exchanged selected edges and their timers. Persistent transfers retained the recipient's fast fields; fast transfers retained its edge destinations and persistent weights. Nodes, fixed readout, body, random state and other connections remained intact.

The fixed A−B response selected one of two doors at 110 ms using thresholds of ±1e−4. That choice determined subsequent sensory input at 250 ms; a second action was recorded at 360 ms. Transferred responses therefore entered an action–perception loop. Only the first choice contributed to the primary score. Each condition contained target cues X, Y and XY, plus unrelated cues Z, W and ZW, giving 672 probes in eight independent sources.

The primary target required at least 6/8 sources to reach donor-consistent target accuracy of at least 75%, gain at least 50 percentage points over sham, unrelated loss of at most 10 percentage points, and target gain from matched transfer of at most 10 percentage points. Persistent and fast transfers were separately judged by at least 6/8 sources reaching 75% target accuracy.

## Results

Target questions below are scored against the donor's new direction; unrelated questions are scored against the recipient's original direction. The intact target score of 0/48 means that recipients retained their own learned direction.

| Condition | Donor-consistent target choices | Unrelated responses retained |
|---|---:|---:|
| Intact | 0/48 | 48/48 |
| Sham | 0/48 | 48/48 |
| **Target bundle transfer** | **48/48** | **48/48** |
| Matched bundle transfer | 0/48 | 0/48 |
| **Target persistent transfer** | **48/48** | **48/48** |
| Target fast-state exchange | 0/48 | 48/48 |
| Original connections restored | 0/48 | 48/48 |

The primary transfer target passed in 8/8 sources, with a mean gain of 100 percentage points and zero unrelated loss. Persistent transfer also passed in 8/8. Matched connections carried the other correspondence: their transfer changed unrelated responses without improving target scores. Changes therefore followed the correspondence whose connections were replaced.

In all 16 target-bundle exchanges, paired edges already had equal numerical states. The changes were their destination nodes and the corresponding timer endpoints. Persistent transfer produced the same effect. All 16 fast-state exchanges swapped identical fields and made no actual change; their transfer target passed in 0/8 sources. This condition supplies a zero-change reference rather than a contrast between different fast states.

Sham and restoration states and millisecond trajectories matched their intact references exactly. Maximum weight and teaching-activity residuals were 0.000591 and 0.000640, within the registered limits.

## Scientific conclusion and meaning for ThreeMind

**The learned choice correspondences in this experiment were carried by local connection destinations. Transferring those connections selectively transferred responses and door choices; replacing the original connections restored the original choices.** The fixed readout did not relearn, and untouched connections continued expressing unrelated relationships. The study completed its registered goal of selective local-structure transfer with behavioral consequences.

This is a favorable 6.1 replication. It extends the blocking evidence to reversible, selective behavioral transfer and identifies the fields that carried the effect in this batch. The primary and persistent-package targets passed. Fast-package transfer failed and was a zero-change condition. No campaign stopping rule was triggered; fixed personal readout and independent 48-source transfer confirmation can proceed.
