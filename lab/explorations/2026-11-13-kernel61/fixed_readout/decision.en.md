# M5 temporal readout: author decision and scope

13 November 2026. The author approved temporal readout as the primary condition and the original endpoint as a separate control. This explicitly authorizes the new M5 measurement condition; previous subjects, readouts, data and reports remain intact.

A personal linear readout will be calibrated from activation, inhibition, direct activation and associative activation over a fixed post-stimulus window. Its parameters will then be frozen to test expression of new relations learned by the Core. Activation at 110 milliseconds will be retained as the historical endpoint control. Full-activity readout, readout restricted to declared output ports and direct sensory readout will be scored separately.

The exploratory scale remains eight origins; the subsequent 48-origin confirmation will have a separate record. Temporal and endpoint readouts use the same individual's recorded activity, retaining their own weights, scales, calibration samples and accumulated regression state. No peripheral of an existing historical individual is replaced. The precise window, temporal bins, curriculum, correction limits and scoring rules will be frozen before registration. Neither condition may replace the other's registered outcome after results are seen.

This document records authorization and design boundaries, not an executed experiment or scientific finding. The 64-group autonomy confirmation continues; bodily transfer and local connection transfer are being prepared.
