The delivered 6.2 therefore retains arrival-window novelty, single-age inverse-logarithmic decay and the existing thresholds. New mechanisms are not enabled by default; compatible configurations passed complete-state comparison under identical inputs. The campaign remains paused. The table below concerns future use of changed candidate configurations, rather than mandatory repetition now.

## Configuration determines the affected claims

With arrival-window novelty, single-age retention, null signal-type overrides and all other parameters held equal, 6.2 provides a 6.1 compatibility configuration. Full-state comparisons test that adding a version has not changed that configuration's dynamics. They do not validate other configurations.

Immediate novelty changes when new connections are enhanced and can change what teaching leaves behind. Stability-based retention changes forgetting. A higher connection threshold or shorter inhibition decay changes propagation and responses. Scientific results affected by these choices need direct testing; the old scores cannot simply acquire a new version label.
