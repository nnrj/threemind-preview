# R51t11 follow-up amendment: identical complete configurations

15 November 2026, registered during phase A and before any follow-up phase.

The historical prefix task enables depletion and disables facilitation (101). The persistence task disables depletion and uses a one-second facilitation time constant (011). Applying only the same candidate inhibition rule and inhibition time constant would therefore not constitute an identical complete configuration. This difference was found while preparing the follow-up plan; the necessary comparison is specified here before execution.

Phases A and B remain unchanged. Phase C crosses these two existing resource configurations with the original inhibition time constant and one quarter of that value. Each of the four complete configurations is fixed at birth and tested on both the prefix curriculum and the persistence curriculum with its background replay. Prefix diagnostic copies retain the assigned resource configuration instead of being overwritten by the historical 101 settings. Teaching, waiting and recording retain continuous state.

The selected 101 prefix result at the original time constant is reused. The other three prefix conditions require 18 stages; four persistence conditions require 24 stages. Phase C thus adds at most 42 stages. With A (96), B (42) and new codes (6), the batch totals at most 186 stages, below its original cap of 192. With the preregistered fallback F (24), the total is at most 168. The original limits of 1 GiB of originals, 4 GiB of delivery space and two hours of scientific execution remain. Budget or resource violations pause at complete boundaries without automatic expansion.

New materials are fixed prospectively: curriculum 0 uses channel XOR mask 0x10000000 and birth seed 9291201; curriculum 1 uses mask 0x40000000 and seed 9291202. A configuration passing both tasks is preferred; ties retain 101 resources first, then the original inhibition time constant. If no configuration passes both tasks, a prefix-passing configuration is tested with the new codes, and the report explicitly states that the two capabilities were not obtained under one configuration. Recovery on historical materials and generalization are judged separately.

This amendment corrects the unexecuted follow-up design. It changes neither phase A data nor historical reports or success criteria. The primary objective remains an implementable recovery of prefix discrimination, accompanied by an accurate persistence test.
