# R45: slowing or disabling half-life relaxation

2026-11-09, prospective protocol v1. [中文](protocol.zh.md)

## Question and authorization

The author explicitly requested testing slower or disabled half-life relaxation, proposing that relaxation might undo acquired retention. We test whether this change permits retention to accumulate during continued teaching and improves joint answers by the complete system. Structural preservation, behavioral benefit and restoration of the original joint goal have separate conclusions.

The genuine `continuous-memory/5.1` implementation and its existing `reconfigure` API are used. Only the research copy's `memory_half_life_decay_rate` changes: 0.125 by default, 0.0125 for slower relaxation, and 0 when disabled, in half-life seconds lost per internal second. **The parameter applies to both nodes and directed connections. This is not an edge-only intervention.** Exponential weight decay, retention growth, inhibition and synaptic resources remain active. Disabling half-life relaxation does not disable weight decay.

Production source, installed modules, defaults, original individuals, old evidence and the author's manuscript are unchanged. Adopting a new default would require separate authorization. R44 completes first; scientific hosts run serially, without resuming older studies.

## Six conditions and complete sources

Eight R43 birth origins, each with two opposite teaching histories, supply complete parents at internal second 431, after initial teaching. Factor one preserves naturally acquired half-lives or raises existing target-connection half-lives to at least 300 seconds using the same R44 intervention. Factor two uses default, one-tenth, or zero relaxation. There are 6 × 8 × 2 = 96 continuous sequences.

Every condition invokes the reconfiguration API once, including default-rate controls. This matches the API's normal settlement of lazy state and expiry scheduling. Apparatus checks must show that current physical weights, node support, current half-lives, activity, resources and internal time do not change additionally at reconfiguration. Stored timestamps and lazy representations may settle normally; differences are recorded. Raising target half-lives is a separate declared intervention. The two default-rate conditions also check agreement with corresponding R44 sequences and do not increase independent N.

Sources, curricula and questions were used in R43/R44. This is mechanistic exploration with reused material, not fresh-material confirmation. Each copy is restored once, then continues two teaching rounds and 24 diagnostic trials without question resets. Core, personal adapters and AdamW/RNG, body, mappings, recent activity and actuators remain complete. Parent and implementation hashes are frozen.

## Teaching and temporal expression

The equal-curriculum causal design from R44 is retained: the original two correction rounds, original and altered-dose examples, old-example review and additional waits of 2/6/18 seconds. Teacher lists are fixed from R43's recorded errors, giving matched copies the same lessons and dose. A previously incorrect question now answered correctly is review. All current answers remain recorded. The design tests retention under the same teaching history rather than changing lesson counts after inspecting treatment scores. It is a separate mechanistic comparison under protocol sections 3, 9, 12.5 and 14, and does not claim to correct every newly occurring error.

Each personal temporal Transformer remains fixed: one second, 20 recorded frames, eight consumed frames, activation/inhibition/direct/associative values and positional encoding. Reader architecture, weights, optimizer and input scope are unchanged. Answers reach the system through ordinary teacher stimuli only; formal inference receives no answer, question ID or history label. Environment feedback follows actual actions. Observation and host delays do not advance internal time.

Each sequence includes one intervention, two teaching stages and 24 trials: 96 interventions, 192 teaching stages, 2,304 formal trials and 2,592 complete boundaries. Each formal trial lasts three internal seconds. Independent N is eight; copies and questions are nested observations.

## Prospective targets

Scores combine each origin's two histories. Joint correctness requires sound, vision and action to be correct together.

|Target|Measurement and criterion|Interpretation|
|---|---|---|
|R1 Valid relaxation intervention|Current physical error at the API call ≤1e-12; clock, queue and peripherals preserved; surviving identical connections have nondecreasing current H at complete boundaries with relaxation off; analytical updates agree with native behavior|Establish intervention validity before interpreting behavioral negatives|
|R2 Benefit without an imposed H increase|natural-slow and natural-off separately versus natural-default: new joint accuracy gain ≥25 percentage points, old-task loss ≤12.5 points; at least 6/8 origins for each|Test whether changing relaxation alone provides the prespecified substantial benefit|
|R3 Benefit after raising H|raised-slow and raised-off separately versus raised-default, with the same gain, old-task and 6/8 rules|Directly test the behavioral consequence of relaxation following an H increase|
|R4 Restoration of the joint goal|Each candidate has new joint accuracy ≥75% in both histories and all three outputs change across opposite histories for ≥50% of matched cues; at least 6/8 origins|Passing establishes restoration; failure does not erase a genuine smaller benefit|

Structural measures include survival of initial X0/X1-to-output connections, current physical weights and H, and total nodes/connections, after intervention, each teaching round and final testing. Old/new trials, conflict revision, viewing and exit choices have separate denominators. Structure cannot replace R2–R4. The four prespecified paired behavioral differences receive eight-origin effect estimates and 95% origin-bootstrap intervals. These are exploratory estimates, not inflated frame-level samples or independently confirmed population rankings. Intervals spanning a 25-point benefit are identified as insufficient for that precision target.

Source correspondences and originals are frozen before execution. No rate selection, change to 300 seconds or added origins based on interim scores. Negative results are delivered normally.

## Resources, validation and decision

A separate instrument subject tests all six physical-state interventions, the default-rate API sham, complete restoration and next-window agreement, observation isolation, continued weight decay with relaxation off, and unchanged implementation. Bilingual protocols are registered and read back before execution. Commit/RAM/C/E reserves are 5/4/5/25 GiB, monitored every second with complete-boundary pauses. Startup requires 8 GiB commit and 34 GiB data-disk headroom; private memory ≤3 GiB, originals ≤3 GiB and wall time ≤6 hours. No deletion or cloud spending. New dead-host in-flight states or unverifiable evidence require preservation and consultation.

Delivery includes boundary checks, independently implemented arithmetic, bilingual scientific conclusions, a complete original-package API readback and pending=0. R44 and R45 jointly determine which candidate to retain, which expectation to reject, or where precision remains inadequate. Slower or disabled relaxation that improves behavior while retaining old ability becomes a parameter candidate. Structure-only gains are identified as such with the behavioral goal answered separately. Defaults still require separate authorization.
