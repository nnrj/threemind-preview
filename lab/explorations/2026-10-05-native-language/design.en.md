## Output instruments and training

Compare the existing scratch byte-generating temporal Transformer with a cosine-attention exemplar reader. The latter returns probabilities and registered phrases, explicitly a finite-vocabulary instrument, not free language generation. Both accept only anonymous activity, never a question, assignment, expected answer or conversation history.

Transformer regimes: individual final-stage data; shared data from the other two individuals; continuing training at24/72/266s (400steps each, replaying prior samples); cold training on the same pooled history (1200steps). Individual/shared final-stage training also1200steps. The history-cold control separates developmental ordering from extra calibration data. Co-development here is alternating Core teaching and output calibration with a fixed input encoder, not end-to-end gradients through Core.

Main features retain all anonymous activity except the entire directly stimulated sensory modality. Language prompts exclude language input channels; visual prompts exclude visual channels. Other activity, including background interactions, remains. Full unmasked trajectories and read-only inhibition/state observations are saved. Sixteen bins over2s, per-bin maxima, episode L1 normalization; magnitude below1e-8 is unresolved rather than amplified numerical noise. Unseen channels enter explicit reported hash residuals. Inhibition is not silently added to the primary post-result analysis.
