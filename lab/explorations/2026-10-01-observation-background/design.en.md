## A Background crossed with observation

Use E22 identity blocks 100121–100124 and saved continuous-memory/2 configuration with facilitation time constant zero. Twelve AX/BX or BX/AX pairs, 550 ms between pair starts, 50 ms within pairs, 40 ms offset, intensity 0.4. Common Y=0.2 at 7 s. Wait 1 or 30 s, give X=0.5 or no cue, observe 5 s. Original K training states and 30 s probe end states must exactly reproduce E22.

Three backgrounds: original periodic K; none (teaching/probes retained); fresh random anonymous channels every 0.5 s, value 1/intensity 0.2. Only identity is randomized, never dose. No channel is reused; times and dose match K exactly. Generate identities beforehand using an independent fixed seed, fail on task-channel collision, never inspect the network to choose connections. This is an identity-random control, not every possible definition of white noise. All live telemetry is disabled.

Cross with minimal versus dense observation from birth. Minimal observation retains common input receipts, coarse global metrics, key checkpoints and final states; dense observation additionally enables existing V1/V2 readers and 20 ms full-state reads. Analyze actual trajectories only from dense cases. Compare full Core hashes at teaching end, before probing and at the final boundary; do not substitute numerical closeness. Both use the same inputs and advancement boundaries. Minimal does not mean physically never measured. Total: 48 teaching and 192 probe instances.

If removing background causes death, preserve its time, structure and delivered inputs. Never revive or extend the grace interval; subsequent cues are recorded as undeliverable, not failed recall. Separate truncated from complete windows. Identity blocks are not independent ecological populations. Removing background throughout formation and expression does not isolate either stage.

From eight original-K minimal teaching states, wait 1 s and cue X. Add two repeats each of read-and-discard and synthetic CPU/I/O load without reading the model: 32 cases. Dummy load uses about 2 ms SHA256 work and 8 KiB output per observation call, with measured cost. It is an additional-load control, not an exact cost match. Any same-input full-state discrepancy stops expansion for localization.

## B Live telemetry feedback

Only after A passes its engineering checks. Twelve 6 s real-time cases from a common trained snapshot, four interleaved blocks of minimal/dense/dummy observation. Fixed preregistered condition order. K and X schedules remain the same. Enable host, process and runtime telemetry, each at intensity 0.2 and 500 ms sampling, using current encoding and default smoothing. Preserve raw readings, encoded stimuli, sampling/delivery times and readout cost. Sequential host sessions are not identical environments; this is a pathway check, not a precise causal effect-size estimate.

Replay each realized input tape once from its identical initial state, with minimal observation and accelerated execution: 12 additional cases. Exact reproduction shows recorded ordinary inputs suffice for that case. Different live tapes are not evidence of same-input quantum effects. Mismatch suspends C for investigation without premature attribution.

## C Bounded later-choice check

Only after A/B validation and if budget permits: 128 cases. Alternate AB/BA trained parents; cue X with strengths uniformly generated from 0.3–0.7 using seed 23001, fixed before results. Common K background, 1 s wait and 5 s post-cue window. Freeze and flush the trajectory, complete early state, A-minus-B integral and SHA256 commitment before drawing a fresh byte from the OS random source. Its low bit selects whether to enable additional dense observation for the next second. The choice generator does not inspect the result or share model RNG. Record the byte, monotonic and wall timestamps and retain all cases.

Primary test: difference in already-fixed early A-minus-B integrals between subsequent choices, with two-sided 9999 permutations stratified by teaching direction, fixed analysis seed. Also report effect size and counts. No outcome-based selection or significance stopping. At approximate balance, 128 cases detect only fairly large effects (roughly 0.50 standard deviations for 80% power). If either choice has fewer than 48 cases, report insufficient power without adding convenient samples. An anomaly requires an audit and independent replication.

This tests association with a later extra-readout setting; it does **not** implement entangled photons or a quantum eraser. Common early recording is already measurement. Classical state copies and random inputs do not establish quantum dynamics. No quantum hardware is present, so physical quantum effects are neither established nor ruled out here.
