# Preregistered study: lasting experience, internal pathways and autonomy criteria

2026-09-29; criteria version `autonomy-criteria/1`; unchanged `threemind.continuous-memory/1`. The Chinese design is the full execution specification. This English version fixes the same comparisons, limits and decision rules.

## Fixed decision

Four criteria: retrievable experience-dependent individuality; causal internal influence on behavior; internal organization of sequential interactions; within-individual reshaping through a changing environment. Passing all four warrants the explicit project-level conclusion **meets the operational criterion for highly suspected autonomous conscious activity**. Failure of any criterion means the full criterion is not met in this study, not that consciousness is disproved. No additional requirements for language, intelligence or indefinite activity without input will be introduced afterward. This operational decision is not proof of subjective experience.

Four prior parents comprise two independent wiring seeds, each with two related selected lineages. They are not four independent populations. Primary measurements are time-weighted motor-control and unprompted-node activation integrals under an identical cue, subtracting a matched no-cue clone. Minimum effect: 0.0001 control-seconds or activation-seconds, also exceeding ten times sham error. Report magnitudes and trajectories; this threshold detects reproducible influence, not automatically a large behavioral effect.

Criteria 1/2 combine previous causal results with P/Q birth replications in at least three parents covering both wiring seeds. Selective lesions, matched lesions and restoration localize rather than erase earlier whole-communication evidence. Criterion 3 requires the 0.5–1.5 s motor interaction `AB−A−B+none` to change beyond threshold when communication is blocked, in at least three parents covering both seeds. Criterion 4 requires changed versus constant environments to leave a history effect under identical test input/body position after stage two, in at least three parents covering both seeds, alongside recorded closed-loop trajectories. Return-environment recovery/competition is measured but perfect reversal is not required.

## A. Localization

All four parents, both prior P=AB/CD and Q=AD/CB histories, public birth clearing short-lived states, A/none cue, 15 s observation. Seven conditions: normal; no-change sham; silence the four A↔B/D directed edges; silence equal-count non-task edges matched by weight/stability; silence all returns to A; silence then immediately restore task edges; block all excitation/inhibition communication. Record exact applied edges and matching errors. Return blocking is not removal of every cycle. All-communication blocking also removes forward learned pathways.

## B. Dose and retention

The region0 parent from each seed starts from the same previous 20 s warm state. Teach AD with eight schedules: none; one intensity-1 input; one intensity-.4 input; ten .4 massed; ten .4 spaced; ten .1 massed; ten .1 spaced; ten .4 spaced with positive existing-edge consolidation disabled only. Training ends at relative 129 s; all final stimuli occur at 128 s. Massed times: 119…128; spaced: 0,1,2,4,8,16,32,64,96,128 s. Ten weak inputs and one strong input match external dose, not necessarily effective internal dose.

Probe A/none for 15 s at ages 1,30,300 s after final training. Background continues while waiting. At 300 s also test newly born copies with communication intact, returns-to-A blocked, or all communication blocked. Testing never writes back into the training parent. Consolidation-off does not disable edge birth, eligibility, node history or forgetting. Three hundred model seconds is the measured limit, not years of human memory.

## C. Sequential interaction and environmental change

Four-cell sequence: prior P/Q birth copies; A/none at zero; B/none at .5 s; intensity .5; normal/blocked communication; 64 copies, each 15 s. Preserve both temporal windows.

The previous ring physics and fixed motor wiring are retained. Environment P supplies AB in region0 and CD in region1; Q supplies AD/CB. True velocity sensing remains. Action changes position and subsequent sensation; no correct action is assigned. Four parents × PPP/PQP/QQQ/QPQ, starting x=.3125,v=0, three 30 s stages, .8 inputs every second. Body and full Core state carry between stages. At every stage fork A/none tests with identical starting body; after stage two add birth tests. Standing still/circling is not a failure.

## D. Novelty and repeated erasure

For both wiring seeds, introduce eight fixed anonymous texture channels identically, individually stimulate them, then familiarize the first four together ten times. Four rounds replace 0,1,2,4 original features; ten .4 inputs per round at 1 s intervals. Conditions: intact; disconnect within-current-pattern edges after each round; disconnect equal-count other edges matched by weight/stability; disable only local novelty gain. Test original pattern, current pattern and none after each round. Erasure may remove older edges and is not precise deletion of a memory object. Assess matched novelty-gain effects, new edges, stability, first-input responses, variability of fixed-cue responses, and whether change magnitude is monotonic. Do not assume it is.
