# E33: remove predation to separate foraging and population effects

Registered 2026-10-03 while E32 was running. The user proposed that more successful predation by Core-controlled bodies could lower final population, making simple survivor totals misleading. This is a separately registered supplement; E32 evidence and rules are preserved.

## Paired conditions

Use E32 environment seeds322101/322102 with normal Core control and independent random commands each second. Copy their registered protocols and change only the existing environmental `predation` flag to`false`: retain the1.2m world,53 founders,97 live-body limit,360 initial nutrients,random replenishment,4096 nutrient capacity,mutation,finite energy and reproduction. Observe120 simulated seconds per world without imposing an individual lifespan.

Contacts,collision feedback,water,temperature,gravity/buoyancy,local food fields and proprioception remain. Core command cadence and amplitude remain unrestricted. Both controls start with equal physical and probabilistic opportunities; their subsequent ecological feedback may differ. Losing predation energy and predation deaths is the intended intervention, without compensatory energy. Future bilateral parent–child protection has been approved but is irrelevant when all predation is disabled; E32 retains its original rule.

## Fixed analysis

Report each pair's final living population,food intake,living-body seconds,intake per living-body second,births,body/Core deaths,and first time reaching97 living bodies. If neither reaches the cap,report right-censoring at120s rather than inventing a winner on that criterion. Retain trajectories; do not choose a favorable endpoint afterward.

Compare against completed E32 worlds with the same seed/control,without rerunning them. A persisting Core–random advantage shows it is not wholly dependent on predation; disappearance/reversal indicates predation and population interactions matter. This contrast alone cannot establish purposeful hunting. Individuals and frames are not independent world replicates.
