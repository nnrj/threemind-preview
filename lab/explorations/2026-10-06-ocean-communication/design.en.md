# E53: Communication before human-language translation

Registered 2026-10-06. Human-language/Transformer training is paused, with E50–E52 retained. Core, defaults, old instances and old sensory mappings remain unchanged.

## Questions and criteria

Test (1) whether one consciousness's emitted signal changes another's behavior, (2) whether acquired auditory connections contribute beyond direct fixed motor wiring, and (3) whether a functioning channel improves feeding, avoidance, reproduction or capture across two environments. Only with support should subsequent experiments test absent referents, delayed information and new combinations. Mutual influence, excitation contagion and automatic energy sharing alone are not language, recollection or reasoning.

## Complete embodied habitat

Use a periodic 1.2m ocean, 37 founders, population cap67, 360 initial nutrient particles, capacity4096 and random new particles each simulated second. Retain flow, temperature, contact, posture, weight/buoyancy-related feedback, finite body/Core energy, mutation, activity-based death and bilateral immature parent/child protection. No CPU telemetry or maximum age. Copy the E38 turn44, turn59 and engulf15 candidates equally: inherited structure/body, cleared fast activity, ordinary equal founder endowments. These are researcher-created copies and compatible lineages, not established biological species.

Same-lineage bodies cannot consume one another; between lineages retain the existing 50% capsule-area enclosure, shell, dwell and competition rules. Compatible surfaces within1mm for1second can share25% of newly ingested food or prey energy at90% transfer efficiency. There is no prescribed pursuit/cooperation policy, attraction, fusion or bonus reward. Joint benefit is possible; deliberate cooperation is a separate outcome. Multi-body force summation for joint capture is not implemented here.

Reuse the oscillator mouth: every100ms, at most12 voices exceeding0.08 activation, with48 anonymous frequency positions. OutputBus transports the mouth signal to a finite-range medium; two ears measure8 bands each. Distance-dependent power and a positive propagation delay precede fixed anonymous InputBus encoding. Hearing gain0.12; emitted band power costs0.1 body-energy units per second. This is a band-power acoustic approximation, not PCM or fluid acoustics. Sender identities, cohort names, object labels, food positions and answers are evidence only and never input to Core.

Self-hearing stays enabled. Muting and scrambling affect only OTHER individuals. Scrambling uses a new band permutation at each arrival, preserving that arrival's power. Later geometry and dose can diverge naturally. Internal observation is separate from public emission; the inherited passive mouth is not evidence of voluntary speech gating. No artificial semantic gate is added.

## Order and analysis

First verify delays, anonymity, self/other controls, energy conservation, recovery and unchanged Core behavior with the added channel inaudible and free. Each of two environments then develops for300simulated seconds, checkpoint at150seconds. The first20seconds assess compute cost and signal delivery, not behavioral desirability; implementation failures are separately retained.

From each complete300-second state run40seconds of intact, other-muted and other-scrambled conditions. Retain ALL organisms and nutrients. Select up to64 strong edges incident to hearing receptors, with count/weight-matched nonauditory edges; compare targeted silencing, matched silencing and silence-then-release rescue. Record any matching gap and any absent eligible connections. Include an earlier checkpoint's short-window control where budget permits.

Primary measurements are within-identity actions, paths, emitted bands, received stimuli, related neural activity and transmission. Analyze initial1second and5seconds separately from ecological cascades. Also record feeding, capture, death, birth and sharing as processes. Worlds, not time samples or forks, are independent sources. Consistent advantages across two sources and causal interventions are needed for preliminary useful communication. A language candidate additionally requires contextual reference, learning/reversal and absent-object tests; these are conditional subsequent stages, not assumed successes.
