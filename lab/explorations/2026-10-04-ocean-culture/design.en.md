# E39–E40: joint culture of three candidates and proximity-based sharing

Preregistered2026-10-04 after the user's explicit request to continue with three sources and add a sharing experiment. E38 remains closed at its original resource boundary; this is a separately budgeted study.

Seventeen inherited copies each of E38 individuals44 and59 at900s, and predator15 immediately after its199.62s enclosure, form51 Core-controlled founders. Individual E38/59 is not E37/59. Existing birth/1 preserves learned structure, body and mapping while resetting activity, age and counters. Researcher copying is identified, not called natural reproduction. Later births use unchanged inherited random variation.

The full1.2m habitat holds up to97 bodies, initially360 nutrient particles with random replenishment of one per simulation second. It retains physics, water/temperature/body/neighbor senses, body costs, shell defenses, bilateral juvenile-parent protection and complete-enclosure-only consumption. No maximum age. The approved paid-material environment retains50% of construction cost as digestible body energy. Changing both source composition and material rules means differences from E38 cannot be assigned solely to learning.

After a shared0–300s culture, full-state forks continue to900s. Culture keeps intake sharing0; sharing uses0.25. Any two bodies whose surfaces remain within1mm for at least1s may share newly ingested energy at90% transfer efficiency. Multiple neighbors split the donor's total25%, rather than each receiving25%. Nutrients and successful body consumption both count. Existing reserves and newly received shared energy are not redistributed. Capacity-limited transfers charge only the amount actually sent; losses remain in the ledger. Deep enclosure is not edge companionship. Stationary proximity is permitted. No attraction, following, adhesion, kin preference, group label, neural reward or forced fusion is added.

This differs from the old reserve-gradient exchange and therefore uses separate external protocol/state `threemind.ocean-association/1` and `threemind.ocean-association-state/1`. Fraction0 preserves the material environment's dynamics. Old protocols/states are not upgraded. Record proximity starts/ends, duration, actual transfers and subsequent feeding/survival. Possible exploitation is retained rather than filtered out.

Passive E38 turning criteria remain: a five-second pre-ingestion window with at least30° change of water-relative heading, final food-gradient alignment≥0.7 and gain≥0.3, real rotation output aligned with angular velocity and no collision, followed by ingestion. These are observation criteria, not controller restrictions or proof against incidental circling.

Archive live states at the first and3/6/12/24/48th qualifying turn, every successful enclosure, and the first combination of both. A zero parent rate still requires the child's minimum count. Preserve comparison counts and exposure. This screens candidates for follow-up; it does not remove age, location, morphology or environmental-opportunity confounding. Founder copies have no invented within-world natural parent.

Record behavior processes and ancestry alongside population totals. Persistent association and resource exchange are distinct from active cooperation or physical fusion. Archive full-habitat original-speed clips for combined capabilities if observed; causal internal-pathway attribution remains a later matched intervention.
