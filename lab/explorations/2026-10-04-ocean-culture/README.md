The live observer archives relevant body/brain states during execution and imports them into this study's species library at segment completion. Its rates are candidate screening, not a causal comparison between unmatched parent and child habitats.

Snapshots retain the external association timers as well as all bodies, brains, random state and material accounts. Separate `observer.json` carries passive counters and a bounded five-second measurement history through segment boundaries and forks. The observer never supplies stimulus or edits Core. Each branch receives independent observer counters.

Read real sharing/capture events while a case is still running, without re-reading earlier chunks or loading inputs/brains:


Only closed Arrow chunks are read; at this recording setting live evidence can lag by up to 20 simulated seconds. Each cursor belongs to one recording and filter. Delete/change only your own cursor to deliberately replay events; original evidence remains immutable. Sharing/capture events are observations, never commands to the model.
