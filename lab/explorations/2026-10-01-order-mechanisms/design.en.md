## B Phase and dose-matched references

Four identical anonymous material blocks, facilitation 0/1 s, 12 AX→BX or BX→AX pairs at intensity 0.4. Pair starts are 2.5 s apart. Within-pair gaps: 50, 500 or 2,000 ms. Teaching phases: 0, 20, 40, 60 or 80 ms. At 32.2 s all receive the same independent Y; after 1 or 30 s give X=0.5 or no cue at exactly matched absolute times. Match frame count, dose, total course duration, maintenance and probe timing. Initial phase waiting includes the same maintenance K.

240 training plus 960 observation instances: 1,200 total. Report phase coverage and effect sizes rather than calling 50 ms universally optimal. Either primacy or recency can establish a bidirectional order effect.

Equal-dose separate references: at 50 ms and phases 0/40 ms replace AX/BX by AX/BZ, with Z a separate anonymous reference. Probe X=0.5, Z=0.5 or no cue at both waits. 32 training plus 192 observation instances: 224 total. Element count and dose match, topology and per-reference exposure do not; this tests sharing without claiming all order memory requires a common X.

## Outcomes and evidence

Primary measure: complete-window activation integral, cued minus matched uncued. Opposite teaching orders must yield opposite A−B directions. Preregistered absolute threshold 1e-6 activation·seconds; also report 1e-9 numerical checks, individual magnitudes, five-second terminal and readout decision values. Neither preferred direction nor positive results define a completed test. Four blocks mainly change anonymous identities, not ecosystems.

Keep formation, retention and expression distinct; artificial swapping itself is not evidence that such swapping occurs naturally.
