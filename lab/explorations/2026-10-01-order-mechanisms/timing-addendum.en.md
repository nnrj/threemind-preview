2026-10-01. State-exchange batch A has completed; timing batch B has not started and no B outcome has been observed. B matches pair starts at 2.5 s intervals to compare within-pair gaps, but also changes E20's approximately 0.55 s repetition cadence. Before seeing B outcomes, add this fixed control without rewriting the original design.

The same four seeds, facilitation 0/1 s, AB/BA, 12 pairs, 50 ms within-pair gap and 550 ms pair-start interval. Phases 0/20/40/60/80 ms. Deliver maintenance K=0.2 first at time zero and every 0.5 s thereafter. All conditions receive Y at 7 s, then X=0.5 or no cue at 8/37 s. Same dose, observation and criteria as B. Eighty training plus 320 observation instances add 400, bringing the registered total from 2,480 to 2,880 within unchanged safety budgets.

This preserves the earlier cadence while matching final/probe times; it is not an exact hash replay of E20. A's shams provide that replay. A sparse-course failure with a dense-course success implicates course/retention conditions, not phase alone. Background counts differ across course durations but are strictly matched within each phase sweep.
