# E38: reuse and environmental selection of an enclosure candidate

Registered 2026-10-04. E37 completes unchanged; this is a separate study.

Individual 59 enclosed 32 at 226.06 s in E37. Its 300 s snapshot is archived with body, brain, mapping and ancestry. One event makes a candidate, not an established hunting lineage.

Two complete 1.2 m habitats start from identical random conditions, with 53 all-Core founders, a 97-live cap and 360 nutrient particles. IDs 0–10 inherit candidate 59's structural memory, body traits and mapping; activity is reset. The remaining 42 retain their original varied bodies and blank brains. Founder energy uses the ordinary equal endowment. This is documented researcher replication, not natural reproduction. Later births retain the established funded inheritance and mutation rules.

Both receive one new random nutrient per second for 300 s. Thereafter the control remains at 1/s; the selection habitat receives 1/2 s. Existing particles are not removed. Positions use the same random sequence, delivery times differ. Lower nutrient supply is a proposed selection pressure, not a guarantee of increased predation.

Full containment for 0.2 s is required; contact capture is disabled. Shell costs/delays, bilateral immature parent protection, water flow, temperature, proprioception, finite body/Core costs and mutation remain. No fusion, sharing, horizontal transfer, neural rewards or steering are added.

Each habitat runs at most 900 simulation seconds, with 300 s segments and snapshots; stop a habitat early if no physical bodies remain. Compare candidate and other lineages' predation, offspring, ingestion, energy and traits. Save successful candidates and full-habitat videos.

Predeclared interpretation: one event is a candidate. At least three successful enclosures by at least two candidate-lineage individuals in the new environment count as repeated enclosure in this study. Successful offspring additionally show intergenerational retention. Neither alone proves active pursuit or a causal structural-memory advantage; those require paired ablations. If not achieved, report the shortfall and stop this round rather than tuning indefinitely.

Budgets: one worker, at most 4 h simulation wall time, 5 GiB evidence soft / 8 GiB hard limit, 4 GiB process RAM; reserve 2 GiB RAM, 5 GiB on C and 15 GiB on E. Account usage +3 percentage points stops expansion, +5 stops new simulation. Old evidence is read-only; no repeated bulk hash scans.
