## Regional coverage after the first closed-loop results / 首批闭环后扩大干预覆盖

The 12-edge cuts alter movement but have little 60 s food effect. This small sample is not a full association-region removal. Before executing the extension, fix both 200 s worlds and a maximum of 128 incoming edges to active nodes absent from the preceding 1 s inputs, now including edges within the unprompted region. Select by pre-intervention weight times source activation; keep a same-count weight/stability-matched cut outside the target region. No food-based target selection. Run each for 60 s using the existing sham, two simulation slots, same budgets. This is an additional intervention scope, not a replacement for the small-cut result or an automatic benefit test. New direct inputs to targets during the freely interacting future are possible; the earlier clean-prefix replay supplies the more specific unprompted-activity evidence.

12条边阻断已改变运动，但60秒摄食影响小，不能代表整个联想区域。补试执行前固定：两个200秒来源不换，每个个体最多128条通向此前1秒未直接受刺激且正在活动节点的入边，包含该区域内部的边；仍按干预前权重×源激活选取。区域外等量、权重/稳定度相近的边为对照，沿用原假干预，均观察60秒，最多两个模拟、预算不变。不根据食物收益挑区域，不替换小范围试验，也不预定正面结果。自由闭环中这些节点以后可能再次直接受刺激；更严格的未提示活动证据来自先前没有目标直接输入的短时重放区间。
