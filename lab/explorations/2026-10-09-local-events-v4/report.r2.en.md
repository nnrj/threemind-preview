H is not completely static, and actual transmission does occur. [Diagnostic evidence](diagnostics.json)

Weak delivered signals after weight/STP/threshold gates cannot accumulate substantial protection with current defaults. Removing scans cannot by itself fix this dose/regression mismatch. Next calibration should separate dose scale, H regression and short-term release gates; no parameter was secretly retuned, and STP is not declared useless.

Baseline text weight is already lower in `/4`: approximately1.314→1.159 for H,1.409→1.237 for stability; a common edge's stability is about0.977→0.745. It is not evidence of regression in every capability.
