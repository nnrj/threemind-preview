# Local-retention repair: bounded mechanism probes

Registered 2026-10-08 before running this probe set. Authorized by the user-approved /3 repair plan. This is Core mechanism validation and read-only instrument calibration, not consciousness teaching or shape/color evaluation. No old individual, peripheral, optimizer or original evidence is changed. Training-protocol subject identification and interpretation limits apply; no peripheral or body is part of these deliberately Core-only probes.

Each backend/condition starts independently from a blank Core and retains its own complete Core state through exposure, delay and relearning. Inputs, times and configuration are recorded. Controls: repaired /3 adaptive log growth (default), /3 legacy-stability with the same other controls, /3 adaptive linear growth, and intact /2 as a whole-version reference. The /2 reference is not a single-factor ablation. Quiescence death is extended to 10^12 microseconds in all probes so structural forgetting can be measured without terminating the subject; no claim about survival of an ordinary individual follows.

Fixed exposure cases: one ordinary paired stimulus; one paired stimulus at intensity 4; paired stimuli every 0.1 simulation second for 120 seconds; reversal from channels 1+2 to 1+3 after 60 seconds; weak repeated background over eight fixed channels; weak background over 80 growing channels. Background lasts 8 seconds. No semantic significance is attached to channel numbers. Observe exposure end and 30, 120, 300 seconds later. Then deliver ten normal paired reminders at 0.1-second intervals and report reconstruction/weight changes, not a behavioral answer.

Report nodes, active edges, support, mean weight, H, dormant counts, actual transport, local settlement/expiry counts, and each checkpoint hash. Dormancy and awakening stay off in these default/control probes; separately test their local mechanics in the contract tests. Save snapshots only as derived new evidence. Measure advance/inject time separately from readout, checkpoint serialization and gzip; no old mixed timing is treated as a Core benchmark.

If default H does not grow under actual transmitted doses, record the zero/negative result. Inspect the activity-to-regression balance and evaluate gains 2 and 4 as explicitly labelled calibration controls on the repeated-stimulus case, without changing the approved default 0.2. This bounded diagnostic is not a final-task parameter fit. It cannot establish shape/color retention or human-time equivalence.

Acceptance: architecture constraints and Python/Rust agreement are mandatory independently of whether memory proxies improve. Preserve all results, including reversals and failed retention; do not run long shape/color teaching or revise old E64–E66 criteria in this phase.
