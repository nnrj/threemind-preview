## Outcome and decision

Local retention, restored ordinary learning, sparse expiry/reclamation, default-off awakening, configuration and state-identity boundaries are implemented and independently checked in Python and Rust. However, with actual transmitted doses, default `memory_learning_gain=0.2` **does not preserve edges until this probe's 300-second delay observation**, performing worse than the legacy-stability control. Engineering completion does not establish the intended memory benefit.

Registered gain-2 and gain-4 calibration controls improve structural retention, but cover repetition only, not sustained background, erroneous associations or reversal costs. The approved provisional default 0.2 remains unchanged. Register parameter/background/conflict controls before deciding a default and then undertaking full shape/color teaching.

## Implementation boundary

- Nodes credit actually accepted input; edges credit actually emitted signals. Each owns H and simulation-time anchors. Dose uses a fixed 0.1-second reference, without endpoint coactivation, double receipt credit or per-event bonuses. Strong salience/fatigue remain node-local; edges do not inherit node H.
- Graph-wide retention updates, ordinary-event synchronization, full-edge maintenance lists/sorting, novelty undo/conversion into D, stability resets and forced plasticity are removed. Ordinary weight/stability learning and novelty rewards are restored. The 0.55 dose learning rate applies only to automatic node support.
- Objects compute their own next expiry. Sparse queues deliver it at the established post-propagation structural phase; same-time input precedes expiry. Structural indices update locally. Observations and metrics project without mutating dynamics.
- Default H forgetting does not stack stability protection; `legacy-stability` is a mutually exclusive control. H growth has log-saturating and linear-saturating choices. Identity/formulas are fixed at birth; numerical edits use explicit configuration interventions.
- Automatic support reclamation is default; manual threshold takes priority, with positive isolated wait only at threshold zero. Touch, reconnection, later disconnection and recovery preserve timing.
- Dormancy/awakening are default-off. Only residual contacts deliver local signals, with explicit separate positive node/edge amplitude thresholds and same-time endpoint sums. No two-hop search, rotation, cross-time charge or granted weight remains; explicit interventions cannot be undone.
- /3 rejects global transmission/adaptation quotas. Host pacing does not change simulation cadence. State schema becomes `threemind.continuous-memory-state/3.1`, with updated operators and unchanged kernel name. Pre-repair /3 evidence is preserved but silent continuation is rejected. Official historical execution, fixtures and recovery remain available.

Existing activation/inhibition graph loops, propagation cadence, older scans, resource mechanisms and source distinctions remain outside this repair. Rust node deletion still preserves Vec order and updates shifted offsets: a real deletion-time storage cost. Loading and explicit configuration intervention can enumerate state; ordinary local maintenance cannot. This is not a claim that the entire executor is fully local.

## Conditions and continuity

These are deliberately Core-only mechanism probes, not teaching of a consciousness with a personal peripheral. Each backend/condition starts from an independent blank Core and continues through exposure, delay and reminders with complete Core checkpoints and exact continuation checks. There is no peripheral, optimizer, body or semantic answer. All conditions extend quiescence death to `10^12 μs` to observe structural forgetting without terminating the subject. Ordinary default lifespan cannot be inferred.

The main conditions are repaired /3 default H, /3 legacy stability, /3 linear H, and complete /2. The first three share ordinary learning/reclamation controls; /2 is a whole-version reference. Ordinary/strong single intensities are 1/4. Repetition pairs inputs every 0.1 second for 120 seconds. Reversal changes channels 1+2 to 1+3 at 60 seconds. Weak background delivers 80 events over eight seconds across eight repeated or eighty increasing channels. Observe exposure end and delays of 30/120/300 seconds, then ten ordinary reminders.

`run-02` completes 26 condition pairs, 52 independent backend runs and 260 stage checkpoints. Discrete states match; floating comparisons use relative `1e-12` / absolute `1e-14`. Same-backend continuation hashes match exactly for every run. `run-01` stopped with `OSError 22` during repeated aggregate-file writing. Its incomplete files remain. The writer was changed to unique per-pair files and one final aggregate, then `run-02` used independent blank subjects; interrupted individuals were not merged.

Default edge H returns from about 40 seconds toward its 30-second floor and weights continue exponential loss. Actual emission after resource/gating is small. Removing the old endpoint-coactivation dose means the same numerical gain cannot be assumed to provide the same protection. Repeated-input node H reaches about 114137.75 seconds; after 300 seconds both nodes remain with total support about 1.99632, while their edges are gone. This disparity deserves a registered follow-up. Node survival is not proof of accessible associative memory.

Ordinary single exposure has no nodes/live edges at +120 seconds; strong single exposure clears by +300 seconds. At +120 seconds the strong case still has two edges of mean weight 0.010576 in default /3. This demonstrates longer retention under this condition, not permanent one-shot memory.

At +300 seconds after reversal, default and linear /3 have no live edges; legacy stability and /2 retain four, mean weight about 0.186314. This observes old/new structural coexistence or loss without a behavioral decision, so it establishes neither successful reversal nor avoidance of incorrect consolidation.

Fixed background ends with eight nodes and no edges; all three /3 conditions clear by +120 seconds. Increasing-channel background ends with seven nodes in /3 and clears by +30 seconds; /2 ends with eighty and clears by +120 seconds. All main conditions have zero background nodes/dormant traces at +300 seconds. Finite cleanup after background stops does not establish bounded accumulation under continuous background. Gains 2/4 were not tested on background.

Ten reminders reconstruct edges but do not establish recovery of old memory. Default repetition reconstructs mean weight 0.42338, below 0.44429 for a single-exposure subject that had fully cleared. Surviving node stability/plasticity and support differ; these are possible explanatory factors, without a causal ablation or a measured sample-saving endpoint.
