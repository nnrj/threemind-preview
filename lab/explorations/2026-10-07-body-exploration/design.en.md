# E61: free exploration before teaching, and learning one's body

Registered 2026-10-07; approved, to execute after engineering checks. Core remains continuous-memory/2; dynamics, defaults and historical evidence stay intact.

## Question and paired conditions
Does an untaught sensorimotor period improve later gaze, fixation and eye–hand control, enabling subsequent whole-field shape/color pointing? Activity is not itself evidence of learned intention; assisted demonstrations are not autonomous successes.

Two source pairs (Core seeds223/279, weather47/103) each receive:
1. 180 simulated seconds of exploration without language instructions, markers or answers. Four colored, differently shaped spring/mass toys can move, rebound and sound upon contact. Vision, visible hand, eye/body proprioception, touch, mouth state, stereo sound bands and physical background remain present.
2. An equal-time environment condition with zero motor commands, ordinary sensation and learning; physical wind/inertia persist. This matches age and surroundings, not the experienced sensory dose.
3. Replay of condition1's actual actuator commands while existing structural plasticity is disabled only during exploration and explicitly re-enabled before teaching. This is a yoked physical replay control, not autonomous behavior. Verify actual input equivalence without repeated large-file hashes.

A seeded, untrained anonymous activity-to-motor wiring plus separately recorded local motor variability (SD0.18, correlation time0.35s) supplies initial exploration. No toy-seeking or task strategy is built in. Variability is an external developmental condition, not claimed Core intention. Measure workspace coverage, contacts, sounds and boundary occupancy; insufficient opportunity does not refute learning.

## Common teaching and attribution
Move each student to the same classroom physical initial state, retaining its full Core state and clock. Sensory identities remain fixed; toys become stationary classroom objects. Text encoding stays unchanged.
Four skills: look, point, both, hold. Ten consecutive three-second demonstrations per skill, then three interleaved review rounds preceded by6/12/24s waits:52 lessons. Record real per-skill revisit intervals rather than calling the schedule an optimal forgetting curve.
A separate branch supplies12 validation demonstrations. Equal architecture, training budget and seed; final12 six-second trials are not used for model selection. Targets switch after3s; each phase starts with0.8s of a physically visible marker. Normal motor inference accepts only16 ordered raw activity samples, never task or answer metadata.
The main route uses the corrected E60 shared force reader. A separately identified pose-plus-physical-servo route may be fitted from the same demonstration records if basic control fails. Servo execution sees only the decoded pose and measured body position/velocity, never teacher/object inventory/answer. This architectural comparison is distinct from the preschool-experience effect.
Cross fixed readers between explored and equal-time Core states; test existing-edge silence/rescue and zero commands. Separate changes in actions from improvements in correctness. Trials/branches from one source are not independent population samples.

## Outcomes and staged stopping
Retain angle error, clear-field entry, speed, dwell, hand distance/arrival and associative activity. No800ms minimum is imposed. Alignment at phase start is separate from actual entry.
Look for directionally consistent exploration effects across both sources; use structural and fixed-reader controls for attribution. This limited study makes no population-significance claim.
Extend to new shape/color combinations only if both sources actually enter and finish aligned/arrived in at least half of requested eye and hand phases. Otherwise report the obstacle and do not expand language/reasoning claims.
Optional twelve student attempts with correction from their actual off-course states, or the pose readout, must receive an appended registration before execution.
No task reward, energy-rule changes or Core attention is introduced. Evolved exploratory predispositions, whole-body mobility, NPC conversation, language adapters and richer touch/taste are later independent conditions.
