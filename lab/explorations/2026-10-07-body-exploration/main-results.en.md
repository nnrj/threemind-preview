# E61 primary comparison: exploration occurred, reliable control did not emerge

**2026-10-07 continuity correction:** These primary measurements and their earlier interpretation are preserved. Fixed preschool wiring was later replaced by a newly fitted Transformer, so this cannot reject preschool benefit for the same continuously developing Core and its own motor center. That integrated-system negative interpretation is withdrawn; see the [correction](continuity-correction.en.md) and [final report](report.en.md).

2026-10-07. Recorded after the six primary conditions completed. Caregiver, connection-attribution and posture-reader results are reported separately. Those later comparisons do not replace these results.

## Actual preschool experience

The body sees its hand and the full foveated scene, senses eye/hand position and movement, contact, wind, temperature, humidity and support. Colored toys move, rebound and sound. Mouth sound reaches its own ears. During 180 simulation seconds of free exploration there are no teacher targets or rewards. Fixed anonymous activity wiring and independently recorded aimless motor variation jointly generate actions.

Each action-replay condition has 3600 numerically identical sensory frames to its exploration source, with zero difference. Only preschool structural learning is disabled and restored before lessons. Activation, inhibition and short-term history remain. Zero-command exposure retains wind-driven movement and contact; it is not no experience. Contact counts alone do not establish intentional play.

The original actuator gain of 1 produced no contacts and 98.19% boundary time in source 0 over 180 seconds. Evidence is preserved; a separately registered gain 0.1 condition created the exploration opportunity above without changing Core input, output or dynamics. Coverage improved, but eyes still spent much time at their physical limits.

## Performance after identical teaching

Each condition receives 52 lessons in looking, pointing, moving both, and holding: 10 consecutive initial demonstrations per skill, followed by three interleaved rounds with 6/12/24-second preceding waits. Lessons last 3 seconds, totaling 198 seconds including intervals. Another 12 independent demonstrations select an adapter checkpoint; the final 12 questions are not used for selection. Teacher-led Core experience was not replaced by adapter fitting.

Questions last 6 seconds with a mid-question target change. Each effector has 12 requested movement phases. **Success means starting outside, entering, and finishing aligned or arrived**, within 0.075 radians for the eye or 0.20 metres for the hand. No 800 ms dwell rule is added. Full trajectories, transient entry and duration remain recorded; initially aligned phases are not newly achieved turns.

**This apparatus and curriculum did not yield a consistent control advantage from exploration.** Experience and activity exist; reliable requested looking/pointing is missing. Two sources do not justify rejecting every preschool-development approach or treating individual questions as independent minds.

Neither source meets the registered minimum of 6/12 for both effectors, so the batch does not expand into whole-field shape/color combinations.

## Apparatus clues

The six force readers achieve independent demonstration MSE 0.0301–0.0365, compared with 0.0919–0.0922 for always-zero commands. They learned some demonstration associations, but lower demonstration error did not produce reliable control in the states reached by their own actions.

Source 0 exploration's mean absolute eye commands during tests are approximately 0.054 and 0.026. A post-hoc calculation using the scoring targets and actual body states requires approximately 0.835 and 0.825 mean absolute correction from the same physical servo. This indicates ineffective corrective action, **not proof that Core has no target information**. Scoring targets are not fed back into inference, Core or training.

The preregistered alternative predicts posture from the same raw activity, then uses a body-state-only servo. Its results and attribution remain separate. The user's heavier-arm/mild-breeze calibration and centered-object recognition proposal are [R39 follow-ups](../../../docs/en/plans/centered-object-and-body-calibration.md), not changes to these completed conditions.
