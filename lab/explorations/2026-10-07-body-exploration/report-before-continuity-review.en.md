## Established results

**The study delivered a responsive play environment, but exploration did not yet yield reliable gaze, fixation and pointing.** The two free-exploration sources made 19 and 18 toy contacts with visual, proprioceptive, contact, toy-sound and self-hearing feedback. After identical teaching, eye success was 2/12 and 0/12; hand success was 0/12 and 1/12, without a consistent advantage over equal-time exposure or action replay.

Each denominator comprises 12 phases requesting movement. Success requires entering from outside and finishing aligned or arrived. Transient responses remain recorded, with no added 800 ms dwell rule. Starting already aligned is not a newly achieved turn. [Complete primary table and curriculum](main-results.en.md)

This is a test of an apparatus and training method. It does not reject exploration generally or establish reliable translation of target choices. Contact counts and complex trajectories cannot replace task performance.

## Conditions and evidence

Two Core/weather sources each undergo 180 preschool seconds, then identical 52-lesson curricula with dense-to-spaced skill review and 12 independent validation demonstrations. Tests last 6 seconds and change targets halfway; every question starts from a clone of the same post-teaching state. Preschool conditions are free exploration, zero-command equal-time exposure, and matched action replay with structural learning disabled only during preschool. Each replay has 3,600 exactly matching sensory frames; learning is restored before teaching.

Normal adapter inference receives only 16 ordered raw Core frames, without task class, object name, correct location or scoring answers. Teacher language and markers use ordinary senses. External supervision uses demonstration action/posture labels during training, not inference. Core dynamics, existing sensory mappings and saved-instance defaults are unchanged. Research interventions operate on new experiment clones only.

Zero-command exposure still has wind and passive physical contact. Fixed anonymous activity wiring and aimless motor variation are recorded separately; variation is not renamed intention. The caregiver moves toys, sounds and supplies short phrases. This is not a full infant/adult simulation; natural-speech recognition, NPC conversation, locomotion, rewards and innate selection are not implemented in this batch.

**An important training-pipeline limitation:** preschool uses fixed anonymous wiring, teacher actions then move the body, and a separately trained Transformer controls formal tests. One motor center does not persist across all three phases. The matched comparison still evaluates preschool benefit within this pipeline, but cannot exclude adapter replacement as a barrier to using bodily experience. It is not completed continuous Core–motor-center co-development. Retaining and gradually calibrating one center is a future control, not a demonstrated remedy.

## After caregiver withdrawal

| 0 | 16 | 0 | 5.33% | 0% |

The environment and senses remain after guidance stops. **The batch did not establish improved continued contact or low-speed toy fixation after withdrawal.** This concerns the measured behavior, not a claim that caregiver experience left Core unchanged.

A separate 30-second common-world probe resets physical positions while retaining preschool motor wiring and matching independent motor variation (W2):

| Source | Guided history, normal control | Free history, normal control | Guided history, Core-to-motor contribution cut |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 0: new contacts | 2 | 2 | 12 |
| 1: new contacts | 15 | 16 | 8 |

The contact effect of Core control differs by source, and caregiver history does not consistently outperform free exploration. The cut removes Core's contribution while retaining the same motor variation and environment. Fewer contacts can reflect movement biases; more can result from undirected motion. Counts alone do not establish intentional play.

**Preserved methodological correction:** the original W probe reset a seed to match variation but unintentionally changed fixed motor wiring too. Its six original records remain, qualified as extra changed-interface measurements. W2 retains original wiring and resets only variation. Continuous 120+60-second withdrawal and primary teaching comparisons are unaffected. [Correction registered before W2](withdrawal-correction.md)

## Approved next direction

1. Calibrate hand physics first. Wind is below 1 m/s, yet the unit-mass carriage accumulates drift. Audit mass, damping, wind coupling and support together rather than only increasing a “weight” value. Preserve old conditions.
2. Present one centered object during teaching and questioning, testing recognition without scanning/pointing demands. Keep physical background, review and corrective demonstrations.
3. Continue separating Core contribution from adapter answering. Centered-object success would implicate coordination, not establish it as the only limitation.

[R39](../../../docs/en/plans/centered-object-and-body-calibration.md) records the follow-up. Neither source reached the registered 6/12 minimum for both effectors, so this batch does not expand shape/color combinations or world complexity. Innate selection, energy rewards, NPC/language and a full robot remain separate work.
