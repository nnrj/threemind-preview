## Established results

**Responsive play occurred and the segmented apparatus scored poorly; this is not a valid negative test of co-development.** The two free-exploration sources made 19 and 18 toy contacts with visual, proprioceptive, contact, toy-sound and self-hearing feedback. After teaching, eye success was 2/12 and 0/12; hand success was 0/12 and 1/12. Measurements remain but cannot determine continuous-system preschool benefit.

Each denominator comprises 12 phases requesting movement. Success requires entering from outside and finishing aligned or arrived. Transient responses remain recorded, with no added 800 ms dwell rule. Starting already aligned is not a newly achieved turn. [Complete primary table and curriculum](main-results.en.md)

The records help locate apparatus problems. Contact counts and complex trajectories cannot replace task performance, and a broken continuity test cannot reject bodily exploration.

## Conditions and evidence

Two Core/weather sources each undergo 180 preschool seconds, then identical 52-lesson curricula with dense-to-spaced skill review and 12 independent validation demonstrations. Tests last 6 seconds and change targets halfway; every question starts from a clone of the same post-teaching state. Preschool conditions are free exploration, zero-command equal-time exposure, and matched action replay with structural learning disabled only during preschool. Each replay has 3,600 exactly matching sensory frames; learning is restored before teaching.

Normal adapter inference receives only 16 ordered raw Core frames, without task class, object name, correct location or scoring answers. Teacher language and markers use ordinary senses. External supervision uses demonstration action/posture labels during training, not inference. Core dynamics, existing sensory mappings and saved-instance defaults are unchanged. Research interventions operate on new experiment clones only.

Zero-command exposure still has wind and passive physical contact. Fixed anonymous activity wiring and aimless motor variation are recorded separately; variation is not renamed intention. The caregiver moves toys, sounds and supplies short phrases. This is not a full infant/adult simulation; natural-speech recognition, NPC conversation, locomotion, rewards and innate selection are not implemented in this batch.

**A pipeline error, not a minor limitation:** fixed wiring, teacher-driven movement and a new Transformer violate the integrated-system requirement. Continuity applies to each individual and its own peripherals; different groups may differ. Declared ablation, transplantation and cold-start branches remain possible, but must not masquerade as ordinary development. Complete state persistence and learning continuation precede the next rerun; they are not yet implemented.

The environment and senses remain after guidance stops. This 120+60-second observation retained fixed wiring and did not show sustained contact/fixation improvement. **It is not a caregiver test with a jointly developing Transformer**, nor a claim that experience left Core unchanged.

The contact effect of Core control differs by source, and caregiver history does not consistently outperform free exploration. The cut removes Core's contribution while retaining the same motor variation and environment. Fewer contacts can reflect movement biases; more can result from undirected motion. Counts alone do not establish intentional play.

**Preserved methodological correction:** W unintentionally changed wiring while reseeding variation. Its six records remain; W2 retains wiring and changes only variation. Continuous 120+60-second withdrawal is unaffected by that seed error. W2 fixed a local probe, **not the separate fixed-wiring-to-Transformer error in the main pipeline**. ## Internal connections with the adapter held fixed

Source 0 normal, connection-silenced, rescued and empty-activity conditions scored eyes 2/12, 0/12, 2/12 and 0/12; all hands scored 0/12. Silencing changed commands and endpoints in all 12 questions; rescue restored them exactly. Accumulated associative arrival fell from about 9.771 to 0.163. History/reader swaps also changed all 12 command sequences despite equal coarse scores. **Internal connections causally affect this assembly's actions**, but that finding does not repair the interrupted development or establish reliable coordination.

A posture-reading plus physical-servo diagnostic scored eyes 2/12 and hands 2/12 on this source, versus 2/12 and 0/12 for direct forces. Two added hand successes did not meet the preregistered three-success expansion trigger; no second-source posture grid followed. This is an interface lead, not a solution. CUDA fitting took 42.05 seconds; testing about 291.97 computation seconds.

## Earlier success remains the starting point

The saved E56 Core, its shared reader and selected-position physical servo achieved 8/8 held-out color, shape and combined tasks: actual arrival 24/24, same-head connection silencing 19/24, rescue 24/24. Existing qualifications about adapter recognition remain; the entire score is not attributed to Core. The [baseline manifest](baseline-reference.json) records files, identities and the existing database artifact without copying large evidence again.

Later studies changed both the visual field and position-selection-plus-servo into instantaneous four-force prediction. Failure cannot be attributed to vision alone. The next phase preserves the multi-field reference and useful shared reading/execution separation, testing whole-scene vision, gaze and teaching separately. It must not attach an unadapted old multi-field head to a new input layout.

The audit requires rerunning E61's primary question, E60 continuous correction and E59 co-development interpretations. E54–E58 sustained-learning claims need scoped rechecks; fixed-head attribution and shared-reading improvements are not discarded wholesale. E52 continued expression weights and replay but restarted Adam; declared ocean controller/transplant branches are not the same silent replacement error. See R40; not all historical data were recomputed.
