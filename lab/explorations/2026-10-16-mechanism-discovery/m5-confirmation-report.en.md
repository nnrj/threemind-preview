# M5 independent confirmation: fixed personal peripherals express new Core experience, and readout scope determines success

16 October 2026. Forty-eight new independent birth origins across six new cue-dose/retention strata; the previous eight exploratory origins are excluded. Protocol, implementation and primary endpoints were frozen at `54e5b28`, using genuine continuous-memory/5.1. Scope: anonymous associations, a passive answering terminal, declared personal output ports, and singleton probes after 2/5 internal seconds of retention. **This batch confirms all three propositions: new associations formed through subsequent Core teaching are expressed by an already fixed personal port reader; separable local connections causally support new and old associations; and the same Core experience succeeds or fails in expression depending on readout scope. All 48/48 new origins meet every preregistered target, consistently across all six strata.** The earlier eight-origin exploration is now independently confirmed on new origins and conditions.

## Purpose, expectations and decisions

The three complete questions concern fixed-peripheral expression, causal double dissociation of new/old pathways, and the advantage of declared-port over all-activity reading. All three were expected to hold. Opposite histories with identical current cues control sensory-only answers; frozen readers control relearning of labels; matched interventions and restoration control nonspecific damage; old questions test preservation.

Each source effect takes the worse of its two histories. Fixed-expression effect takes the smaller gain over baseline and the plasticity-bundle-off condition; dissociation takes the smallest of four registered selective contrasts. PASS requires at least 36/48 qualifying sources, old-skill preservation, respective mean effects ≥50/50/25 percentage points, and Holm-adjusted p<.05 across the three targets.

## Causal evidence and interpretation

Old teaching calibrates each personal reader on 12 samples, with no-intercept ridge regression, regularization 0.001 and initially fixed scaling. Weights and sufficient statistics stay fixed during new teaching and testing. All-activity and port readers use identical materials, algorithm and Core trajectories, differing in declared feature scope. The Core then receives 16 rounds of new associations with old-association review. Ordinary questions preserve complete current state.

Baseline new answers are zero and old answers perfect. Baseline contains 192 distinct singleton questions per group, scored under two later histories to give 384 nested scores. The sensory-only control gives 0/384 new and 384/384 old answers. Opposite histories both answer correctly under identical current cues, establishing dependence on prior teaching.

**Silencing new connections removes only new expression; silencing old connections removes only old expression; restoration returns the exact responses. This causal double dissociation confirms distinct local support for the two learned sets.** Target and matched sets each contain four connections with equal degree distributions. Maximum effective-weight mean residual is 0.006247904; teaching-activity residual is 0.000260521, below the frozen 0.05/0.02 limits. Restoration score error is zero. Silencing changes only declared silenced_edges. The existing plasticity switch jointly affects learning and retention processes and is interpreted as a bundle, not an isolated parameter.

**The port reader is the clear winner for fixed expression of new singleton associations in this comparison; the fixed all-activity reader expresses none.** Core acquisition and peripheral access must therefore be measured separately. The all-activity reader retains perfect old answers even when the relevant old Core routes are silenced, so old-question accuracy alone does not establish expression of new experience.

## Secondary findings: continued fitting and joint cues

Declared fixed/continued-reader branches start from complete current state. Continued fitting appends six samples while preserving the original 12 and sufficient statistics. On unseen doses 0.86/0.96, all-activity new answers improve 0/768→768/768 and old answers stay 768/768. Port new answers remain 768/768 before and after fitting, with perfect old answers; a ceiling is not an additional gain.

These preregistered crosses never write back to subjects and partly include fitting material, unlike the held-out-dose tests. **Port expression follows the change in Core activity after teaching; continued all-activity fitting assigns new labels even to pre-existing cue representations.** The experiment separates these two learning contributions, so success after peripheral fitting cannot automatically be attributed to new Core memory.

Joint cues keep separate, prospectively specified denominators. Port sham answers are 108/192 new (56.25%) and 120/192 old (62.50%); target silence gives 0/192 and 120/192; matched silence 108/192 and 0/192; restoration 108/192 and 120/192; plasticity-bundle-off gives zero in both. All-activity joint answers are always 0/192 new and 192/192 old. These are equal-total-dose multi-input cues, not combined shape/color naming; they remain secondary M2 cue-condition material and do not replace the singleton targets.

## Scientific conclusion and implications for ThreeMind

This is favorable confirmation of ThreeMind's experience-learning and local-plastic-connection approach: **Core forms newly expressible associations after its personal peripheral has been fixed, preserves the tested old ability, and the two sets have causally separable local carriers.** Fixed all-activity failure versus fixed-port success identifies a practical architectural consequence: exposing more activity does not guarantee better expression of new experience.

Plastic-network learning has prior literature; this batch adds complete causal attribution and new-origin confirmation within the specified model, personal readers and anonymous task.
