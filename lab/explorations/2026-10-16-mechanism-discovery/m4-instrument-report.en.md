## Purpose, expectation and conclusion

The target was reliable preservation and continuation of a physical eye–hand system, including learned peripherals, without observation or wall-clock waiting changing its internal experience. Four three-second paths had to yield identical complete final states and raw input/output trajectories. Predetermined eye, hand and joint forces had to move the physical body, with exact internal-time and optimizer counts.

**The apparatus passes. All four complete final states and trajectories match; 60 before/after observation checks are identical; save/restore and wall-clock pause preserve subsequent inputs, learning and body state.** This is favorable evidence for ThreeMind's experimental integrity and clears the preservation/observation gate for a balanced prospective M4 curriculum. Functional specialization was explicitly assigned to the subsequent scientific experiment, not the instrument acceptance target.
