## Small gaps to repair without declaring them repaired already

1. Lineage: infer at origin/world level; preserve question, descendant, condition and copy nesting. Platform `native_subjects` is an inventory count, not automatically scientific N.
2. Matching: add same-time effective weight, degree and activity residuals to E50-type comparisons; retain original reports separately.
3. Original readback: local selected-summary hashes are verified; full platform readback, parent lineage and endpoint recomputation remain. E50 has two source keys in the backfill index: retain source/project/experiment identities instead of merging by label alone.
4. Boundaries: E69 is unfinished; birth `region` is not a learned brain area; missing hand-only observations stay missing; E67 per-question restoration differs from E68 continuity.
5. Reuse: `state_exchange.py` constructs an engine; historical groups do not certify 5.1 event queues/local clocks. Only source review occurred here, not subject loading or grafting.
6. Observation/background: identical-input invariance differs from changed real telemetry feedback. An offline witness comparator cannot generate evidence of actual observer noninterference.

## Literature scope

Official abstracts/bibliography were checked for [trainable plastic recurrent networks](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v80/miconi18a.html), [biological storage/retrieval separation](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26023136/) and [theory-derived consciousness indicators](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41219038/). Potential additions are selective predictions from local rules, general retrieval/adaptation conditions and mechanistic criterion specificity. This is not a systematic review or basis for global-first claims. Unavailable full texts remain explicitly unreviewed.

Overnight16 October update: the [Ryan et al. main text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5583719/) was accessible. This is a2015 Science paper;2017 is its PMC availability date. Direct optogenetic activation recovered specific memory expression in the animal study, distinguishing storage from natural-cue retrieval. Surviving software edges plus zero fixed readout do not reach that evidence level or establish intact memory content. Miconi et al.2018 already studied trainable plastic recurrent networks; plasticity itself is not a novelty claim. [Butlin et al.](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41219038/) appeared online in November2025 and in the June2026 issue. Only official abstract/bibliography were checked; the DOI full-text route failed, so no claim of auditing its complete indicator list is made. This remains a bounded comparison, not a systematic review.
