## Purpose, expectation and decision

The question was selective transfer of experience content and closed-loop choice benefit, rather than an arbitrary action change. Target transfer was expected to shift both first choice and final gate toward donor experience, outperform sham and matched transfer, spare unrelated tasks and reverse exactly after replacement. Readout stays fixed. Opposite histories receive identical current cues, so history determines the correct direction.

The single primary proposition is conjunctive. Each origin contributes its minimum of eight gains: two histories × first/final endpoint × sham/matched comparator. Qualification requires gain≥0.50, donor accuracy≥0.75 in both histories/endpoints, unrelated loss≤0.10 and exact restoration of reference rates. Batch PASS requires all measurements valid, at least 36/48 qualifying origins, mean minimum gain≥0.50 and source-level p<0.05.

**Observed: 48/48 qualify, mean minimum gain=1.00, unrelated loss=0 and restoration error=0; PASS.** Identical source effects invoke the frozen exact two-sided sign test, p=7.10543×10⁻¹⁵. A 200,000-draw sign-flip sensitivity gives p≈5.0×10⁻⁶. The empirical bootstrap interval is [1,1]; the exact 95% positive-origin fraction interval is [0.9260,1], retaining source sampling uncertainty. Standardized effect and t are undefined at zero variance. Prospective N=48 power at paired standardized effect 0.5 was 92.295%; no outcome-driven addition of sources occurred.
