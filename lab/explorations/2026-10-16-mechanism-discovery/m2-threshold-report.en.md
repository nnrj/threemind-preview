## Interpretation and meaning for ThreeMind

**P2 supports distinct control of a stored association and its present expression.** Filling target resources reveals the taught direction under a medium cue; emptying them suppresses the strong-cue response. Matched interventions do not reproduce these effects, and complete restoration recovers the response exactly. This rejects persistent-weight change as a sufficient explanation and rejects the claim that any equal-sized fast-state manipulation has the same effect. The favorable contribution is selective causal evidence for temporary expression restriction: absence of this response need not mean loss of the experience trace. Causation applies to the manipulated resource bundle, not separately to each of its fields.

**P1 fails because the prediction implementation omitted an existing input-gain factor.** All 16 errors among unseen conditions occur for intact dose 0.57 at zero delay: expression was predicted but absent. Eighty additional errors occur at two seconds and dose 0.55 in the five arms other than target-empty. Thus the first predictor is correct in 608/704 total conditions.

The following is explicitly **retrospective diagnosis**. Native stimulus receipt multiplies input by `direct_gain=0.9`; `m2_threshold_predictor.py` omitted this factor. File-only arithmetic including that existing factor and the receiving node's inhibition response reproduces 704/704 directions, with maximum endpoint error 8.68×10⁻¹⁹. No subject is loaded or advanced, and no Core, frozen predictor or P1 score is changed. This locates an implementation omission; it does not convert retrospective reproduction into prospective success.

The result calls for correcting the research formula and testing it on new materials, not changing Core dynamics. The original failed prediction remains part of the record, alongside the independently supported P2 causal conclusion.
