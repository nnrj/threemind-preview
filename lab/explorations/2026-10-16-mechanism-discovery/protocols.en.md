## Shared data and continuity

Record origin ID, independent-birth basis, genuine kernel/source/build hashes, configuration, interface seed and course seed. Every causal copy records branch ID, complete parent boundary/hash, reason and changed fields. Branches, questions, frames and descendants do not increase independent N. M1 may create declared opposite-history experimental copies from a common blank origin, preserving the reference; this does not permit ordinary students to reset between questions.

Ordinary cultivation follows the current complete state; testing is experience. Development, confirmation and replication use disjoint origins/materials. Mapping seeds are independent of answers. Confirmation cannot select edges, fit readers, choose thresholds or select reported endpoints. Seal origin lists, tasks, generators, input streams, scorers, configurations and hashes before confirmation. Internal plasticity does not authorize rearranging the external answer map.

Minimal event evidence includes origin/branch/stage/question identity, internal start/end times, actual input, teacher assistance, private scorer-truth reference, public output, initial attainment, body trajectory, whole-state reference and event order. Truth cannot enter reader input, servo thresholds, file order, node IDs or cache keys. Randomize table ordering independently. Identical current cues after opposite histories must be byte-identical; identity metadata does not enter sensation.

Compare **effective weights at the same internal time**, not stale lazily stored values. Disclose changes to anchors, fast traces, activity, inhibition, resources and in-flight events. Aggregate repeated questions within origin before origin-level contrasts, intervals and tests. If a paired t analysis is selected, check its distributional suitability; robust or randomization alternatives must be frozen and power-simulated before confirmation.

Freeze the primary comparison family per package, use Holm error control and conservative Bonferroni power planning. Decide shared claim families across phases before outcomes, never split families after inspecting results. Label marginal versus simultaneous intervals; separate exploratory interpretations and post-hoc strata.

## M1 Local carriers and transfer

Anonymous pairing/order families begin from historical AB/CD versus AD/CB and AX→BX versus BX→AX structures. Randomly permute channel labels; letters are observer notation. Generate fixed output projections from declared interfaces before teaching, never by final-label fitting. New combinations, unrelated tasks and closed-loop choice are separate generalization levels, not a pooled accuracy.

Alternatives are current-input shortcuts, residual fast activity, persistent local experience, generic damage and reader relearning. Fixed readers and identical cues constrain shortcuts; separate persistent/fast interventions locate effects. Any change to expiry queues, resources or mappings is disclosed as a combined intervention rather than a weight-only operation.

For each independent origin, retain full reference and declared research copies: intact, sham, target silence, matched silence, release, target graft and matched graft. Shams traverse the same save/restore/conversion without biological-state changes. Recovery compares equal internal times and histories; releasing silence must not erase experiences accumulated during ordinary testing.

Target selection reads development only. Report raw and standardized differences in edge count, effective weight, endpoint degree and development activity. Freeze tolerances before confirmation. Insufficient matching makes a source uninterpretable under preregistered exclusions, not eligible for favorable final-test edge reselection.

Reserve three primary estimates:

1. Selective impairment: loss of opposite-history response contrast under target silence minus loss under matched silence, also reporting shams and unnormalized responses.
2. Donor-directed transfer: a development-defined output direction and target-minus-matched graft displacement. Near-indistinguishable development donors/recipients are inconclusive under a frozen measurability floor; no inflated ratios with near-zero denominators.
3. Specificity/cost: unrelated-task and target-task impairment intervals separately. Nonsignificance is not equivalence. Later behavioral benefit uses actual environment outcomes in separate confirmation.

Cross-subject grafts require declared interface correspondences; no answer-derived alignment. Freeze projection, donor/recipient provenance and development definitions. Replicate with new origins, random mappings and combinations. Response transfer without useful behavior remains a valid narrower result.

## M2 Retention, retrieval, expression and adaptation

Measure retention from the last scheduled teaching event. Independently cultivate continuous 1/30/60/300-second cohorts without earlier probes. Equal-total joint cues reduce per-channel dose; equal-per-channel joint cues increase total dose. Do not pool these as one cue comparison. Register maintenance/background/sensory input during retention: no task cue does not mean no external input.

| Layer | Measurement | Interpretation boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Trace | Declared/development-defined edge-set survival, effective weight, structure and retention quantities; deleted edges count zero | Existing structure is not necessarily usable content; birth templates are not acquired traces |
| Retrieval | Target/associated activity integrals in a fixed simulated window; direct/associative separate | Activity alone is not correct content; exact zero and below-threshold differ |
| Expression | Fixed-reader first answer and autonomous behavior | Reader retraining cannot rescue the fixed-reader endpoint |
| Relearning | Additional experience/updates to a frozen standard, with an untrained control from the same origin | New learning cannot retroactively improve the retention score |

First isolate inhibition, local energy, fast state and input competition; combinations need development evidence. Historical dose/phase/hot-switch replication retains old versions and tables; `/5.1` is separate. Backpropagation belongs only to declared external baselines, not Core.

H/S candidates use existing `consolidation_mode`. H's `memory_learning_gain` candidates are the existing default 0.2, 2 and 4. `memory_half_life_decay_rate` controls relaxation of H itself; zero still allows weight decay under finite H. `edge_consolidation_enabled=false` is not all learning disabled: historical code retains edge creation, eligibility and other processes. These are source-review cautions, not certification of `/5.1` runtime/save behavior; acceptance must verify actual fields and effects.

For adaptation, equalize total experience in stable/reversed/returning environments; report retention, new-rule adaptation area/time-to-standard, interference and relearning. Define at most one simple candidate prediction and comparator from exploration, such as retained weight alone, then compare held-out time/intensity/course prediction error. Freeze complexity, fitting data and failure conditions. Deliver negatives without unbounded search.

## M3 Original criteria and explanatory reach

The [E15 preregistration](../2026-09-29-experience-and-autonomy/design.en.md) defines reproducible experience differences, causal internal activity, internal organization of successive interactions and environmental reshaping of the same individual. Preserve its numerical threshold 0.0001, tenfold-sham-error rule, at least three parents/two wiring seeds, AB factorial and specified windows. Four verbal summaries alone are not the same instrument. E15's four parents belong to two wiring families rather than four independent populations.

Specify all five systems' memory/learning state and complete-state interfaces. A reactive controller truly uses current admissible input only. Declare finite-state capacity/rules, Hebbian updates, recurrent size/reader training and ThreeMind configuration before development. Match information, materials and tuning opportunity; report rather than falsely equalize parameter counts, computation and memory. Strong controls receive reasonable learning opportunities.

For each criterion report pass/fail/undetermined/not-applicable, effects, sham error, internal intervention and reasoning. When historical physical units cannot map to another system, mark noncomparability first. Cross-architecture operationalization needs a separately frozen version; changed units cannot be silently presented as the original numerical test. Unavailable structural interventions are disclosed, not resolved by architecture labels. These comparisons test software behavioral/mechanistic standards, not subjective experience directly.

Closed-loop controls record an origin's actual full sensory stream and replay it into a declared control copy, matching state, seed, sensory budget and action statistics where possible. Open-loop actions cannot change replay: action–sensation coupling is the intended manipulation, not an identical environment claim. Unseen combinations distinguish recombination from old-item memory. Preserve simple controls that pass all four criteria; never raise thresholds after results.

An initial theory table will cover actual recurrent processing/interventions, cross-module information access/pathway blockade, internal-state monitoring/calibration, prediction/error/update, and action-dependent sensing. Each needs theory version, measurement, applicability, simple counterexamples and residual explanations. These are candidate measurement dimensions pending full-text literature mapping, not a claimed transcription of every indicator in the review or a composite consciousness score.
