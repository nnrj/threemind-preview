## Single primary target and inference

For each history and each endpoint (first/final), calculate target-bundle accuracy gains over sham and matched-bundle. A source effect is the minimum of these eight gains. Each history/domain has five cues. Qualification requires minimum gain≥.50, target accuracy≥.75 in both histories and endpoints, maximum unrelated loss≤.10, and reversal accuracy identical to intact for every history/domain/endpoint.

PASS requires all 48 measurements valid, at least 36 qualifying origins, mean source effect≥.50 and a two-sided source-level paired t test against zero with p<.05. For constant effects use the registered exact two-sided source-sign test: all-zero p=1, nonzero p=2^(1−48). Report 20,000 source-bootstrap intervals, exact 95% intervals for positive-source fraction and 200,000 source-sign-flip sensitivity tests; a degenerate empirical bootstrap does not imply population certainty. Mixtures, strata and persistent/fast packages are secondary and cannot rescue a failed primary goal.

Before new data, 20,000 independent paired-normal power simulations at standardized effect .5 and one primary comparison give N48 power .92295, MC95% [.91917,.92657]. This plans test power under its assumptions, not the chance of satisfying every conjunctive gate. Freeze 48; never add sources based on significance. FAIL means the registered effect/consistency target was not achieved; intervals crossing the minimum effect are explicitly insufficient precision, not proof of zero effect.
