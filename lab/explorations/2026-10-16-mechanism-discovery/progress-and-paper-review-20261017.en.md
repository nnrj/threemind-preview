## Scientific conclusions

**M1 confirms that a local persistent structural package can carry and selectively transfer an expressible experience effect.** In interface-aligned, same-origin counterfactual-history copies, all 48 new origins met the complete target; donor-consistent target choices were 480/480, unrelated performance remained 480/480 and restoration was exact. Fixed readers, matched transplantation and unrelated tasks identify content specificity and rule out reader retraining and arbitrary disturbance. This is the main evidence for a causal carrier of experience. The package includes connection structure/state and scheduling indices; the fast-state arm actually made zero changes and is not treated as a sufficient fast-state perturbation. [Report](m1-confirmation-report.en.md)

**M2 establishes distinct conditions for persistent experience and current expression, with prospective numerical prediction.** Local fast-resource interventions switch learned responses on or off while persistent connections remain unchanged; matching and restoration controls hold. The corrected predictor succeeded on 160/160 new conditions in eight new origins, versus 144/160 for the old formula. The original prediction failure remains recorded, and the successor was frozen before testing new materials. This supplies an explicit retrieval mechanism. Conditions are nested within eight origins. Because the predictor is derived from implemented dynamics, its present success validates explanation and prediction in this apparatus; transfer to different structures/curricula has additional scientific value. [Causal report](m2-threshold-report.en.md) · [Prediction](m2-gain-report.en.md)

**M5 confirms that Core can acquire and express new correspondences with a fixed personal reader, and that readout scope causally determines expression.** Each of three targets passed in 48/48 new origins. Fixed-port new and old responses were both 384/384. Selective new/old pathway silencing produced double dissociation. The fixed all-activity reader scored 0/384 on new responses, so the port reader clearly won that registered comparison. Offline crossing and peripheral retraining separately locate the reader's own contribution. This distinguishes internal learning, retrieval and expression rather than assigning every score to Core. [Report](m5-confirmation-report.en.md)

**M3 answers its registered comparison: ThreeMind is the only one of five tested implementations to meet all four joint criteria.** It passed jointly in 64/64 new origins; each comparator passed jointly in 0/64. Individual criteria achieved by simple comparators remain reported. This supports the coexistence of internal-history effects, local causal dependence, temporal/nonlinear interaction and closed-loop adaptation. The comparison concerns these implementations and materials; architecture names do not establish that all recurrent networks must fail. [Report](m3-confirmation-report.en.md)
