## Corrections and prospective progress

First, no reading on the original grid had been interpreted too broadly as no activity throughout. Source and traces suggested millisecond-scale decay near the.004 floor. Dense instrument replay found it without changing final whole states. **The defensible claim is absent sustained late expression, not absence of every transient response.** Original reports/archives remain; bilingual corrections were appended to the original record.

Second, input time differs from emission/arrival time. The prespecified timing approximation failed. A separate frozen study with new origins/material then validated the common-cycle/first-batch boundary account. With X at99ms and Y moved100→101ms, summed first-cycle contrast drops .014548515→.007262309, while normalized response changes only slightly. The failed model was not edited into a retrospective success.

This supports an implementation-informed, testable response condition. The equation follows known code, so the present contribution is mechanism localization, prediction scope and measurement reliability. **It is not yet a new universal memory law or a solution to consciousness.** Plastic-network and biological storage/retrieval studies remain novelty comparators, not findings this batch has demonstrated it surpasses.
