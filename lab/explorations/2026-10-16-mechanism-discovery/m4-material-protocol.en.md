# M4: identifiability audit of completed E69 material

Preregistered read-only analysis, 2026-10-16, R11. Determine whether committed E69 material supports the planned gaze/hand/joint functional-differentiation comparison and establish concrete gaps for prospective M4. This is measurement preparation, not completion of the functional-organization mechanism study.

Freeze all 192 complete development questions in gaze-dev-0–3 and coordination-dev-0–3, excluding unfinished semantics and absent final tests. Hash every selected unit, clip, stage result, chain boundary and current STOP/latest before motion stratification. There is one independent complete individual; questions/frames are nested. Exclude assisted/teaching observations. The tool uses only standard-library JSON/gzip reads, never imports Core or a runner.

Primary movement thresholds: eye speed≥.08rad/s (the existing gaze-stability boundary), hand speed≥.01m/s, at least six consecutive frames/.3s. Count all frames, qualifying episodes and questions for eye-only, hand-only, joint and neither movement. New MOVE/WAIT commands and inherited motion remain separate. Report all nine sensitivity pairs: eye .04/.08/.16 × hand .005/.01/.02. Inertia or naturally observed movement is not an isolated training condition.

Availability gate: each of the three moving strata must occur in at least eight qualifying questions in both early rounds0/1 and later rounds2/3. A full three-condition functional-differentiation test also requires isolated-hand training, interleaved task conditions and independent subsequent confirmation material. These latter conditions are known absent; the expected decision is that dedicated prospective M4 remains necessary. Quantify coverage; do not infer absence of natural functional organization, or declare the hypothesis verified from post-hoc clustering.

Check availability of node id/channel/birth_us and activation/inhibition/direct/associative fields. Preassigned region is not a learned functional area, and coactivation is not measured edge traffic. Do not fit/select functional groups in this audit. Deliver the frozen manifest, all sensitivity counts and bilingual decision with original references/hashes through the existing API. E69 remains read-only, its STOP is retained, and no simulation advances. Balanced tasks, randomized mappings, independent origins and selective interventions belong to the subsequent prospective M4 study.
