Registered 2026-11-18. The author authorized a small matched comparison of the genuine threemind.continuous-memory/1 implementation, current threemind.continuous-memory/6.3 defaults, and the previously successful 6.3 prefix configuration. ## Material and controls

Use E13 peripheral seeds 9293201 and 9293202 and the saved 60-internal-second G0 c0 and c2 histories (initial positions 0.125 and 0.625). These predetermined starts represent both regions. Replay exactly the recorded sensory and host-telemetry inputs across three configurations. Apply recorded actions during this controlled teaching and retain each Core's proposed actions. Subsequent behavioral assessments are closed-loop. No current host telemetry or AS63-01 constant background is substituted.

Before this study, inspection established that the old motor adapter hashes node IDs, whereas /1 assigns IDs in formation order and 6.3 addresses nodes by channel code. Identical adapter code and seed therefore need not yield identical channel-to-motor coefficients.

`legacy1` loads each archived individual's complete /1 configuration. `default63` uses all current 6.3 defaults, setting only the peripheral seed and enabling energy simulation. `prefix63` loads the entire saved R51t16 `S101/bypass` configuration, with only those same overrides. Its prior success concerns the prefix task, not the present ring task. Each version retains its real identity and birth rule. Energy supply is one unit every 0.1 internal seconds; sensing every second; motor control every 0.1 seconds; mass 1, drag 0.8, and force scale 0.3.

For every history/configuration pair, retain four states: a blank control, the individual after matched teaching, its newborn offspring, and that offspring after another continuous 60-second presentation of the same curriculum. Assessments use complete-state research copies; birth is invoked only when producing offspring. The source individual remains intact.

Test every state from positions 0.0625 and 0.5625 for 60 seconds, with fixed region layout, under two motor rules. `id` retains the original hash-of-real-node-ID rule. `aligned` freezes the archived channel-to-node-ID bindings and uses their motor coefficients across all configurations and the blank controls. Complete the absent positive-velocity channel for seed 9293201 by assigning IDs above the historical maximum in channel order; unknown channels are errors. This is a declared peripheral control, not a change to Core addresses or production peripherals. A blank control using the archived table has not learned that history.

Two additional five-second fixed-body research copies per state receive region-0 or region-1 inputs and identical background. Save activity and calculate both motor interpretations offline. Measure connections, associative-activation integrals, and channel-aligned activity differences. Do not pool non-equivalent version-specific state fields.

Total: 12 initial teaching stages, 12 offspring-development stages, 192 behavioral assessments, and 96 fixed-body diagnostics: 312 stages. The four historical materials are not four randomly independent population samples. All copies, configurations and starts are paired within materials; report both peripheral blocks and every material separately.

## Prospective criteria

T0 validity: the four /1 teaching replays must reproduce archived channel activations and motor outputs at all 600 samples within maximum absolute error 1e-10. Before formal work, check passive observation, complete save/restore, host pause, and short native/reference agreement for all configurations. Verify matched input times, intensities and channels. Death, unmet energy demand, unknown motor channels or configuration mismatch stop the study and preserve evidence.

T1 formation: compare pre-birth connections, activity and behavior with same-configuration blank controls. Sum of absolute channel-integral differences greater than 1e-6 in fixed-input diagnostics counts as a measurable history effect. Fewer connections do not mean worse learning; near-50% occupancy does not mean absence of a behavioral tendency. Also report travel, circling and stationary fractions.

T2 inheritance: predefined target is region 0 for c0 and region 1 for c2. A developed parent qualifies at target occupancy at least 75%; its offspring retains that tendency at at least 75%. A decline of at least 10 percentage points is substantial. A parent that never qualified cannot establish loss through birth. Report offspring redevelopment separately and inspect node, edge and weight correspondence. Expected short-term clearing or persistent-state settlement under the native birth rule is not an implementation defect.

T3 expression: changing only the motor interpretation is substantial if target occupancy changes by at least 10 percentage points. A 6.3 condition recovers a material if alignment raises it to at least 75% and closes at least half its gap from /1 under the original rule. Include blank controls and offline interpretation of identical activity when attributing this effect.

T4 prefix configuration: call a material improved only if prefix63 raises original-rule target occupancy by at least 10 percentage points versus default63 and reaches 75%. A whole-configuration comparison cannot identify an individual switch; no ring improvement does not invalidate the earlier prefix result.

These are diagnostic criteria, not new joint consciousness criteria. Report continuous values and every target's outcome. No population significance tests or overall kernel rankings are inferred from two peripheral blocks.
