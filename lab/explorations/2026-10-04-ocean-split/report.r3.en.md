## Plain-language interpretation appended after completion, 2026-10-04

**This cultivation produced repeat consumers and natural descendants with both turning/feeding and consumption records.** Eighteen partial consumptions involved eleven consumers. Individual 5 succeeded five times, from 330.98 to 821.92 s, spanning about 491 s; individuals 6, 27 and 38 each succeeded twice. These provide stronger reuse candidates than a single successful instant. Individual 5 remained present with body energy at 900 s and had three natural children, although the children did not repeat its consumption behavior in this batch.

Individuals 6, 12, 13, 21, 59 and 60 have both types of record. Individual 6 has one turn/feeding episode and two consumptions. Individual 59, a first-generation child of 25, has three turn/feeding episodes and one consumption. Individual 60, a first-generation child of 3, has one of each. Relative to their parents, 59 has a 3.68% larger radius and 5.35% less thrust; 60 has a 7.00% smaller radius and 4.62% more thrust. Combined observations therefore occur in descendants that became either larger or smaller. This does not establish those mutations as their cause. No preregistered parent-rate improvement marker was reached.

Successful behavior did not guarantee survival: 5 consumed 12 at 502.52 s, 6 consumed 60 at 783.78 s, and 5 consumed 59 at 821.92 s. Their earlier living event states were archived. The worlds were saved and paused at 900 s, without deleting remaining bodies or reviving lost individuals. Reuse of a species bundle creates an explicitly inherited new individual.

After the fork, the turning branch added 31 qualifying turn/feeding episodes, 13 by natural descendants; the coverage branch added 25, 14 by descendants. Turning-branch individuals 25, 28, 31 and 34 each accumulated three events and remained present at the endpoint. Both cultivation lines are retained. These counts are not a contest between controllers: every controller is Core and the environments differ in consumption permission.

Shell costs and continuous dwell were retained in all eighteen consumptions. Seven prey had no body reserve, yet yielded previously paid construction material. For example, individual 8 received 51.79 material energy at 320 s and separately paid 17.68 shell cost. This shows the material rule operating in the habitat; a long-term fraction-0/0.5 comparison has not been performed.

**The useful next step is to reuse these stored candidates.** Prioritize 5 for repeated consumption, 6 for repeated consumption plus turning, and 59 for repeated turning plus consumption. First test recurrence in complete new oceans, then use matched copies, internal-activity suppression and structure/state transplantation to separate opportunity, body and experience contributions. These are recommendations, not additional experiments run in this batch.
