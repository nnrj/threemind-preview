# R51t14 interpretation addendum: separate resources and transmission-time updates

Appended 15 November 2026. The [original report](report.en.md), measurements, criteria and delivery package remain intact.

The user requested a test of independent excitatory and inhibitory resources, not a claim that shared resources were the sole cause. The report connected its sufficiency hypothesis with an interpretation of a sole remedy, which could misattribute the assistant's reasoning to the user. Independent resources were a candidate improvement requested by the user; the sole-cause interpretation was the assistant's addition.

The independent-resource groups in R51t14 already had inhibitory updates of the most recent transmission time disabled. With both settings, neither prefix pair was correctly distinguished in any of the four configurations. The original 0/2 results remain unchanged.

The earlier R51t11 positive combination also disabled explicit inhibitory learning, bypassed resources for inhibition and used a zero inhibitory connection signal threshold. Independent resources still deplete and recover. Disabling transmission-time updates does not disable the other inhibitory learning contributions. Whether those differences cause the contrast in performance requires matched tests. The new [R51t15 protocol](../prefix_causes/protocol.en.md) reconstructs the complete positive combination in an isolated 6.3 research implementation and compares the relevant conditions, without changing historical scores or production defaults.
