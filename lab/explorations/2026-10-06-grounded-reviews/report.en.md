This bounded study is complete; the main capability target is not met. Preserve the [original design](design.en.md) and [dated execution amendment](execution-notes.md).

**Conclusion: some shape/colour questions can be answered, but reliable pointing to the requested object has not been achieved.** The three motor heads reach 0/32, 1/32 and 1/32 targets. Occasional success is not mastery. Continuous bodily background, ordinary-strength teaching and initially dense, then sparse reviews are retained. Attribute/object binding remains unreliable, and low demonstration error does not become actual movement ability. These results do not yet identify a missing Core mechanism; Core is unchanged.

## Completed recognition tests

**Actual Core activity supports some correct shape/colour answers and sensitivity to the teacher's pointer, but not reliable binding of those attributes to the requested object.** Restoring physical background and expanding reviews does not by itself solve that problem. There is no matched no-background group in E55, so this does not establish that background is useless; all groups retain it.

Each scene contains four objects with each attribute appearing once. Test every pointing location and both attribute questions while holding the scene/source fixed. Both heads fit 96/96 calibration questions, yet performance falls on tests. Shuffling the normal input processes gives 11/48 familiar answers; zero activity gives 6/48. The head uses activity rather than returning one invariant word. All twelve scene/category groups change their answer with the pointer, but only three groups answer all four objects correctly. Sensitivity is not correctness.

Same-head edge silencing changes nine answers: four correct-to-wrong and two wrong-to-correct. A net loss of two is not a large dependence on association. Unsilencing the fixed first eight trials restores raw features exactly (maximum difference zero), recovering 8/8 answers. The intervention silences edges present at the branch point, without retrospectively withdrawing in-flight signals or forbidding newly created test edges. It does not remove every internal process. The no-plasticity control retains activation, inhibition and reception history; it is not an empty constant system.

Do not subtract E55's 29/48 from E54's ordinary 42/48 and call it a regression: the earlier ordinary scenes admitted frequency shortcuts removed here. Relative to E54's strict 8/32, materials, teaching and calibration coverage also differ. E55 measures current usefulness; isolating the contribution of each modification requires matched controls.

Familiar views with new arrangements still score 20/48, so unfamiliar viewing angle alone cannot explain failure. Untrained-combination shape questions score 0/8 in both sources, while normal colour questions score 6/8. Attribute recognition, reference selection, sentence form and actual limb control need separate diagnosis before claiming composition.

## What was actually taught and sensed

Four grey solids precede four colours. Each concept receives twelve consecutive complete lessons followed by item-specific 12/24/48/96-second review phases and a final review: 21 presentations each, 168 lessons over 516 simulated seconds. Text intensity remains one, with 50 ms character timing and unchanged normalization/anonymous mapping.

Wind, ambient temperature/humidity, body temperature, acceleration, gravity support and motor power remain sampled throughout. Wind physically moves the pointing carriage and heat exchange changes its temperature. Existing position, velocity and contact senses remain. No semantic body or temporal-answer labels are injected. Weather is independent of answers and observer CPU load.

Heads read raw output-bus observation frames: activation, inhibition, direct and associative contact. They receive neither full edge graphs, baseline-subtracted responses nor per-example rescaling. Teacher targets exist only in adapter training/scoring. Normal and no-learning sources receive the same curriculum except for Core plasticity; their separate heads share architecture and fitting steps. Question branches are not independently trained consciousness subjects.

Teaching corner counts are random rather than exactly balanced (46/36/34/52); this was corrected in the execution record. Calibration/tests balance the locations. Per-item review phase intervals expand, but cross-phase and initial-block boundaries are not globally monotonic. No fitted human forgetting-curve claim is made.

The normal checkpoint has 403 nodes and 147341 edges, weight sum about 77183.45; 9194 edges join background ports with other ports. This confirms physical input participation, not self-awareness or task mastery. Associative contact is zero at the single 516-second checkpoint, but nonzero in nine of seventeen frames in the fixed first question. Dynamic evidence remains intact; a single frame cannot erase activity in the process.

## Actual motion

The [registered amendment](execution-notes.md) continues from that 516-second source. Twelve taught colour/shape pairs each receive twelve consecutive force-guided demonstrations and three expanding reviews. Identical motor heads compare the most recent four frames with sixteen samples spanning the current request/action episode. The longer history is a bounded external observation capability, not newly introduced Core memory. Teacher forces are used only during demonstration; tests provide no target coordinates or teacher marker.

Teaching completed 180 lessons over 684 simulated seconds, bringing the source to 1200 seconds including the earlier classroom. Teacher-guided movement reached all 180 targets, confirming that the planar inertial pointer can perform the action under the current wind/drag. There are 1800 supervised examples.

**Longer observation reduces demonstration fitting error without improving actual reaching.** Thirteen nearest-object choices change between heads, with median endpoint separation 0.538 m, but each has ten correct choices. Change is not improvement. At a familiar view the episode head still selects only 5/16 correctly and reaches none; the recent head selects 7/16 and reaches one. Unfamiliar angle alone cannot explain failure.

Descriptive kinematics: the recent head moves closer in 23/32 trials by 0.25 seconds but only 17/32 by two seconds; median progress falls from 0.055 m to 0.019 m. The episode head's median two-second progress is negative, −0.104 m. These measured trajectories do not establish stable goal control.

After both primary groups, pause the automatic large control sequence. Changed-request, edge-silence, zero-activity and rescue motor groups remain unexecuted. The changed-request stage initialized but stopped through this study's STOP file before its first input; preserve its record rather than count it as Core failure or a negative trial. Prioritize improving 0–1/32 actual reaching before extensive attribution.

### Whole-demonstration validation and exploratory task retest

Hold out each combination's last review: twelve complete episodes/120 samples; train on the other 168 episodes/1680 samples. Select the additional full-process head by held-out loss, without formal task-test answers. Training MSE is 0.03529 and held-out MSE 0.07872, versus 0.23895 for constant training-mean actions: about 67% lower. The records contain usable action-related information, but this does not identify a Core target-intention mechanism.

The candidate fails the registered absolute 0.05 gate; preserve `passed_registered_gate=false`. That threshold has not been calibrated to physical reaching accuracy. A dated post-result decision permits one 32-trial task retest within remaining resources. This is an explicit exploratory deviation from the expansion gate, not preregistered validation success or independent confirmation. The 0.15 m reaching criterion remains unchanged.

The retest **reaches 1/32, enters the target in 4/32, selects the correct nearest object in 6/32, and has median endpoint error 0.968 m**. For untaught combinations, nearest selection is 1/8 and reaching 0/8. It does not improve on the two primary heads. Better held-out demonstration prediction than constant force does not imply autonomous control after teacher removal. Incorrect target selection, activity drift from demonstration, and compounding sensory changes caused by erroneous actions are distinct possible explanations, not mechanisms established by these scores.

## Scope and open work

Following the user's renewed focus, remaining resources prioritize actual pointing. The planned equal-interval course and second wiring source remain unexecuted. E55 therefore does not establish an independent spacing/background advantage; E43's earlier retention evidence remains unchanged. Energy-based motivation, self/other concepts, further imitation and RGB/cone-channel comparisons remain open.

Core dynamics, defaults, historical world protocols and old evidence are unchanged. No pretrained language model supplies answers. Partial finite-vocabulary recognition is useful, but reliable compositional recognition and word-guided pointing have not yet been established by the completed stages.

Next priorities (not executed): separate learned object/target selection from motor execution, retaining direct-force control; calibrate using whole new demonstration episodes and compare current-source calibration with continuous co-development; teach common reference through moving, pointing to, touching and describing the same objects. Physical gaze/hand movement may change local sampling without deleting the environment or writing semantic importance into Core. Equal-interval/second-source controls, energy-shaped motivation, imitation/self–other and RGB/cone comparisons remain open in R35 and related plans. Low fitting loss alone does not justify changing defaults.
