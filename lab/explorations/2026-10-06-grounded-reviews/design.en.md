# E55: continuous bodily background and item-specific spaced classroom reviews

Registered 2026-10-06, following E54. Historical results/criteria and Core dynamics, defaults and sensory mapping remain intact.

The user requires CPU telemetry or equivalent simulated physical background throughout teaching, and the initially dense, later sparse review direction supported in E43. E54 did have position, velocity, contact, teacher-spot and visual measurements, but lacked changing environmental background. Its ten repeats plus two review rounds did not implement that schedule. The claim that missing background prevents self/world differentiation remains a hypothesis, not an established explanation of E54.

New external protocol `threemind.grounded-classroom/1` retains E54's 272 ports and adds twenty signed components: wind, ambient temperature, humidity, body temperature, acceleration, vertical specific force, support force and motor power. Wind exerts force on the carriage; heat exchange changes its temperature. Humidity is a prescribed measurable field, without an invented mechanical effect. Gravity is resisted by the horizontal guide. These are simple simulated physics, not real CPU measurements or a full weather simulator. A fixed independent weather phase, body dynamics and all units/scales are recorded and recoverable. New port gain is fixed at 0.02; existing visual/body gains remain 0.03/0.5. Every condition receives continuous background, including waits and probes. Nothing depends on question answers or observer load.

Core remains continuous-memory/2 with its existing forgetting, inhibition, depletion/recovery and death; extra facilitation remains disabled. Text uses ordinary intensity 1, anonymous mapping, NFKC and 50 ms character timing. The output head receives raw activity/inhibition/direct/associative observation frames, not the connection graph, baseline-subtracted responses or per-example rescaling. Teaching labels remain outside Core and inference.

Eight concepts (four shapes, four colours) first receive twelve consecutive complete question/answer lessons each. Grey solids precede colour cards. Position and view vary independently. Reviews use four-object scenes with every shape/colour equally represented, all pointing locations and both question types. Initial starts are 1.5 s apart; review phases use per-item intervals 12, 24, 48 and 96 s, twice per phase, followed by a common final review. Each concept has 21 presentations: 168 lessons, 516 simulated seconds. Cross-phase/initial-block boundary intervals are explicitly listed rather than called globally monotonic. This is an expanding curriculum, not a fitted human forgetting curve.

Primary: learning with spaced reviews. Mechanism control: identical inputs, Core plasticity off. Timing control: identical lesson order/dose, 1.5 s duration, total duration and final review times, with uniform 44.8 s middle review cycles. All retain the physical background and full environment. During waits the four-object neutral scene remains, with the teacher spot removed. Start with source/wiring 223's three groups; if resources allow, repeat learned/frozen groups on 279. Do not broaden indefinitely when a clear bottleneck has been located.

Individual tiny Transformer heads are calibrated using 96 actual observation branches: three non-diagonal pairings, four views, all four pointing positions and both attributes. Freeze heads before formal tests. Primary tests: 48 familiar-pair questions in new views/arrangements and 16 held-out-pair questions, changing pointer/category within otherwise identical scenes and source states. Report shape, colour, wrong-category, pointer sensitivity and correctness separately. Chance references are 1/4 within category and 1/8 among all labels; scene-related questions are not independent subjects. Include frozen-learning, zero and shuffled-process controls. Only a credible advantage over no-learning motivates existing-edge silence/rescue attribution. Reader sensitivity alone is not correctness. Any additional fitting requires a dated amendment and uses no test targets.

Keep process trajectories, structural statistics and background-only windows. Eight-word classification is not general dialogue; failure does not establish absence of all learning. Subsequent motor/imitation and relational work depend on the observed bottleneck.

Budgets: account baseline 62%; +5 percentage points stops expansion, +8 stops new simulation. Keep 2 GiB available RAM, 5 GiB free C and 15 GiB free E. Refresh usage within 30 minutes. No old-data deletion, broad data scans, service restarts, Docker or paid language calls. Publish plans, bilingual reports, actual inputs/states/heads through a separate ThreeMind project; update histories and R35 while preserving E54.
