# 3-SAT local-choice pilot v1
2026-11-01. Explicitly authorized new study R42. Earlier E69/M4/M6/M7 studies and automations remain paused. This pilot tests experience-dependent search, not P versus NP or the invention of a new SAT algorithm.

## Scientific question and division of work
The complete system solves 3-SAT with a declared hybrid local-search scaffold. The environment uniformly selects an unsatisfied clause and computes the break count of flipping each of its three candidate variables. Only one of four categories (0, 1, 2, or at least 3) reaches Core as an anonymous stimulus. The environment does not select the preferred candidate or reveal a satisfying assignment. A fixed personal readout subtracts N-port activation from P-port activation. The highest score chooses a flip, with uniform ties and 25% uniform exploration.
The teacher demonstrates zero-break preference using actual local states of separate training formulas. This is an experimenter-selected heuristic, not a claim that Core invented it. Categories each receive 16 presentations; U/Q/R duplicates provide a task-irrelevant matched pathway. The shuffled-history control rotates the preferred category every round, preserving category exposure, total dose, port marginal counts and duration. No reader fitting or Core backpropagation is used. Kernel identity is genuine continuous-memory/5.1 with default dynamics and individual seeds.

## Sources, material and continuity
Eight independent birth seeds 2011100–2011107; paired histories and causal branches are nested observations, not additional independent N. Apparatus seed 2011000 is excluded from scientific results.
Each source has eight 12-variable/50-clause training formulas. Random 3-CNF is conditioned on satisfiability using an independent DPLL reference, without planting assignments. Sixteen actual local examples cover each break category. Held-out tests comprise 12 formulas each at 12 variables/50 clauses and 20 variables/84 clauses, interleaved, with randomized variable names and clause order. Canonical formulas do not overlap training. Oracle answers remain in the material evidence and cannot enter actor or readout interfaces.
Two 3-variable/eight-clause unsatisfiable sentinels test false positives only. The pilot does not require proving UNSAT. Each problem permits 96 flips. A separate bit-mask witness checker must validate every SAT claim; failure is UNKNOWN.
Training is 128 presentations of 250ms plus 1s before and after (34 internal seconds). A query lasts 200ms with readout at 110ms. Background K=0.2 arrives every 500ms. Teaching activates two channels at 0.4 each; a query activates one category channel at 0.8. No answer-bearing test feedback, tuning or per-question restoration. All ordinary stages preserve full state, clock, queue, mappings, reader definition, background cursor, recent activity, world and RNG.

## Registered causal copies
Five separate branches (intact, sham, target, matched, restored) originate from each intact post-teaching state at 34s. The shuffled history is a sixth condition. Each branch continuously experiences its complete test sequence.
Target edges are the four C-to-taught-P/N directed edges. Matches are four U-to-Q/R edges, selected only from teaching and the current graph. Four pairs, equal corresponding degrees, maximum weight difference <=0.05, and maximum same-phase mean teaching-activity difference <=0.05 are required. Publish all residuals. Invalid matching invalidates G2, not the independently valid performance observations.
Use existing silence/restore interventions only; immediate differences must be confined to silenced_edges. Restoration must exactly recover the pre-intervention scientific state. Sham applies an empty mask. These declared research copies do not authorize resetting an ordinary individual.

## Targets fixed before scientific testing
G0 apparatus: independently check witness validation, break counts and DPLL against small exhaustive enumeration; full restore and subsequent response equality; passive observation invariance; intervention field restriction; answer isolation. Apparatus failure is invalid measurement and requires repair before scientific testing.
G1 actual search benefit: intact minus shuffled mean held-out SAT success >=0.10 and a positive source difference in at least 6/8 sources. Report paired effects and a fixed-seed 10,000 source-bootstrap interval. This is an exploratory selection rule, not a large-sample confirmation. Failure to meet the rule does not imply a zero population effect when meaningful effects remain inside the interval.
G2 causal local preference: for actual non-noise decisions with both zero-break and nonzero-break candidates, mean sham-minus-target zero-break choice >=0.20, absolute mean sham-minus-matched <=0.05; at least 20 eligible decisions per source and relevant arm; all matching gates pass; intact/sham/restored scientific trajectories agree exactly. G2 concerns candidate choice and cannot substitute for G1. Also report the interventions' SAT-success and capped-flip effects.
G3 practical comparison: report actual outcomes against random local selection and standard WalkSAT (zero-break priority, otherwise 0.5 noise/minimum break). No assumed winner. DPLL supplies material truth/reference timing only, not actor computation.
Report purpose, expectation, observations, analysis, explicit scientific conclusions and implications for ThreeMind, separately for each target. Preserve every failure and denominator. No post-result additional teaching or revised pass rules. Report training and solving costs separately and cumulatively, wall time, flips, internal seconds, input counts, graph size, process memory and evidence storage. Small-range scaling is not a complexity proof.
