# R63c: kernel 6.3 retains bodily experience and recalls it through the relevant connections

15 November 2026. This batch repeats the E18 bodily-experience and process-readout experiment under comprehensive revalidation plan v2. Four independent encoding blocks provide the formal materials. Configurations, retention intervals and intervention copies within each block are paired conditions, not additional independent samples. Sixteen apparatus-calibration conditions are separate from the 616 formal stages.

## Purpose and expectation

The experiment asks whether joint sensory and bodily experience remains available for recall after 1, 60 or 300 internal seconds, and whether the response depends on connections formed through experience. Trained conditions were expected to express the taught direction, whereas untrained references were not. Target blockade was expected to disrupt expression, matched blockade to have little effect, and immediate removal of the blockade to restore expression.

Each block includes ordinary and facilitation configurations and five sensory conditions: full sensory input, no inertial input, dose-matched input, shuffled inertial input and no training. Physical trajectories and sensory materials were frozen. Registered research copies begin from complete post-teaching states. An independent background stimulus K continues every 0.5 s during the waiting intervals. Passive readout records activation, inhibition, direct activation and associative activation without learning or advancing the internal clock.

The native analyzer reads target nodes at convergence. The process measure instead uses the complete five-second trajectory, subtracts the corresponding no-cue trajectory and compares the two target activation integrals. Both measurements were specified before execution and are reported separately.

## Results

| Comparison | Result | Finding |
|---|---|---|
| Process response after training | 192/192 follow the taught direction | Experience can be recalled at all three retention intervals |
| Untrained process response | 0/48 | The corresponding response depends on teaching |
| Target blockade after 300 s | 0/32 retain the corresponding response | Relevant connections causally support expression |
| Matched blockade | 32/32 retain the response | The effect is selective for the relevant connections |
| Immediate removal of target blockade | 32/32 restored; complete final states and recorded processes equal the references | Removing the blockade restores expression |
| Native convergence-time readout | 0/240; targets are not elicited at the convergence endpoint | This endpoint misses target responses that occurred earlier |

Every trained sensory condition scores 8/8 at each interval under both configurations. Immediately before the 300-s probes, target activation, inhibition and direct activation are all zero; the largest recorded associative-activity value is 0.00114034. The cue subsequently elicits the taught response in the complete process. The absence of current target activation therefore coexists with retained, recallable experience.

All four trained sensory conditions reach the process-score ceiling. This measure shows no additional benefit from inertial sensation. Subsequent bodily-transfer and inertial-reversal experiments examine the response direction and transfer produced by physical sensory input itself.

## Scientific conclusion and significance

Kernel 6.3 reproduces retention of bodily experience, cue-dependent recall and the selective causal contribution of the relevant connections. Reading only at convergence misses information already expressed during the response; recording the complete process detects that information. This experiment measures retention with background input. Recall after a 300-s delay and sustained activity without external input remain separate experimental questions.

The results establish that ThreeMind retains and expresses experience through relevant connections after the timely weak-activation settlement fix. The failed convergence-time readout is retained as a distinct outcome. No new adverse contradiction or resource stop was triggered.
