# R63u preparation checks

16 November 2026. This study is not registered and no subjects have been created. Prerequisites are R63g and R63q.

The tools retain the four X2 retention delays and eight independent origins per delay. The primary condition uses the 6.3 default; the existing stability-retention option provides a paired comparison. Only `consolidation_mode` differs between these configurations. The teaching function has the same syntax tree as the original X2 function. Cue doses, observation times and the original dissociation criteria are unchanged.

Read-only checks cover 256 diagnostic processes from the 32 historical origins. Independent high-precision integrals differ from the recorded values by at most 1.0842×10⁻¹⁹. Cue-dependent dissociation passes in 0, 8, 8 and 0 origins at 1, 30, 60 and 300 s; transient expression passes in 8, 8, 8 and 0, respectively, matching the historical scores. Every previously taught and untaught individual reached the old relearning criterion in the minimum two rounds, confirming the measurement floor.

The new course therefore calibrates a process-response standard using independent materials before any formal subjects are born. Courses are bounded at 16 rounds. If calibration cannot resolve a learning trajectory, the measurement is reported as unavailable; formal outcomes will not be used to tune the threshold. Synthetic counterexamples verify that success for one direction cannot pass a two-history retrieval criterion, floor and censoring effects cannot be presented as a sensitive learning-speed comparison, and retrieval by a weak cue does not become a false loss of retrieval when the original dissociation criterion fails.

The audit will distinguish stored, sleeping and graph-visible connections. A connection disappearing from the displayed graph is not evidence of deletion. Pauses occur only after a complete origin/configuration unit. Analysis checks complete initial and final states, chained state hashes for every lesson, all stimuli and process records. Intermediate lessons retain hashes and the complete continuing history rather than repeatedly copying the accumulating full state.

These checks created no Core instances. Registration, actual apparatus-equivalence checks for both configurations, independent calibration and the complete storage budget remain required before formal execution. Preparation checks are not new scientific results for 6.3.
