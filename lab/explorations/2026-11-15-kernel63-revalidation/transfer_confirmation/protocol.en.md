## Scientific question

Can experience-formed local connections selectively transfer a learned response and its consequences for closed-loop choice while preserving unrelated responses? Does this effect extend to new independent sources, teaching intensities, retention delays and untrained stimulus combinations? Target transfer is expected to increase donor-consistent first choices and final door positions after feedback. Matched and sham transfer should not produce the same benefit; replacing the original connections should restore the reference.

## Intervention and task

Connection exchange and same-time preparation checks follow [R63g](../transfer/protocol.en.md). Two directed XY-to-A/B connections are targeted; two ZW-to-A/B connections provide the matched control. Counts and endpoint degrees match, and mean contemporaneous weight and teaching-activity residuals must each be at most 0.02. Selection uses teaching data alone. Interface correspondence never depends on final answers. Bundle, persistent and fast exchanges are reported by their actual changed fields; zero-change fast exchanges are identified. Parents and donors remain intact.

At 110 ms, fixed A−B output with thresholds ±1e−4 selects the positive door, negative door or no door. This actual choice determines the next sensory input at 250 ms: X, Z or N, respectively, at dose 0.2. At 360 ms a second response updates door position, limited to −1, 0 or +1. Observation continues to 1.5 seconds. The environment receives no correct answer, donor history or group label. First choice and final position are scored separately; unrelated tasks retain the recipient's original target.

## Registered decision rules

The original confirmation has one primary target. For each source, take the minimum of eight target-accuracy gains: two histories, first/final endpoints and contrasts against sham/matched transfer. A source qualifies only if this minimum is at least 0.50, target accuracy is at least 0.75 for both histories and endpoints, maximum unrelated loss is at most 0.10, and restored and intact accuracies agree throughout.

Confirmation requires all 48 sources valid, at least 36 qualifying sources, mean minimum gain at least 0.50, and a two-sided source-level paired t test against zero with P<0.05. Identical source gains use the predefined exact two-sided sign test: P=1 for all-zero gains and P=2^(1−48) otherwise. Report 20,000 source bootstrap draws, an exact 95% interval for the positive-source proportion and 200,000 sign-flip sensitivity draws. Strata, novel combinations and persistent/fast packages are secondary and cannot replace the primary target.

Historical planning power at standardized paired effect 0.5 was 0.92295 for N48. It remains a planning assumption; no significance-driven expansion is allowed. Passing confirms selective transfer of learned responses and closed-loop choices through the selected local connections across the new materials. Failure must identify the unmet effect or consistency requirement. A valid, clear adverse discrepancy invokes the campaign stop after current-batch evidence delivery. Invalid matching, disturbed observation or incomplete state is an apparatus blockage rather than a scientific negative.
