# R63e: independent confirmation of four autonomous-activity criteria in 64 groups on 6.3

16 November 2026. All 64 independent ThreeMind origins satisfy all four criteria; none of the 64 origins in any control system jointly passes. The complete preregistered confirmation target is met. Every scientific summary value matches the corresponding 6.1 batch. No new adverse contradiction or performance stopping condition was triggered.

## Purpose, expectation and design

This batch asks whether, with the same anonymous teaching materials and circular environment, ThreeMind consistently exhibits experience dependence, selective internal causality, temporal interaction and feedback adaptation together, and exceeds four controls in their joint pass rate. The expectation was that ThreeMind would exhibit all four properties, while each control would lack at least one.

The 64 new origins use seeds 1096100–1096163, separate from the preceding eight-group pilot. Five systems are paired within each origin, yielding 320 system units. ThreeMind uses the current 6.3 defaults. The reactive controller, finite-state controller, local Hebbian network and fixed recurrent network with a personal readout retain their original architectures and learning procedures. Version comparisons use the same birth materials as the historical 6.1 confirmation. Results are paired by origin across versions, rather than pooled as 128 independent origins.

Each system undergoes two teaching histories with opposite pairings. Declared research copies then receive target blockade, matched blockade, sham intervention, restoration or interruption of internal communication. Four continuous closed-loop lives undergo different environmental sequences: actions change position, which changes subsequent sensory input. Identical input replays and reversed actions test the role of environmental feedback. The model, personal peripherals, learning state, random state, body and recent activity are saved together. Diagnostic copies do not overwrite the teaching or closed-loop lives.

The original confirmation rules are retained. Each criterion requires at least 48 of 64 origins to pass, and ThreeMind must also jointly pass in at least 48. No control may meet the batch threshold on all four criteria. ThreeMind's paired joint-pass advantage must be at least 10 percentage points over each control, with all four exact tests significant at the 0.05 family level after Holm correction. Measures, windows, connection selection and thresholds were frozen before outcomes were available.

ThreeMind exceeds each control's joint pass rate by 100 percentage points. Each exact two-sided P value is 1.0842×10⁻¹⁹; each Holm-adjusted value is 4.3368×10⁻¹⁹. The 95% intervals from 20,000 paired bootstrap samples are [100,100] percentage points because the paired difference equals one for every origin. All 9,097 scientific summary values and all categorical decisions are identical to those in the 6.1 confirmation.

## Analysis, scientific conclusion and significance for ThreeMind

Opposite teaching histories produce different responses to identical cues. Targeted internal interventions selectively change these differences. Successive inputs interact through internal processes, and action-dependent environmental feedback continues to reshape the same individual. These four forms of evidence hold jointly within each origin and recur across all 64 origins.

This batch confirms that 6.3 constructs autonomous activity organized by experience, supported by internal causal processes and continually changed by environmental interaction. Among the five prespecified implementations and courses, ThreeMind has a clearly higher joint pass rate than all four controls. Timely settlement of weak activation in 6.3 preserves the previously demonstrated joint capacity.

All registered targets in this batch have been answered. The campaign proceeds to bodily transfer, connection transfer, fixed readout and subsequent mechanism studies, each with its own tests.
