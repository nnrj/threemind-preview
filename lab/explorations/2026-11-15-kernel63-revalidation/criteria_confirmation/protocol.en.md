# R63e: independent 64-group confirmation of four autonomy criteria on 6.3

15 November 2026, preregistration. This batch may begin only after R63d's eight-group replication has passed and been delivered. It implements the original 64-group confirmation in revalidation plan v2. Pilot and apparatus material are excluded from its N. Historical M3 evidence, conclusions and statistical rules remain intact.

## Question, expectation and competing explanations

The study asks whether ThreeMind 6.3 consistently satisfies experience dependence, selective causality, temporal interaction and feedback adaptation together in the five declared implementations, anonymous tasks and continuous ring environment, and exceeds each comparator's joint pass rate. The expectation is that 6.3 meets the complete target and each comparator misses at least one criterion. Any comparator meeting all four will be reported as satisfying them; criteria and source inclusion will not be changed.

Opposite histories and blank controls test whether current input explains the response. Target, matched, sham and restoration conditions test selectivity. Four sequential-stimulus cells and communication blocking distinguish internal interaction from addition. Closed loops, identical-input replays and action reversal distinguish environmental feedback from replay. New sources test dependence on a small set of encoding materials.

## Material and whole systems

Birth seeds are 1096100–1096163. Each of 64 independent sources pairs a reactive controller, finite-state controller, local Hebbian network, fixed recurrent network with a personal RLS readout, and ThreeMind: 320 system units. The statistical unit is the paired source; copies, trials and frames are nested observations. Apparatus seed 1096002 is separate.

The verified R63d `rig.py` and `measurement.py` are reused unchanged, including teaching, models, bodies, readout and source-level criterion arithmetic. ThreeMind uses explicit current 6.3 defaults; the four comparators retain their implementations. All receive the same eight public channels and materials, with their declared learning methods and computational structures. Core uses a fixed A−B readout; the recurrent network learns its personal RLS readout as specified previously.

Each system has two continuously taught parents receiving 16 rounds of opposite pairing histories. Ordinary teaching is continuous; subsequent diagnostics and interventions use registered copies of complete parents. Target, matched, sham, restored and communication-blocked branches retain distinct identities. Selection uses teaching data only. Core sets contain two edges each with equal node degrees and absolute mean weight/activity residuals no larger than 0.02. RNN matching selects the original nonoverlapping 8+8 hidden coordinates with standardized residuals no larger than 0.5. Invalid matching is invalid measurement, not a scientific negative.

Four PPP/PQP/QQQ/QPQ lives each run continuously for 90 internal seconds in three 30-second stages. P and Q exchange position-dependent sensory input. Post-stage diagnostics use separate copies at a common body position and never alter the main lives. Stage-two identical-input replays, reversed-action replays and genuine closed-loop action reversals retain complete states. Models, personal peripheral weights and learning state, random state, interfaces, recent activity, body and actuator are preserved together.

## Frozen criteria and statistics

Source-level rules are unchanged from R63d and historical M3: history contrast exceeds 1e−4; target effect exceeds 1e−4 and ten times sham error, matched effect is at most 25% of target, and restoration error is at most 1e−9; the communication-dependent temporal interaction exceeds 1e−4; environmental reversal changes the common-body diagnostic by more than 1e−4, genuine closed-loop reversal changes inputs and model, and identical-input replay preserves the model. Exact windows, inputs and formulae are in the [R63d protocol](../criteria/protocol.en.md) and frozen tools.

The complete confirmation target requires all of the following:

1. Valid originals, matching and replays.
2. At least 48/64 ThreeMind sources on each criterion and at least 48/64 satisfying all four together.
3. No comparator reaching at least 48/64 on every criterion.
4. A paired joint-pass advantage of at least ten percentage points over each comparator, with all four exact two-sided McNemar/sign tests significant after Holm correction at family α=0.05.

Report every source, criterion and conjunction denominators, paired differences, 20,000-replicate paired-bootstrap 95% intervals and exact joint-proportion 95% intervals. The historical power design yielded 0.75575 simultaneous-rejection power at N48 and 0.97370 at N64, motivating the original N64. This calculation does not guarantee the full conjunction. No significance-dependent expansion or new confidence-bound pass condition is permitted.

A pass confirms stable formation of the four properties and discrimination within these implementations. A failure identifies the unmet expectation; invalid measurement is reported separately. If valid 6.3 results fall below 48/64 on any criterion, a comparator meets all four cohort thresholds, or another clear adverse contradiction of a verified mechanism appears, preserve and deliver the batch, then pause the campaign for the author's decision. Ordinary completion proceeds to the next eligible original-plan study.

## Apparatus, resources and delivery

Before formal execution, independent apparatus material checks identical whole endpoints under ordinary observation, frequent observation, save/restore and a host pause in all five systems, plus sham and restoration identity. Synthetic score tables test statistical handling of a positive result, comparator counterexample, negative result, threshold boundary and invalid measurement; no scientific individuals are created.

Scientific execution is serial. A once-per-second guard reserves 5/4/5/25 GiB of commit/RAM/C/E capacity and limits process private memory to 1 GiB; runtime is bounded at two hours. Safe pauses occur after a complete system unit, earlier than the old five-system-source boundary, without altering scientific criteria or saved content. Budgets are 4 GiB originals, 4 GiB archive, 4 GiB API readback and 128 MiB analysis files: 12.125 GiB total peak in addition to the 25 GiB reserve. The historical 6.1 pilot used approximately 165 MiB. This batch freezes its per-origin estimate from the new R63d measurements and checks the first and each subsequent complete source against it; exceeding it by 50% pauses execution for review. A new dead-host in-flight state or rollback is not handled automatically.

Expected output is 320 systems, 20,480 probes, 33,920 scientific whole states and 1,280 closed-loop replay groups. Read-only analysis loads one system at a time and independently verifies integrals, criteria, body feedback, intervention fields and statistics. Bilingual reports and originals must pass complete API readback with zero pending requests before closure. Production Core, historical experiments and manuscripts remain unchanged.

This reruns historical R51i on 6.3. Registration requires completed and verified R63d; no current R63d outcome is assumed. Birth materials, the four controls, teaching, measurements and statistics are retained. The new pilot determines the frozen per-origin storage estimate and is excluded from confirmation. Historical 6.1 results are read-only paired references; matching seeds across versions do not create 128 independent origins.
