# R63g: local connections transfer learned responses and actual choices

16 November 2026. All eight independent origins meet the preregistered transfer criteria. After target-connection transplantation, all 48 target choices agree with the donor and all 48 unrelated responses are preserved. Sham and matched transplantation do not produce the same target transfer. The persistent-state package passes; the fast-state package does not. All source-level scores match 6.1.

## Purpose, expectation and design

This batch tests whether local connections formed through experience transfer specific responses and actual gate choices while preserving unrelated responses. Persistent and fast connection states are compared to identify what supports transfer in this task. Target transplantation was expected to succeed, matched transplantation to lack the same target benefit, and immediate replacement with the original connections to restore the intact reference.

Eight origins and four prespecified teaching-intensity strata are retained. Each origin supplies two continuous teaching lives with opposite XYA/ZWB and XYB/ZWA pairings. Complete parents are preserved after 16 teaching rounds. Every individual uses the 6.3 defaults. Declared research copies enter seven conditions: intact, sham, complete target-connection package, matched package, persistent target package, fast target package and immediate restoration after transplantation.

The targets are two directed connections from XY to A/B; matched connections run from ZW to A/B. Selection uses interfaces declared before teaching. Connection counts, node degrees, same-time weights and teaching activity meet the registered matching rules. Transplantation changes only the selected connections and corresponding timers. Donors, parents, other connections, body, readout and random state are retained. Before transplantation, pending state updates are evaluated at the same internal time. Before/after states are saved, and apparatus checks establish equivalent subsequent behavior.

Each copy receives X, Y, XY and unrelated cues Z, W, ZW. A fixed A−B readout selects the positive gate, negative gate or no gate 110 ms after stimulation. At 250 ms, that choice changes subsequent sensory input; another action is recorded at 360 ms. The first choice remains the primary measure, with the full process recorded every millisecond. The 672 probes are nested within eight independent origins.

Intact target choices follow the recipient's original relation and therefore disagree with the oppositely taught donor. Matched transplantation changes the ZW relation while leaving XY choices unchanged; its 0/48 preservation count for unrelated responses records that selective change.

Every origin achieves 100% target-transfer success, a 100-percentage-point gain over sham, zero loss on unrelated responses and zero target gain from matched transplantation. All eight pass the original primary criteria. The source-level bootstrap mean target gain is 1, with a 95% interval of [1,1]. Persistent-state transplantation also passes in all eight origins.

All 16 fast-state exchanges leave the exchanged contents unchanged. They are zero-change controls and pass in 0/8 origins. For the complete and persistent packages, the only edge-content change in this batch is the destination: the other states of corresponding donor and recipient connections are identical. Transfer is therefore localized further to the reconnection of learned relations. Restoration and sham match the intact reference in complete state and every response. All 157 source-level scientific summary values and classifications are identical to 6.1.

## Scientific conclusion and significance for ThreeMind

In this anonymous-pairing task, local connectivity carries the specific response relations established through experience. Transferring these connections into a recipient with a different teaching history transfers the corresponding response and first gate choice, while retaining responses associated with connections left intact. Matched transplantation changes the other relation, and replacing the original connections restores the reference. These effects establish selectivity and reversibility.

Version 6.3 retains the previously demonstrated capacity for local experience transfer, with consequences for actual choice and subsequent sensation. Success of the persistent package and the zero-change fast-state comparison are reported separately. The primary target is met, with no new adverse contradiction. The planned 48-origin confirmation remains a separate batch.
