# R63b: Selective causal effects of experience-related connections replicate in 6.3

15 November 2026. Eight new independent origins use current 6.3 defaults. Each origin receives two opposite teaching histories, yielding 16 complete teaching references. Five research copies begin from each complete pre-cue state, for 80 copies. Copies do not increase the independent sample size.

## Purpose and expectations

The same cue can produce different responses after different teaching histories. This study asks whether relevant connections carry that difference. Blocking target connections was expected to markedly reduce it, while blocking other connections matched in number and internal measures should have little effect. Immediately removing the block without advancing time should recover the complete reference. The passive readout is the fixed activation difference between A and B; no readout is trained.

The eight origins, 16 teaching rounds and primary window 0.1–0.3 seconds after the cue are retained. Preregistered success requires positive experience effects and valid matching in every origin, at least 90% reduction under target blocking, at most 10% under matched blocking, and exact restoration of the complete reference state and recorded process.

## Results and conclusions

All 8/8 origins pass. Connection matching is valid in all 16 teaching references. Target blocking reduces the experience-dependent response difference by 100%; matched blocking reduces it by 0%. Sham intervention matches the intact reference, and immediate removal of the block restores the complete state and recorded process exactly.

| Comparison | Results across eight origins | Scientific conclusion |
|---|---|---|
| Same cue after opposite teaching | Positive experience effect in every origin | Teaching history determines the subsequent response difference |
| Target connection block | The difference becomes zero throughout | Relevant connections causally carry the response difference |
| Matched connection block | The difference remains throughout | The effect is selective for the relevant connections |
| Sham and immediate restoration | Identical to the intact reference | The masking procedure itself does not cause the effect, and removing the block restores expression |

Selective connection causality is therefore replicated in 6.3. Restoration here means removing a block at the same internal time; it does not measure relearning after a period of blocked activity. Complete primary-window, late-window and 0.5-second endpoint values remain in analysis.json.

## Integrity and cost

All 192 complete states and teaching-to-copy relationships passed verification. Interventions change only the declared connection masks. Independent Decimal arithmetic reproduces the primary results from all 80 trajectories. Time remains continuous within each teaching reference and copy; the copies do not constitute ordinary per-question resets.
