# E31 原位补试执行细则 / Prospective shared-world probes

2026-10-03，在执行补试前冻结。沿用已登记的可选补试及总预算；不参考补试结果改选区。

从 E30 两个意识世界 200 秒的原副本分别观察最多 60 秒。先收集 2 秒真实输入和活动。按个体编号升序，取每种任务第一个满足：表面间隔大于接触皮层、小于该个体感官范围，且相对运动正在接近的对象。营养取最近颗粒；邻体强弱按既有 `thrust × (0.25 + grip) × radius²` 区分，仅分析使用。较强邻体主动靠近焦点也会触发逃离观察，不要求焦点先表现出逃离。没有达到条件就记为未触发。

选区使用过去 2 秒的活动，排除最近 1 秒直接输入通道；这能定位“未被近期直接输入的活动相关通路”，不能仅凭此把所有活动都称为联想，因为残余激活也可能存在。选择源快照已有连接，最多 128 条及等量权重/稳定度匹配连接。保存整个海洋，再分别原位、目标静默、匹配静默各观察 20 秒。能量、身体、物体位置、繁殖/变异及其他意识完整保留。事件选择不看后续成败，吞食/摄食计数均减去源快照已有计数。活动/影像 100 ms，输入/动作 20 ms。

English: Continue copies of both original 200-second E30 worlds for at most 60 seconds. After two seconds of input/activity history, select the first ID-ordered prospective food, weaker-neighbor and stronger-neighbor encounter whose surface gap lies between contact skin and sensory range and whose relative separation is decreasing. Food is the nearest particle; neighbor strength uses the existing physical holding formula only in observation. A stronger neighbor approaching the focal individual qualifies, without requiring prior escape. Untriggered events stay untriggered. Select up to 128 pre-existing incoming edges from activity not directly stimulated in the last second, with equal-count weight/stability matched controls. Residual activation is a possible contributor; the selection alone does not identify associative propagation. Preserve the entire world and compare intact/target/matched copies for 20 seconds. Subtract pre-existing intake/capture totals. All physical feedback, reserves, reproduction, mutation and other agents remain. This is the preregistered optional follow-up within the unchanged total budget.
