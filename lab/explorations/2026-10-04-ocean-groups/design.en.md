## E35: compare the same population

Copy each of the two complete E32 Core-world snapshots at60s into three60s continuations: all normal Core control; all random control with sensory processing/Core energy costs retained; all random control with Core injection,supply and advancement stopped. Six conditions share actual bodies,ages,positions,velocities,traits,current reserves,neighbors,nutrients,environment time and random state. This is not a comparison between independently chosen organisms. Descendants inherit the experimental controller; bilateral juvenile kin protection applies.

Both random variants use identical commands for a common identity/time. Core retains its unmodified20ms cadence and amplitude. Primary outcomes concern the whole group; E34 separately examines focal mechanisms.

Stopped Cores are frozen recoverable artifacts. Their stored energy is neither erased nor transferred to bodies. Descendants still pay the original construction/body/reserve costs and retain frozen inherited artifacts without neural execution. This is disabling an existing neural component, not inventing a cheaper brainless species. Saved operating energy is reported without an artificial balancing charge.

**Death boundary:** the current apparatus mainly removes starving bodies through Core energy/activity death; bodily unmet maintenance is recorded but has no separate irreversible body-starvation equation. A stopped Core cannot use that neural death rule. Report physical presence,powered bodies,unmet maintenance,capture and births separately. An unfunded body left physically present is not an effective survival advantage. Longer brainless ecology would need an explicit body-failure rule; this experiment does not silently add one.
