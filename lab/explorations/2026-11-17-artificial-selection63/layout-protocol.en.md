# AS63-02: Layout change and current sensory feedback

## Question and design

AS63-01 tests artificial selection, retention across generations, and accumulation in a fixed environment. AS63-02 asks whether, after the two regions exchange positions, current sensory feedback helps an individual retain its selected regional tendency compared with replaying its old actions. Region identity follows its sensory pattern rather than a fixed physical semicircle.

Use every G2 candidate from all three selection conditions in all 16 populations: 192 candidates. Do not select high-scoring champions. Create complete-state research copies at positions 0.1875 and 0.6875 with velocity zero, matching the main held-0 and held-2 tests in Core state, readout history, input queue, energy supply, and starting position.

At each position add two 60-internal-second conditions:

1. Exchange the regions and let current sensory input and internal activity continue controlling movement.
2. Exchange the regions and replay, at each 0.1-second interval, that candidate's saved actions from the original layout at the same starting position. The Core continues receiving actual current sensory input, background stimulation, and energy. Its proposed actions are recorded but do not move the body.

Reuse the original-layout reference without rerunning it. Each added condition contains 384 stages, for 768 additional stages. These are related research copies; the independent sample size remains 16 populations. No additional reproduction takes place and research-copy experiences are not written back to candidates.

The only interventions are the declared exchange of sensory patterns and, in replay copies, replacement of applied motor commands. The production Core and birth rules remain unchanged. Check complete-state copying, original-layout closed-loop/replay equivalence, and swapped sensory channels using separate apparatus material before registration. Preserve complete initial/final states, inputs, proposed/applied actions, body trajectories, and occupancy.

## Criteria

Average the two starting positions for each candidate, then all four candidates per population and selection condition. Compare target occupancy under swapped-layout closed-loop control with swapped-layout action replay, once per selection direction. Each direction requires mean improvement >=0.01 and a one-sided exact sign-test P<=0.05 after Holm correction across these two supplementary tests. Both directions must pass for the joint result. Differences with absolute magnitude <=1e-12 are ties. Report signs, ties, all population differences, and 95% percentile intervals from 20,000 population bootstrap samples (seed1117178). This family is separate from AS63-01's eight registered primary tests.

Describe original-layout versus swapped-layout closed-loop scores and random-selection results separately. Do not call this goal-reversal learning or interpret remaining at a fixed location as adaptation. Negative supplementary results do not change the main artificial-selection scores.
