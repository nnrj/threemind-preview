# R46 six-condition decay comparison: design awaiting preregistration

2026-11-09. This draft implements the author's authorized three decay laws and
relaxation switch. R45 delivery, /5.2 verification and separate apparatus
calibration precede freezing the materials, thresholds, source and budget.
Neither this design nor engineering tests are completed scientific experiments.

The first layer asks how long connections formed by different experiences
persist and whether a cue still evokes the learned response. The second asks
whether this retention improves correct answers while allowing weak experiences
to fade and obsolete rules to be updated. Each has its own conclusion.

All six conditions use genuine /5.2: exponential, inverse-logarithmic and
power-law decay, each with relaxation on/off. Exponential plus original
relaxation is also checked against the /5.1 trajectory. Equations, the immutable
birth-time curve selection, contribution ages and relaxation are specified in
`design-notes.zh.md`. No curve or exponent is selected after formal results.
Adopting a new default is a separate decision.

The first layer uses new anonymous inputs and declared output ports, without
loading old R43–R45 subjects. Conditions include a weak single exposure, ordinary
single exposure, strong single exposure, massed repetition and expanding spaced
repetition. Repetition schedules match event count, delivered dose, course end
and final retention interval. Actual accepted activity is recorded separately
from input dose because saturation may differ. Novel/familiar conditions are
crossed with strength: a stimulus can be both novel and weak, so criteria must
not demand contradictory outcomes from that condition.

Measurements preserve node identities, surviving connections, current weights,
decay timescales, full activity trajectories and the first cued response.
Opposite histories receive the same current cue. The entire observation window
contains activation, inhibition, direct activation and associative activation;
a brightest-node snapshot cannot replace it. Different retention delays use
separate continuously developed sequences so an earlier probe cannot rehearse
the later probe. Any necessary causal copies receive declared parentage. Ordinary
questions never restore an earlier checkpoint.

A fixed output projection, if used in the first layer, is a local mechanism
instrument. It does not replace the second layer's personal adapters or count
as joint-task success. Independent apparatus materials must first establish the
response direction, temporal information and history sensitivity. Invalid
measurement requires an instrument repair rather than a negative Core claim.

The second layer binds each new complete /5.2 individual to its own trainable
temporal Transformer output adapters. It retains actual question sampling,
20 raw frames over one second, eight fixed time positions, four activity fields,
and personal weights/AdamW/RNG. Teaching includes expanding review intervals;
errors are recorded before ordinary demonstration, related-item correction and
review of old correct examples. Rounds, lessons, updates and stopping rules are
fixed before execution.

R43's sound/vision/action joint task remains an explicit, separate endpoint.
A smaller apparatus task cannot substitute for repairing joint judgment.
Each curve's adapter development must cover its actual internal trajectories;
a separately registered frozen-adapter comparison locates the Core contribution.
After learning an initial rule, individuals learn its reversal and later return
to the old rule. Outcomes include actual experiences required, old interference,
new-rule accuracy and recovery. Final unseen materials never enter training or
model selection. Longer retention with slower adaptation is reported as a cost.

Independent apparatus data determine measurable delays and feasible costs.
Numerical rules for the following targets are frozen before research begins:
retention and expression after repetition; novelty/strength effects and fatigue;
weak-transient forgetting; eventual fading without review; reversal and return;
and wall time, memory, connection/contribution counts and evidence size. Feasible
long delays use actual complete trajectories. Predictions beyond that range
are explicitly conditional calculations from a saved state, never simulated
development that did not occur.

Exploration starts with eight independent birth origins. Histories, conditions,
copies and questions remain nested observations. New-origin confirmation is
registered according to mechanism value, precision and cost, without adding
samples based on significance. Commit/RAM/C/E reserves remain 5/4/5/25 GiB.
Observation, saving, logs and host delays cannot advance internal time. New
incomplete states are preserved rather than silently rolled back. The tiny
engineering cost probe cannot set the full Transformer budget; one complete
unit must be measured first.
