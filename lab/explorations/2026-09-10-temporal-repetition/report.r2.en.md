# Temporal readout replication: repeated complete sentences and unseen families

> **Revision r2 · 2026-10-10**
> Revision reason: Verified original-file relocation; append location-resolution guidance and preserve the experiment conclusions.
> Original: [report.en.md](report.en.md); parent r1: [report.en.md](report.en.md)


The registered curriculum preserved all speakers, punctuation and the default sensory mapping (NFKC; anonymous-distributed; 192 channels). Each new sentence was presented **20 consecutive times before switching**, over three complete rounds. Four independent encoding seeds were planned; 4 completed result files are included. We stored and audited 7400 training deliveries across 10 source/branch consciousnesses.

The primary evidence is each actual temporal window and its complete recorded nodes sorted by final activation. Ties are retained (1e-12 tolerance); node IDs never break scientific ties. Existing composite ranking, peaks, integrals and fixed-window probes are secondary. A converged window is not synonymous with a correct answer. Non-converged and unmeasurable cases are retained.

There were 360 temporal probe copies, including 180 positive-dose copies and equally sized zero-dose controls. Recorded classifications: {'residual_decay': 124, 'transient': 144, 'converged': 92}. Among the 56 converged positive-dose windows that ask for a specific answer, prespecified exclusive-channel outcomes were {'tie': 52, 'unmeasurable': 4}. These are descriptive counts within 4 seeds, not independent statistical replicates.

The full Chinese report contains every pair and unseen-grandfather test in readable tables. analysis.json retains all questions, controls, classification/coverage data and word-channel projections; node-rankings.jsonl retains complete raw-node strength rankings plus the original composite ordering and time series. Missing sensory-template channels are not silently zero-filled. Word projections are an external measurement, not generated speech or proof of semantics.

Important distinction: the existing reader classifies changes above a pre-input baseline; residual_decay/transient do not mean that internal activation has disappeared. In the seed41701 final grandfather example, 169/169 nodes remained active, with ending activation from 0.744045075768 to 0.744045075768. Identical ending strengths cannot rank one answer ahead of another.

The predeclared follow-up observed four independent full-state copies for 12 simulated seconds at 20 ms intervals while preserving the original 100 ms temporal-reader output. See diagnostic-plan.md, diagnostic-summary.json and the complete diagnostic evidence for measurements after the original window ended and within a propagation period. It does not relabel the original windows.

The study made no Core, sensory, or reader-algorithm changes. Full-state probes inherited activation, queues, baseline history and ongoing plasticity; they never wrote back to training sources. Training/probing took 23.45 minutes; peak training process RSS was 276.7MiB. Prepublication DB growth was 340.1MiB. The study stayed within its 4 GiB storage budget, 30-minute computation limit and the monitored account-allowance stop rule. Account percentage readings are shared/lagged, not exact token accounting.

The findings are limited to these defaults, carriers, seeds and measurement definitions. They neither establish relational reasoning nor demonstrate that AGI is impossible. Future work should examine local activity differentiation, connection-weight dynamics and observation cadence with controlled parameter changes while preserving sufficient contiguous repetitions.
