## Purpose and original expectation

The study asked whether complete sentences, the default sensory mapping and twenty consecutive presentations per sentence would let the actual temporal reader distinguish correct pairing and unseen-family answers. The expected evidence was a correct-target lead within the existing converged window that followed swapped teaching. Activity or convergence alone was not success. The Chinese report already stated the negative outcome; the shorter English edition emphasized counts and boundaries. This revision makes the scientific answer explicit in both languages.

## Results and scientific judgment

**This curriculum–reader combination did not produce distinguishable pairing answers or unseen-family inference.** Of 360 probe copies, 180 received positive doses. Among the 56 converged positive-dose windows asking for a specific answer, 52 tied and four lacked exclusive-channel coverage. The four unmeasurable cases are not incorrect answers, and 56 converged windows are not 56 successes. The independent level is four encoding sources, not copies or questions.

This is unfavorable to the curriculum expectation that adequate complete-sentence repetition and the current endpoint reader suffice for selective answers. Activity continued and some target endpoints had equal strength; the observed failure concerns answer differentiation rather than cessation of activity. The twelve-second diagnostic did not rescore the original windows or substitute an earlier transient for the primary endpoint.

**For ThreeMind, the evidence favors resolving activity differentiation and measurement conditions before scaling this language procedure.** More of the same teaching is not justified by these results. This recommendation follows the recorded negative result; it changes no Core mechanism and does not classify structural memory, other readout windows or all language capability as failures.

## Revised conclusion

Selective pairing answers and unseen-family inference were not obtained. Persistent activity and convergence do not substitute for answer differentiation. The curriculum expectation failed; activity differentiation and measurement conditions are the next concrete targets.
