## Subjects and fixed conditions

Every probe begins with a new, empty kernel. No learned peripherals, semantic labels, optimizer, body or inherited mappings are involved. Old experiment files, subjects and running services are untouched. Use the isolated native build and record both kernel identity and effective configuration in final checkpoints.

1. **Exposure/retention:** compare preserved memory/2 and new memory/3. Two channels receive amplitude 0.8 once, once at intensity 4, or every 250 ms for 120 seconds. Disable propagation and inhibition explicitly to separate passive retention from recurrent learning; extend quiescence grace to 10,000 seconds. Observe 0.1, 30, 300 and 1,800 seconds after the final input. Report actual weights, half-lives, support and fatigue, not answer accuracy.
2. **Background/reclamation:** fixed 32-channel and expanding-channel schedules, 120 seconds, paired input every two seconds; then 300 seconds without input. Use max_nodes=128 and max_edges=512. Compare memory/2, default automatic memory/3, manual memory/3, optional dormancy, and optional dormancy plus revival. Both memory/3 research switches remain off as creation defaults regardless of these probes. Report live nodes/edges, dormant traces, revivals, capacity rejections and measured wall time. This bounded check is not a production-scale or indefinitely long background benchmark.
3. **Changed pairing:** channels 1+2 receive input every 250 ms for 20 seconds, then 1+3 for 20 seconds. Inspect old/new pair weights immediately and after 300 seconds. Use the same no-propagation/no-inhibition instrument settings as (1). This is a structural adaptation check, not behavioral reversal learning.
