# Explicit intensity consolidation and history dependent sensitivity

Prospective registration, 2026-09-30. Baseline Git `2d23297`, `threemind.continuous-memory/2`. No changes to Core, mapping, thresholds, clocks, death or historical studies. Public Runtime/Lab interfaces create independent copies and apply existing research controls.

## Questions and factorial controls

Can strong input produce more persistent cue-accessible associations without the separate intensity bonus? Does the same cue elicit different responses after different experiences? The study does not require a positive substitution result.

Eight I×F×L conditions: intensity_gain 0/0.075; facilitation time constant 0/1 second; positive existing-edge consolidation disabled/enabled. The four I×F cells implement the proposed artificial-rule versus candidate-mechanism comparison; L adds attribution. L-off retains edge birth, node history and forgetting. Ordinary first/repeat consolidation, local depletion/recovery, inhibition, novelty, fast trace, local competition and forgetting retain defaults except separately registered single-factor ablations.

## Materials

A/B/C/D are anonymous channels with external observer labels. Discovery blocks 930401/930402 and validation blocks 930411/930412 vary prior training intervals (0.5/1 seconds) and amplitudes (0.3/0.4). Encoding seeds are not independent worlds.

Each block trains AC and BD 12 consecutive presentations each, then waits five seconds. All factorial branches derive from this identical ordinary-default parent and explicitly reconfigure. AB learning is none, one input of intensity 1, one of 4, or ten inputs of 0.4 at 0.5-second intervals. Final teaching times match. Single 4 and ten 0.4 match nominal quantity, not temporal distribution.

At 1, 30 and 300 seconds after the last scheduled input, fork A-cue intensity 0.5 and uncued controls for five-second observation. A separate maintenance channel receives 0.2 every 0.5 seconds, never grouped with task input; this is disclosed synthetic maintenance, not hardware telemetry. No-cue creates no task envelope. Planned total: four parents, 128 training branches, 768 observation branches, 900 instances.

## Measurements

Retain original steady-state classification/decision-time activation ranks and full V2 processes side by side. Mechanistic endpoints are cue-minus-no-cue integrated B activation, B versus the old associate C, and retained AB weight/stability. Directly stimulated A is not an associative answer. Integrate the fixed five seconds, retaining all nodes. Process activity is not re-labelled as steady convergence.

Save every input/delivery, immediate post-teaching state, pre-probe state, complete trace and model hash. Compare identical probes across histories, preserving validation blocks and all negative conditions. Replacement requires repeatable target-specific retention with I=0 beyond ordinary-input and uncued responses. Report the fraction retained versus I=1 under matched nonzero conditions; 80% is a preregistered engineering approximation, not a biological standard. Two zero effects do not establish equivalence. A task-specific substitution does not delete historical formulas.

## Bounded extensions

Check equal incident input represented as (1,1) versus (0.25,4): I=0 should restore identical Core state. If strong-input advantage persists without I, separately ablate ordinary first/repeat consolidation, existing-edge consolidation, fast trace, context and novelty. Do not call a basic amplitude-dependent formula a facilitation effect. Register the exact extension before running.

Compare facilitation 0.1/0.3/1/3 seconds; assess specificity and uncued activity, not brightness alone. Examine the same learned state under varied cue strengths, recent repetition and recovery intervals. Where useful, record inhibition/resource/utilization histories and use selective silencing, restoration and matched controls without inventing learned state. At most 1148 additional instances, 2048 total.
