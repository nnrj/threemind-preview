## First-study results and next steps

Thirty-four stages, 206 instance creations/restores and 126.14 compute minutes. Nose/no-nose each received 52 lessons with per-skill reviews; 24 corrective demonstrations were also performed. A new motor trainer's empty-batch bug was fixed; preserve its failures rather than attributing them to Core.

With the reader fixed, silencing existing connections changes every eye/hand endpoint across 12 questions; restoring them reproduces all commands. Internal connections participate, but correctness benefits are mixed. Correction improves different measures inconsistently, and look-only tasks remain unreliable. Normal eye-task aligned-slow time is 8.47%; zero commands reach 25% only through already aligned starts. The matched nose prototype comparison shows no benefit. Core/defaults are unchanged.

Recommended next steps separate target reading from physical servo execution, reuse earlier shared spatial reading where appropriate, and interleave reviews after initial concentrated practice. These were not added to this batch. Shape/deformation/plural/negative tasks await basic control. Reward/teaching combinations are recorded separately as [R37](training-regimes.md), not executed here.
