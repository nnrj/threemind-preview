## 2026-10-06: correction and execution decision

E54 had position, velocity and contact input, but lacked continuously changing environmental background. Its ten repetitions plus two revisits did not reproduce E43's expanding per-item review direction. Plasticity and an otherwise visible environment do not remove these omissions.

E55 starts blank instances with simulated wind, temperature, humidity, thermal body state, gravity support and inertial acceleration, through external senses and the input bus. Old text mapping, Core, world/1 and historical data remain intact. Simulation is the user's permitted substitute for CPU telemetry; no real CPU sampling is claimed. Every concept receives twelve initial lessons followed by review phases at12,24,48,96 seconds, with actual individual intervals exported. Same-scene pointer/question changes are primary, avoiding majority-attribute shortcuts.

Compare learned, plasticity-disabled and equal-dose uniform-review conditions; all retain bodily background. Whether this supports self/world differentiation remains a hypothesis. This study localizes the reference bottleneck without presupposing success.
