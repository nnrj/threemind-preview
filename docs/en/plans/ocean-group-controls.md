# Whole-population copies and competition in one ocean

Approved2026-10-04;[E35–E36 registered](../../../lab/explorations/2026-10-04-ocean-groups/design.en.md) to follow E34. The primary comparison is the **same whole group copied**,not differently selected individuals. Focal experiments remain supplementary mechanism tests.

- E35: two actual complete-world60s snapshots,each continued60s with normal Core control,random control retaining Core costs,and random control stopping Core. Descendants inherit controller assignment.
- E36: doubled area,53 bodies per group and conserved initial/replenishment densities. Both cohorts share the ocean and can consume only the other cohort. Initial body/brain twins and swapped controller-position assignments across two new seeds;120s observation.
- Cohort identity is external apparatus,never a sensory enemy/friend label. Physical feedback,finite energy,mutation,shells andbilateral kin protection remain. No fusion,cooperative policy or restriction on Core action cadence.
- A stopped Core does not donate its reserve to the body. Bodies currently lack an independent irreversible starvation-death equation,so physical presence,powered bodies andunmet maintenance are reported separately. Absence of neural death after stopping Core is not a longevity advantage.

See[registration](../../../lab/explorations/2026-10-04-ocean-groups/design.en.md) for criteria,budgets anddecision provenance. The new external`threemind.ocean-groups/1`protocol/state preserves cohorts,controllers andpredation permissions. Old Ocean/3 andCore behavior remain. Registration does not preannounce results.
