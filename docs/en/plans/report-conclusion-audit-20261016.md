# Historical report conclusion audit and revisions

16 October 2026. At the user's request, this audit reviews conclusion reporting without rerunning experiments. Scope: 111 groups of reports, fragments, supplements and early notebooks, comprising 416 pre-existing language/version files. The purpose, results and interpretation in each current effective version were read, with Chinese/English comparison. Historical editions remain evidence, not independent experiments. The research API additionally inventoried active and archived reports in all 587 accessible projects: 174 documents, comprising 157 scientific reports and 17 explicitly named engineering-acceptance fixtures. Fixtures are not scientific-discovery reports and receive no invented conclusions.

**Sixteen groups receive 32 Chinese/English revised editions: eleven current-batch groups and five historical groups. The remaining 95 groups are preserved with individual reasons.** Decisions concern whether the report answers its question and interprets its findings, not whether a conclusion heading exists. Scope statements alone are not defects. Explicit negatives, invalid measurements, termination and withdrawn interpretations remain valid conclusions.

Pre-existing report files are checked byte-for-byte before and after this work; new editions are appended through the existing ReportRevisions registry. Runtime source, raw trajectories, scores and criteria remain unchanged. This audits scientific interpretation and publication completeness, not experimental reruns or external replication. Platform commentary is compared paragraph-by-paragraph with repository sources. Extra passages are evidence indexes/link annotations; E67 termination and the candidate-mechanism interpretation are included separately. The timing platform report contained only status and a data reference, so it receives a complete narrative edition.
