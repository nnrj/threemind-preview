# Depletion, facilitation and consolidation experiments

[中文](../../zh/plans/memory-timescales.md)

2026-09-29: **Independent research controls and 598 whole-network instances completed.** Existing kernels and evidence are preserved. All eight combinations, exact reproduction, retention and bounded mechanistic follow-ups were executed.

Cross D/F/L (depletion, facilitation, positive consolidation of existing edges) in all eight combinations. First compare transient responses from shared learned networks, then retention at 1/30/300 simulated seconds after repeated training. Preserve forgetting, inhibition, fast traces and other configuration values.

Limits: 2 GiB new storage, 650 instances and a soft stop at three additional account percentage points. Consolidation retained associations recalled after 300 simulated seconds; facilitation increased responses; unlimited supply plus facilitation and consolidation supported persistent activity. Qualifying converged temporal output remained absent; primary and secondary outcomes are recorded separately. The preregistered design remains unchanged as a historical document.
