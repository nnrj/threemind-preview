# Environmental selection, energy feedback and teaching: future training memo

## Proposed division of work

| Route | Intended capability | Candidate approach |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Environmental selection | Inherited tendencies and basic survival behavior | Extend plankton-style energy/body constraints, survival and reproduction; preserve inherited structure as an initial instance |
| Energy supplied after successful behavior | Conditioned responses and particular actions | Limited supply with task-dependent replenishment; actions and consequences shape experience |
| Demonstration, explanation, correction and review | Goal understanding, expression of intention, relationships and reasoning | Teachers demonstrate across contexts; students try and receive correction, then face unseen relationships |

“Inherited,” “conditioned” and “reasoning” are initially the user's research categories, not established one-to-one correspondences between methods and capacities. Teaching may produce fixed responses, and energy feedback may support flexible choices. Reversal, transfer, delay, ablation and rescue should determine what was learned.

Turning, braking and holding involve both basic movement and following teacher cues; their difficulties need not all require advanced reasoning. Future combinations of teaching and feedback should have separate controls. Do not add rewards after a failed batch and present the result as evidence for the original teaching method.

## Boundaries and suggested controls

- The environment/teacher holds success criteria. Supply actual energy through a versioned interface; do not inject correct directions, reward labels or answers into Core, or silently change learning rates. Specify the energy mechanism and retain its accounting.
- Keep teaching, background, proprioception, vision and available actions comparable. Do not interpret starvation before a learning opportunity as another group's superior intelligence.
- Suggested groups are teaching, energy feedback, their combination, and no task-related feedback. Add equal-total but action-independent supply to separate task consequences from simply surviving longer or remaining more active. Register courses, budgets and stopping rules separately.
- Report trained skill, unseen layout/object transfer, adaptation after rule changes, persistence without immediate rewards, and Core-history/connection contributions with the reader fixed.
- Preserve inherited and individually trained instances separately. Old instances, experiments and established energy/forgetting rules retain their meanings.

This memo organizes future experiments without equating rewards with conditioning or teaching with demonstrated reasoning.
