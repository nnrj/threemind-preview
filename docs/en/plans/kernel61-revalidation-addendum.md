# Continuing comprehensive revalidation plan v2 on 6.1

13 November 2026. The author explicitly authorized continuing the previously approved ThreeMind 6.0 comprehensive revalidation plan v2 on 6.1 after the build and optimization checks pass. This addendum records the decision without rewriting [plan v2](kernel60-revalidation-v2.md) or historical results.

Advancement takes 0.8073 and 0.8234 times the 5.1 reference at the two tested sizes; allocated artifacts are approximately 1.10 times as large. Both pass the campaign's twofold stopping threshold. After backend and historical checks, new subjects explicitly select `threemind.continuous-memory/6.1`: inverse-logarithmic decay, deletion threshold 0.008, local deletion, and activation/inhibition sender thresholds 0.004/0.002. Birth rule 2 inherits decay age by default.

Origin counts, controls, continuous teaching, review and correction, denominators, evidence preservation, resource reserves and stopping conditions remain those of v2. Old 6.0 engineering batches, STOP markers, E67/E68/E69 and earlier scientific batches are not resumed. New batches register their own version and parent states; historical results retain their actual identity.

Execution begins with stimulus units, grouped input and connection formation, followed by embodied process readout, local resources, event order and major mechanism confirmations, then visual, ecological and other approved tasks. Each batch first verifies historical scale and freezes its scientific targets, materials, measurements and budget. Normal completion leads to the next eligible batch. Adverse mechanism contradictions, severe performance regression, insufficient resources or incomplete state trigger preservation and reporting.

Decay curves differ from historical versions, so exact retention times need not match. Protocols separately measure residual structure, elicited response and deletion, distinguishing expected longer retention from a mechanism failure in advance. Further Core changes require authorization. Manuscripts remain untouched until the approved experiments and figures are delivered; the original deferral and r3 rules still apply.
