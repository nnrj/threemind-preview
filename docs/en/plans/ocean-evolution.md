Do not tune sparsity until Core wins. Freeze conditions before comparison and retain random superiority, equality or extinction. Independent worlds are replicates;31related/interacting individuals are not31independent trials.

- Positive mechanical work plus declared actuator losses consumes body stores through an explicit energy conversion. Insufficient fuel reduces delivered force; passive flow/inertia remain possible.
- Volume, shell and pigmentation affect maintenance. Offspring Core reserve, body stores and material construction are funded by the parent. Retain an unaffordable prospective offspring's traits rather than rerolling until a cheaper body appears.
- Heritable variation includes radius, thrust, sensory range, shell, reflectance, material compatibility and a genuinely neutral marker. Variations may help, do nothing or harm depending on consequences. Neutral diversity is not species diversity.
- Shell adds mass, drag, maintenance/construction cost and resistance to engulfing. This is a declared material approximation, not real fracture or biochemical digestion.
- Four local reflectance measurements and enclosure strain join chemical/proprioceptive signals on anonymous channels. No enemy, kin or escape labels enter Core. Lower reflectance reduces received signals; beneficial camouflage remains an empirical question.
- A parent cannot engulf its offspring before60simulated seconds. This is an explicit user-requested environmental protection, not emergent parenting.
- Separate contact-at-birth recombination and engulfing-mediated retention of body-trait sources. Brain structure comes from the actual reproducing parent; retained material changes future offspring, never a living body for free.
- The user approved lossy contact exchange first, with active cooperation judged separately. Compatible surfaces exchange stores along a local difference with10% loss. Compatibility is a mutable material property, not an observer-assigned species label. No partner-seeking policy is programmed.

The planar inertial/linear-drag environment is not quantitatively calibrated seawater or early Earth. Conversion factors, shell resistance and protection rules are testable environmental choices, not biological constants. Motivations include [foraging costs](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1512307113), [body-shape tradeoffs](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6642390/) and [sharing that need not help](https://arxiv.org/abs/1412.7187); none validates this apparatus's particular coefficients.

## Beyond a finite family of bodies

The current parameter family cannot invent arbitrary new organs. A future independently designed material/part graph could inherit local binding, force, diffusion, energy conversion and replication costs; mutations change composition, placement, links and reaction parameters. Validate conservation/heredity/basic survival before allowing greater complexity. Do not label a predefined eye/pain/escape module spontaneous organ emergence. Acceleration executes the same simulated events faster when hardware allows; time alone guarantees neither organs nor intelligence.
