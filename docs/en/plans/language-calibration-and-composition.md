# Language calibration and unseen composition

[中文](../../zh/plans/language-calibration-and-composition.md) · [Backlog](research-backlog.md) · [E45 registration](../../../lab/explorations/2026-10-05-language-calibration/design.en.md)

2026-10-05. Approved work, hypotheses and progression gates remain distinct.

E44 established learned word-to-visual effects and localized some causal paths. It did not establish reliable object–new-name recall or associative language expression. Continue testing associations and composition while calibrating precision; cross-sensory recruitment is not automatically mastery of every concept.

1. Match language amount, visual exposure and course duration across sentence, repeated-sentence, dense/sparse character and simultaneous variants. Preserve older mappings under their identities. Calibrate visual observation against actual sensory responses; retain brief windows alongside whole-process summaries.
2. If peripheral calibration is insufficient, compare a bounded existing-parameter set for decay, inhibition, facilitation, resource recovery, emission and event learning. Keep forgetting, depletion, inhibition, death and propagation timing. Select on calibration material, validate with held-out seeds/names; propose task-specific profiles without changing historical instances or claiming a universal optimum.
3. Start unseen composition with observable colour/shape combinations and viewpoint changes. Deictic and personal terms need physical positions, identities, speaker changes and joint attention. Kinship needs cross-event relational teaching and is not the first gate. Do not bury an unresolved basic association under a large grammar course.
4. Preserve continuous no-new-teaching activity with background, actual transmission, visual effects and raw words. Use matched no-cue and causal interventions. Instrument interpretations are not direct access to subjective experience.

The user's hypotheses include excessive activation/association, parameter-dependent strengths and the role of bodily background. These compete with encoding, exposure and expression explanations. E44 weak-signal expression is an instrument improvement, not a Core default change. Disabling death for background-free long training or adding plastic inhibitory connections remains deferred discussion; existing mechanisms remain enabled. Check Docker only when a subsequent operation actually uses it.

### Later the same day: meaning, expression and heterogeneous internal patterns

The following are English translations of the two later user messages dated 2026-10-05. The [Chinese page](../../zh/plans/language-calibration-and-composition.md) preserves the original wording. These messages propose research interpretations; they are not recorded experimental facts.

> The current behavior may resemble what we expect of human brains: words may evoke vision more readily than vision evokes language, though this may be my forced explanation. We need not demand perfect matching, because human correspondences are also fuzzy. Both groups had word-to-vision 3/24 and vision-to-name 0/12; perhaps some indirect, deeper fixed relation formed that our instruments cannot decode. Thought is primary and language is a lossy expression. If calibration brings no further pairing progress, consider peripherals plus Core as a black box: can teaching produce expected responses to taught, similar and reasoning inputs? Preserve internal observations for tracing mechanisms. Continue the current plan and consider this at a suitable stage. Cross-sensory association need not be identical every time; a sufficiently frequent correspondence can matter.

> An association evoked by a stimulus such as a word may be an object, a feeling, a process, a pattern, a series of intentions or multiple objects. If a word consistently evokes similar local activation or a process, including inhibition, that can be a successful mapping even without a literal external counterpart. People need not understand how another brain internally handles a concept to communicate about the same thing. This fits ThreeMind's founding hypothesis: minds may be internally heterogeneous yet communicate through a common-world correspondence, including ThreeMind individuals, you and me.

Adopted interpretation and boundaries:

- A repeatable, discriminable stimulus-to-native-process relation can operationally define an internal mapping without resembling a physical sensory template. Expression adapters may learn individual representations.
- Record cross-sensory influence, repeatable native patterns and usable shared-reference communication separately. Neither of the latter requires 100%, but distinctions between inputs and changes of wording, experience and context matter. Identical state/input replay alone does not demonstrate acquired learning.
- Feelings, inhibitory processes, transmission rhythms and multi-object patterns remain allowed candidates. The E45 supplement measures activation in declared sensory regions only; it neither rules out nor presumes unmeasured codes.
- Identical totals require item/time/pattern checks. The two 3/24 totals later proved to come from different successes; equality did not confirm the proposed shared deeper rule.
- A future black-box communication task can retain internal evidence and use new assignments, reversal, learning-off, bypass/mismatched-Core and local silence controls. This recommendation does not relabel previous task failures as successes.

Original exact-retrieval/composition criteria remain unchanged.

## 2026-10-05: E45 completed; separate pathways, patterns and expression

E45 completed 2041 sources/branches and recorded 67.91 minutes of simulation, training and decoding. Equal-dose routes and six parameter alternatives did not beat sparse character input with defaults; Core/defaults were unchanged. Visual cues influenced name channels in 24/36 normal and 24/36 reversed cases, versus 0/36 learning-off. Six target-edge interventions removed at least 99.9813%; matched-other-edge silence retained 98.995%–165.205%. These learned reciprocal effects must not be concealed by correct-name retrieval of 0/36. Original object retrieval was 10/72 and two-step name-to-sound 0/48; the first link also lacked reliable retrieval after second teaching, so a composition-specific gap is not isolated. Fifteen 300-second histories contained no registered late sensory replay candidate.

The user emphasized feelings, processes, multiple intentions and inhibition as possible native representations; communication need not reproduce an external visual template. A post-result registered rereading found name/name-plus-is pattern matches of 12/36 normal and 9/36 reversed, with no discriminable learning-off patterns; delay/reversal stability remained weak. Two earlier 3/24 totals involved different cues and do not alone prove a common hidden rule. This supplement does not replace exact-retrieval criteria or exhaust non-activation codes. Recommended next work adapts expression to individual patterns, freezes peripherals for new-experience attribution, retains both component relations and broadens contextual/inhibitory/transmission observations.
