A 400-instance dense-cadence control was registered before timing experiments, raising the total from 2,480 to 2,880; it was not selected after timing outcomes. All registered cases completed. All 126,640 frames were checked, 16 old physical baselines and 32 sham cases exactly reproduced, and seven observer-disabled replays retained the same Core hash. Every case retains intensity gain 0.075; Core is unchanged.
