# Embodied communication before human translation

Approved for E53 on 2026-10-06. [中文](../../zh/plans/embodied-communication.md)

Natural-language expression training is paused. Learning, recollection and reasoning do not require human words, but remain distinct experimental questions. A changed response alone cannot establish all three.

Retain complete oceans, finite energy, three archived candidate sources, mutation, within-lineage compatibility and between-lineage predation. Add mouth→OutputBus→approximate acoustic medium→two ears→InputBus. Only anonymous bands are transmitted, never object names, identities or food coordinates. Internal observation remains separate from public physical emission. The existing passive mouth does not establish voluntary speech gating.

Contact-based sharing of newly ingested nutrients/prey energy supplies an opportunity for cooperation, not a prescribed search or joint-hunting policy. This is not a full fluid-acoustic or multi-body capture solver. Test other-directed behavioral effects, acquired pathways and utility across environments before absent-reference, delayed-event and combination tests. Muting preserves self-hearing and emission cost. Scrambling preserves each arrival's power. All causal forks retain the whole habitat.

No Transformer, Core modification or alteration of prior evidence. Preserve the hypothesis that structurally different minds may communicate through shared worldly relations. Useful communication is distinct from human language, general reasoning and subjective experience.
