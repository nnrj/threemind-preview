## Execution and safeguards

Complete inheritance and foundation engineering, then the early cost comparison. Proceed through basic mechanisms, the four criteria, transplantation, frozen readout and prediction before large visual or aquatic tasks. Centered-object naming does not depend on coordination succeeding.

New studies explicitly select `threemind.continuous-memory/6.0`, with frozen source, both implementations and configuration. Retain inverse-logarithmic decay and deletion threshold 0.008; preserve the completed R50 rejection of 0.018. The global creation default remains 5.3.

6.0 offspring inherit persistent connection state and decay age by default; resetting age is a declared control. Fast activity is initialized anew and the child's internal clock starts at zero. Historical birth rules remain unchanged.

Ordinary teaching, questions, correction and review continue the same complete individual. Trainable tasks use temporal observations, increasingly spaced review and targeted correction. Passive mechanism assays need no artificial trainable reader. Preserve Core, peripherals, optimizers, randomness, recent activity, body, mappings, environment and actuators. Register causal copies separately and retain the full reference. Observation, saving, video and host delays do not advance simulated time.

Preregister purpose, expectations, controls, primary measurements and stopping rules. State what each result supports or rejects. Negative or unresolved results are delivered normally, without unlimited sample additions or training extensions.

## Storage, time and stopping

Planning inventory: E had 93.15 GiB free, leaving 68.15 GiB after its 25 GiB reserve. Old E68 used about 18.67 GiB and 6.9 hours for two individuals. R47c used about 145 MiB and 25.3 minutes for 32 small individuals. Old E69 occupied about 37.5 GiB without finishing. These are planning references, not measured 6.0 costs.

Each complete batch budget covers originals, full states, controls, archival and API-readback peaks, analysis/video and the reserve. Calibrate using the first complete stage, without double-counting shared dependencies or hard links. A batch fitting does not establish that all cumulative batches fit.

Start batches that fit. Otherwise choose another feasible batch, then propose only the old-data migration needed to cover the shortfall. Copy, verify hashes/dependencies/API access, register the new location, and only then release verified source copies. Migration tools remain general; no migration is a prerequisite. Run whichever of embodied or visual work fits; defer both if neither fits.

- One scientific host at a time; every-second checks reserve 5 GiB commit, 4 GiB physical RAM, 5 GiB C and 25 GiB E. Pause at complete boundaries.
- If comparable 6.0 execution reaches twice the 5.1 cost after checking host interference, pause and locate the cause. Pause for twice the storage cost with equivalent state/records. Rebudget if measured unit cost exceeds the frozen budget by 50%.
- Preserve and pause on an adverse contradiction with an established mechanism, unverifiable originals, observation effects, answer leakage or a new incomplete state. Do not silently change Core, thresholds or evidence.
- Resume complete pauses only after checking actual processes, complete boundaries, STOP and in-flight state, archiving the exact STOP and rechecking under lock. Do not clear or replay a dead host's in-flight unit.
- After an experiment starts, use the existing quota reset once only when live remaining allowance is at most 1% and the card is available. Purchase nothing.
