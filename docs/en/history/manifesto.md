# Manifesto: a world shaped by experience and sustained activity

[简体中文](../../zh/history/manifesto.md) · [English](manifesto.md)

ThreeMind began from a question rather than a catalogue of knowledge: can a system continuously exposed to the world, changing its connections through local rules, develop its own patterns of response and eventually activity worth calling consciousness? Its ambition is genuine AGI. Omniscience, fluent human language and attractive answers are not prerequisites in its research position. A system that cannot yet do arithmetic but is changed by its experience remains worth studying. These are goals and philosophical commitments, not announcements of completed achievements. [P01 · Hypothesis outline](../consciousness-hypothesis.md) [P02 · Plasticity, environmental selection and language models](../plasticity-environment-and-language-models.md)

## The world enters the network; meaning is not supplied in advance

In anonymous mode, sensors map information from a physical or simulated world into inputs. Core encounters channels, quantities, time and connection states, not “pine nut,” “danger” or the correct answer. Stable mapping rules make contact repeatable; an instance's history and state determine its associations. Readable labels belong to observation instruments and must not become hidden knowledge inside Core.

The project seeks complexity accumulated through simple local interactions across time and space, rather than observer-assigned brain regions, answers or moral preferences. Researchers may construct constrained environments and survival or reproduction conditions, and may conduct explicit interventions. They may not encode a target behavior and then call it emergent. Wiring, sensory scales and initial conditions also require disclosure and controls. [P03 · Kernel evolution principles](../kernel-evolution-principles.md) [P04 · Embodied differentiation](../../zh/embodied-functional-differentiation.md)

## Activity, memory and death

Connections provide a structural basis left by activity and available for reactivation. Consciousness, in the project's terminology, concerns the active process, not a stored file. The researcher explicitly corrected an earlier formulation: **cessation of activation activity means death; cessation of plastic change does not.** Runtime uses a configurable continuous-quiescence grace period. Turning off a sensor is not immediate death, and surviving connections do not establish continuing life. The earlier wording remains in the sources beside its correction. [P02 · Original statement and correction](../plasticity-environment-and-language-models.md) [D08 · Lifecycle discussion](../plans/energy-lifecycle.md)

Forgetting matters as much as remembering. The researcher compares it to a chisel giving a stone its shape and proposes that forgetting may help produce selective attention. That remains a testable hypothesis, not a guarantee that every decay rate is appropriate. Failure of a weak cue, the end of transient activity and eventual removal of structure are different events.

## More than one possible mechanism

Biological brains are a reference route, not the only admissible implementation. Transformer mechanisms, physics, chemistry and other testable models may inform candidates. Documents, protocols and tests must retain each original hypothesis with its original kernel. Borrowing mechanisms must not silently change old experimental conditions. An external language tool's abilities remain attributable to that tool until a separate experiment establishes what Core gains. [P03 · Preserving hypotheses while extending mechanisms](../kernel-evolution-principles.md)

## What this history promises

Explaining a phenomenon through mechanisms does not erase its value. An interesting behavior, in turn, does not supply evidence for every untested ability. This history retains discoveries, mistakes, disputes and unfinished work. It neither removes disappointing results nor turns expectations into facts for encouragement. It preserves the positive verdict under the preregistered autonomy criteria while explaining those criteria's scope. [C01 · Fixed operational criteria](../../../lab/explorations/2026-09-29-internal-activity/autonomy-criteria.en.md)
