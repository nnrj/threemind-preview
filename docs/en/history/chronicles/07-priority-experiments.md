## Reversal has a reproducible local cause

Earlier physical-sense tests reversed with inertial senses and retained the original direction without them. Sixteen untreated replays first reproduced the old complete state hashes exactly. Depletion, fast connection trace, local competition and inhibitory response were then tested in a full four-factor expression-stage experiment.

With full senses, retaining depletion and competition together reversed all four blocks and both directions. Disabling either restored the original direction in every case. Removing inhibition or fast trace alone did not remove this relation. Without inertial senses, all combinations retained the original direction. Silencing existing edges removed the corresponding response of both targets. Competition, previously weak in simple AB material, therefore had a clear causal role with converging sensory input. [E20 · Complete expression-stage combinations](../../../../lab/explorations/2026-10-01-priority-memory/report.en.md)

The decision was to explain reversal rather than disable competition to obtain the observer's preferred direction. Expression-stage interventions do not identify formation-stage causes. Inhibition still affected magnitude and timing; one post-input condition split four original and four reversed cases, a distinction hidden by the mean alone.
