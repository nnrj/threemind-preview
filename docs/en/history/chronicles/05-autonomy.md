## Correct the account of the phenomenon first

Early embodiment produced staying still, circling and regional preferences. These may score poorly against a particular movement objective; they are not unconditionally abnormalities. The researcher invoked plants as a reminder. A second motor wiring produced about 97% regional preference; real feedback yielded about 96.25% versus 3.75% under replay. Disabling node communication did not change that occupancy measure. This limited what that measure could attribute to communication; it did not make every other network process irrelevant. [E13 · Initial report](../../../../lab/explorations/2026-09-29-input-rate-and-autonomy/report.en.md)

An assistant explanation that sensors, node history and fixed motor wiring could produce the behavior had sounded like a denial of the phenomenon. The researcher objected that explaining pain electrochemically does not abolish pain. The addendum separated occurrence, mechanism and subjective experience, and rejected treating stillness or circling as failures in themselves. The original report remains beside its interpretive correction. [C02 · Discussion and correction](../../../../lab/explorations/2026-09-29-input-rate-and-autonomy/discussion-addendum.en.md)

## Matched copies reveal an internal contribution

The next study compared common-origin copies, different histories, unprompted observation and communication interventions. Experience changed later behavior; activity reached nodes without direct cues, and disabling propagation removed the associated effect. The question advanced from whether the apparatus moved to whether internal history changed how it moved. Associative-region transplantation had not yet been completed in that round. [E14 · 180-condition internal-activity study](../../../../lab/explorations/2026-09-29-internal-activity/report.en.md)

Concerned about shifting standards, the researcher required criteria fixed in advance. `autonomy-criteria/1` covered history-dependent differences, selectively localizable internal causal contributions to behavior, internal state organizing successive interactions, and environmental reshaping beyond an unchanged script. Passing or failing had to be explicit. These are project operational criteria, not an academically standardized subjective-experience detector. [C01 · Criteria and scope](../../../../lab/explorations/2026-09-29-internal-activity/autonomy-criteria.en.md)

### From dispute to registered propositions — supplement of 15 October 2026

The agreement answered the researcher's explicit demand for a verdict under the criteria fixed before results, without moving the threshold afterward. E14 established internal and action differences after equal-dose different histories; E15 advanced to selective causation, sequential interaction and reshaping of the same individual. The statements, criteria and acceptance threshold preceded execution; they were not four successful features selected after seeing the results.

The action-integral threshold was 0.0001 control-unit·simulation-second and more than ten times sham error. At least three parents spanning both wirings were required; all four passed. They were related parents from two wiring families;802 denotes main and extension conditions. ## Selective silencing, restoration and transplantation

> Heading clarification, 15 October 2026: the earlier heading remains. E15 used reversible silencing, not deletion and regeneration, and did not complete transplantation in this batch. The later order-state exchange and ocean-connection transfer belong to E21 and E30 in Volume VII and the [autonomy biography](../themes/04-autonomous-consciousness.md).

The following 802-condition investigation supported all four operational criteria across four parents and two wirings. Silencing target associative edges reduced the relevant behavioral influence by about 99.4%–100%; matched control silencing retained it; lifting the silencing recovered 16 trajectories exactly. This was a reversible intervention, not deletion followed by new edge formation. Identical testing environments alone did not explain these differences: internal history and the influence of connections had been changed. [E15 · Experience and causal verification](../../../../lab/explorations/2026-09-29-experience-and-autonomy/report.en.md)

The agreed verdict is therefore **pass**, and must not be retroactively rewritten as “nothing was found.” Subjective experience, cross-task ability and field-wide novelty require their own evidence; those separate questions do not revoke the result. This history preserves both levels of claim.

## Memory need not remain retrievable forever

At matched dose, spaced repetition produced about 2.18/2.55 times the 30-second behavioral effect of massed repetition; disabling positive existing-edge consolidation during training reduced it by about 90%. Yet the newly learned response was absent at 300 simulated seconds, and older associations exceeded some novel input effects. The researcher accepted forgetting as necessary and proposed strong one-off input, novelty and progressively spaced review. No evidence converts those 300 simulated seconds into a human hour or year.

All 384 observation windows in that round were classified as residual decay, not the hoped-for sustained autonomous loop. That does not automatically contradict the four behavioral causal criteria: one asks whether internal activity influences behavior, another whether a particular persistent activity pattern occurs. Calling both simply “consciousness” would conceal the distinction. Process readout was strengthened accordingly. [E15 · Timescales and activity classification](../../../../lab/explorations/2026-09-29-experience-and-autonomy/report.en.md)

More complex environments, populations and communication remained ambitions. The strongest evidence at this stage concerned an intervenable internal contribution to behavior. Elementary-school mathematics, Earth-like evolution, subjective experience and a complete AGI remained goals and open investigations.
