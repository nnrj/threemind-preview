## The formula remained, but the default did not engage it

Calibration found that the strong-input formula had not vanished: it reads the portion above intensity 1, while ordinary text used exactly 1. A strong preset of 4 improved the tested 30-second trace. Novelty had a timing issue; `continuous-memory/2` introduced arrival-window evaluation while retaining the older option. Under the tested facilitation setup, the gain was about 40.66% at intensity 0.4, 2.26% at 0.8, and both groups had no measurable response at 0.2. The largest percentage is not a universal benefit. [E16 · 786-condition calibration](../../../../lab/explorations/2026-09-29-mechanism-calibration/report.en.md)

With weight forgetting disabled, a connection near 0.041 could remain at 300 seconds while a 0.5 cue failed and a cue of 1 responded. “Forgotten” can mean lost structure or structure that the current cue cannot activate. Forgetting, resource recovery and cue strength must be separated.

## Stimulus units make joint observation explicit

The researcher proposed that a model need not start with meanings for time and space but may acquire relations through correlated physical input. Elements, groups, units and provenance were added. Proprioception remains a peripheral on the buses; Core still receives anonymous quantities. MuJoCo scenes supply body-local acceleration and angular velocity without injecting absolute time or answer labels. [D05 · Discussion, decisions and original text](../../plans/stimulus-intensity-and-spacetime.md)

In the 295-instance background audit, fresh random channels were not forcibly connected to an existing network; grouped input formed local islands. In four independently kept-alive copies, 96 weak edges from one-off joint observations faded in about 57.6–68.4 seconds, while the two previously reinforced edges survived to 120 seconds. Real telemetry reuses channels and is not independent random noise. Intensity 0.2 is an input multiplier, not a sum budget across every channel. [E17 · Background, intensity and spacetime material](../../../../lab/explorations/2026-09-30-stimulus-spacetime/report.en.md)

Equal incident quantities with different parameter factorizations produced different consolidation, exposing the extra intensity term rather than establishing physical equivalence. Grouping and shuffling physical observations changed structure but did not establish subjective time. Thirty-one readouts were repeated after source-registration errors; Core hashes matched 31/31, locating the error in observational binding. Failed evidence was retained.

## Retention, transfer and adaptation have different answers

The next 1,536-instance study examined memory, sequence and sensory conditions. Some curricula yielded measurable response at 300 seconds; others did not. Joint-input transfer retained its direction in 48/48 comparisons, while inertial input reversed direction in 144/144. More sensory input was not automatically better; reversal itself deserves explanation. AB/BA did not reliably establish bidirectional discrimination, and the closed-loop slider showed no overall target advantage. That round did not perform actual environmental selection, so it was not a failed test of natural selection. [E18 · Memory, sequence, transfer and adaptation](../../../../lab/explorations/2026-09-30-memory-transfer/report.en.md)

Instrumentation also needed repair. JSONB lost negative-zero signs, causing some replay hashes to differ. Sign reconstruction from retained physical observations concerned 85,925 records; the original database evidence remained. Thirty-two restored trajectories matched exactly, and eight read-only observations preserved hashes. Trajectory analysis, probes and experiment operations began moving from one-off scripts into reusable tools.

## A possible substitute is not established equivalence

The recent 1,246-condition experiment separated the extra intensity term, facilitation and positive learning on existing edges. Removing the extra term while enabling facilitation produced about 132% of another ordinary configuration’s response at 1 second (about 32% higher), but only 25.7% at 30 seconds (about 74.3% lower). That comparator had the term enabled and facilitation disabled. New AB traces still disappeared at 300 seconds. Edge birth, contact and novelty remained active; disabling one consolidation path did not disable all learning. [E19 · Intensity-mechanism comparison](../../../../lab/explorations/2026-09-30-intensity-mechanisms/report.en.md)

Within a fixed state, cues of 0.2, 0.5, 1 and 2 did not show stronger input producing weaker response. Across different histories, an untrained instance could fail with cue 2 while a trained one responded to cue 0.5. History-dependent sensitivity became clearer, without establishing that the extra intensity term can be removed without loss.

Among 582 comparable windows, 172 had positive process integrals despite target absence from the terminal decision readout. This does not convert the old primary endpoint into a success. It shows why questions about an unfolding process need more than a final snapshot. Four observer-disabled comparisons preserved state hashes, checking the read-only boundary.

## Provenance gives a provisional rule its history back

The researcher recalled adding strong-input consolidation to obtain lasting impressions from intense experience. The reference value 1 may have been provisional, and factorization may have addressed saturation. Git initially reached only 24 August. Compiling this history recovered all 314 turns of the first project conversation and found the 25 July request, cap discussion and implementation reply. The goal and bounded-consolidation motive now have contemporary support; the exact numerical choices remain unresolved. The present recommendation is to retain the optional term provisionally and investigate a replacement through simpler local mechanisms, not silently change the formula during this documentation work. [Full provenance and current judgment](../decisions/strong-stimulus.md)

The history pauses here with inspectable local mechanisms, behavioral causal evidence, instrument failures and unmet conditions. The next improvement should retain its reasoning and evidence rather than lose the process that made it possible.
