# Volume III — Teaching language and correcting the experiment

[简体中文](../../../zh/history/chronicles/03-teaching.md) · [English](03-teaching.md)

## What pairing left behind

The first studies after reconstruction returned to the pine-nut and bell pairings. Would the same cue produce a different response after paired experience? Calibration separated vocabulary, encoding seeds, intervals and controls. Twelve new seeds showed a pairing difference in the same direction; swapping the pairing reversed it, and disabling the relevant learning path removed it. The mean difference was about 0.000958. This small but controlled effect supports “pairing changed a measurable response,” not “the instance can answer in words.” [E01 · Initial exploration](../../../../lab/explorations/2026-09-09-memory-relations/report.en.md) [E02 · Calibration and generalization](../../../../lab/explorations/2026-09-09-calibrated-generalization/report.en.md)

Family composition was harder. Exposure to a grandfather template did not establish composition for unseen names. Correct and incorrect relation teaching both produced 4/96 target-leading trials in the calibrated report. Those conditions showed no advantage attributable to correct teaching. One activation of X would not replace the controlled comparison.

## Do not silently change either the lesson or the mapping

The researcher required speakers, question-answer roles and complete short sentences, with substantial repetition before rotating. Early teaching rotated too quickly to satisfy that intended curriculum. Blocked repetition followed. Another run changed sensory normalization to `none` to retain full-width punctuation. The researcher clarified that preserving punctuation while composing lessons did not authorize a different mapping. A subsequent run restored default NFKC and completed punctuation in the family examples. The earlier run remains a separate condition. [E03 · Language scaffolds](../../../../lab/explorations/2026-09-09-language-scaffold-relations/report.en.md) [E04 · Blocked teaching and normalization change](../../../../lab/explorations/2026-09-09-blocked-sentence-teaching/report.en.md) [E05 · Default-mapping rerun](../../../../lab/explorations/2026-09-09-default-mapping-repetition/report.en.md)

This was not merely cosmetic. Stable encoding defines anonymous channel identities. Changing normalization or sentence boundaries changes the world delivered to the network; resulting differences cannot all be attributed to learning capacity.

## From a peak to an unfolding response

The researcher made activation ranking at the end of temporal readout the primary measure, with probes secondary. Chat also had to show sent stimuli, passive output and temporal output in one message stream, directly readable rather than separated by a dropdown. Composite ranking, peak ranking and end-state ranking needed explicit identities. [D02 · Chat and temporal restoration](../../plans/temporal-chat-restoration.md) [E06 · Temporal-primary experiment](../../../../lab/explorations/2026-09-10-temporal-repetition/report.en.md)

Probe pairing effects and failed final temporal answers are compatible observations. One measure compares target activity against controls; the other requires a recognizable ordering at a particular stopping point. Activity may occur without leaving the expected terminal state. Later process-based instruments arose from this gap; they do not retroactively turn the earlier primary outcome into a success.

## Continuing activity, waiting and background input

Unpunctuated pairs, longer intervals and host telemetry entered subsequent controls. One run used 10 simulated seconds between inputs, background intensity 0.25, teaching 0.9 and probes 0.5. Continuous background kept training possible; its no-background control reached the activity-death condition at about 2.19 seconds. Waiting a long time after death is not equivalent to continuing through that interval alive. Robust pairing convergence still did not emerge in the background run. Both the lifecycle boundary and the unmet readout objective remain in the record. [E08 · Unpunctuated pairs](../../../../lab/explorations/2026-09-10-pair-no-punctuation/report.en.md) [E09 · Host-background control](../../../../lab/explorations/2026-09-10-host-background-pair/report.en.md)

The independent time-architecture comparison hit a 180-second worker limit. Its completed portion cannot establish the uncompleted comparison or reject a timing hypothesis. Budgets and failed states became evidence to retain alongside successful runs. [E07 · First-stage time comparison](../../../../lab/explorations/2026-09-10-time-comparison/report.en.md)

This volume leaves two distinct findings: controlled probes detected pairing, while full family composition and reliable textual terminal answers remained unestablished. Curricula, mapping, lifecycle and instruments must all be explicit experimental conditions.
