## An empty folder and a proposal

At 23:07 China Standard Time on 23 July 2026, the researcher opened the earliest recoverable project discussion by saying that BlueBox was an empty folder. He had an AGI idea and asked which languages could build it, including whether assembly was appropriate. This is the documented project starting point. The conversation was created at 23:05; that metadata is neither the first utterance nor proof that the idea had never existed earlier. [Founding and naming records](../sources/origin-records.md)

At 00:30 on 24 July he presented the founding hypothesis: perceived color, self-boundaries, dreams and waking experience concern relations shaped inside an individual through stimulation. Connections should change with experience; forgetting matters alongside consolidation. Models, tools and an interface should make this research possible without encoding answers. At 00:45 he clarified that associative contact also counts as stimulation and that local rules should produce collective effects, as in the Game of Life, without a source-free intrinsic drive. The source volume retains the full statement and distinguishes its early death wording from the later activity-based correction. [Founding statement and clarification](../sources/founding-statement.md)

Later that day, literal text stored as node content was challenged: language was to be a carrier, not semantic knowledge built into anonymous nodes. Core should resemble an interpreter/runtime, models should retain their attributes and state, and CLI and UI should provide equivalent operations. The original BlueBox and bluebox-next were separated to preserve a comparison. Preserving old kernels was therefore a principle before the September rewrite.

### Supplement, 15 October 2026: how the conjecture became an experimental question

The July proposal was more than a network able to answer: sustained activity and local plasticity would form an individual with a history, so identical present input could produce different responses after different experience. On 29 September this became four explicit criteria—history-dependent differences, internal causation, sequential interaction and environmental reshaping—which E15 passed. The origin chapter records the question, and the verification chapter its result; July's proposal is not retroactively an experiment, nor should later success remain buried in engineering appendices. The [autonomy biography](../themes/04-autonomous-consciousness.md) brings together the dispute, preregistration and data; [Volume V](05-autonomy.md) follows the contemporary sequence.

## Strong input, local rules and a name

At 14:10 on 25 July the researcher explicitly requested that strong stimulation leave harder-to-forget traces, through changed connection attributes rather than programmatic protection. The ensuing discussion proposed consolidation impulses with saturation or a cap so one extreme input would not create absolutely permanent memory. An implementation reply in the 14:15 turn described first exposure, repetition, intensity and local plasticity. **This documents the motive; it does not establish why the later formula chose exactly 1 as its intensity reference or 0.075 as its coefficient.** [Three contemporary exchanges](../sources/strong-stimulus-dialogue.md)

The 26 July discussion challenged mechanisms that scored novelty or importance before filtering the world for the instance. Novelty should depend on existing local neighborhoods, new links and actual associative contact. Repeated-input retention regressions between old and new implementations were already being investigated, predating the larger second-generation rewrite. [Anonymity, forgetting and non-intervention](../sources/origin-records.md)

At 13:17 on 31 July, ThreeMind was proposed in relation to the researcher's ThreeJSON and ThreeBox projects. At 13:36 he authorized renaming the actively developed bluebox-next to threemind while retaining the old BlueBox. This history therefore records **23 July as the documented project start and 31 July as the naming date**. The current Git root of 24 August is a later repository boundary, not a birthday. [Naming sources](../sources/origin-records.md)

## Where the triangle came from

On 25 July, a minimal body projected weak background activity into three recurring anonymous channels: slow variation, fast variation and drift. A roughly triangular arrangement and recurring structural fingerprint appeared. Core had no instruction to draw a triangle: three recurring channels constrained which receptors and connections could form.

The 27 July investigation connected this observation to dense global coupling. Few fixed channels repeatedly reinforced the same local pairs. The changing environmental field was consequently separated from the optional body filter. “Continuous stimulation” was already too coarse a description: diversity, identity reuse and sensory bottlenecks mattered. The issue returned later in the comparison between random background and physical telemetry. [N02 · Sensory constraints and saturation](../../../../lab/en/002_2026-07-27_0000_sensory-constraints-and-global-saturation.md)

## Text helped observation and created opportunities for misreading

The 28 July note describes repeated short inputs touching similar anonymous subsets. With external labels, some fragments resembled imitation or replies. But the diagnostic mapping then associated a character's leading UTF-8 byte with a channel. Characters sharing that byte could overwrite one another's displayed labels. Anonymous identities, observation labels and literal-character controls therefore had to remain distinct. [N03 · Recurring signatures and label collisions](../../../../lab/en/003_2026-07-28_0000_recurrent-v3-signatures-and-readable-output.md)

A long-text request also stalled service and graph updates. Too much work had been scheduled in one processing round, motivating ordered frames, backpressure and source metadata. Submitting a long text at once did not mean all its sensory content should arrive simultaneously. Input delivery became an explicit part of experimental method. [N04 · Temporal framing](../../../../lab/en/004_2026-07-28_0000_temporal-framing-of-long-textual-stimulation.md)

## Pine nuts, bells and an unfinished family inference

Notes dated 1 August describe short probes re-entering learned local structures. Adding another pairing also produced mixed responses. Recurrence during background input was not activity with all input absent. Note 005 was written retrospectively on 3 August and lacks complete parameters and tick traces; original screenshots cited by note 006 were unavailable to the export interface. These observations remain worth retaining, with their missing evidence made visible. [N05 · Repeatable convergence mapping](../../../../lab/en/005_2026-08-01_0000_v3-probe-stimulus-convergence-mapping.md) [N06 · Relation probes and missing attachments](../../../../lab/en/006_2026-08-01_0055_relation-probe-recurrence-and-over-association.md)

On 2–3 August, observation moved from individual snapshots to time windows. The displays “乙·铃·铛·是” and “乙·是·铃·铛” used different ranking algorithms. Their difference could reflect persistent internal competition, instrument ordering, or both. It was not an established grammar-correction result. Other notes distinguished fast activity from slower structure, moving hotspots under long input, and coexisting local responses to several relations. [N07 · Temporal evidence](../../../../lab/en/007_2026-08-02_1246_from-snapshot-readout-to-temporal-evidence.md) [N08 · Candidate word order and competing explanations](../../../../lab/en/008_2026-08-03_2152_v3-candidate-word-order-convergence.md) [N09 · Fast and slow scales](../../../../lab/en/009_2026-08-03_2202_fast-slow-timescales-and-evidence-chain.md) [N10 · Migrating activity](../../../../lab/en/010_2026-08-03_2239_migrating-activity-under-a-continuous-long-text-stream.md) [N11 · Endpoint response clusters](../../../../lab/en/011_2026-08-03_2327_relation-endpoint-reactivation-and-response-clusters.md) [N12 · Coexisting relations](../../../../lab/en/012_2026-08-04_0014_coexisting-local-structures-for-multiple-relations.md)

On 4 August, a second family received two father relations but no grandfather answer. Probes recovered trained father relations and briefly contacted an older grandfather template, without stable grandfather convergence. This explicit negative result posed a question that persisted: what separates reactivating shared experience from composing a relation for new entities? [N13 · No transitive relation](../../../../lab/en/013_2026-08-04_0041_no-transitive-relation-and-analogy-contact.md)

These records establish an early concern with sensory constraints, structure, activity and observation. They do not supply a complete parameter set with which every early observation can now be reproduced exactly. Later experiments must not be used to fabricate missing early data.
