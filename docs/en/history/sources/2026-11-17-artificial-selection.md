# 17 November 2026: A new test of artificial selection and intergenerational retention

## Author's requirements and authorization

In this conversation, the author required measuring a candidate parent's own behavior before selecting it for reproduction. Assessment copies must come from complete snapshots, not from the birth transformation. The new experiment must include opposite selection directions and random selection, compare all candidates across generations, and distinguish retention from progressive strengthening.

The author then required that the two regions remain unchanged when testing descendants, while allowing different starting positions, and explicitly authorized a new artificial-selection experiment using Core 6.3. During execution, the author requested continued work and allowed layout change as an additional control.

This authorization covers only the new experiment and its layout controls. ## Historical checks and interpretation

[E13's original protocol](../../../../lab/explorations/2026-09-29-input-rate-and-autonomy/selection.en.md) assessed independently born copies. Its final tests crossed four new starting positions with normal and swapped layouts, and separate controls replayed old actions in the swapped layout or changed patterns during a run. Layout changes therefore do not, by themselves, invalidate that experiment. Its findings retain their original scope: parents were selected using offspring performance, which does not directly establish a correspondence between the parent's own behavior and that of its descendants.

## New decision and evidence

AS63-01 uses the actual threemind.continuous-memory/6.3 implementation in 16 independent populations, three selection conditions, and three generations. Complete-state assessment copies measure candidates; only selected original candidates produce offspring through threemind.birth/2. The primary experiment keeps regions fixed, separates selection and held-out starting positions, and retains every candidate. Bidirectional retention, advantage over random selection, progressive strengthening, and the contribution of inherited persistent states are judged separately.

AS63-02 was registered separately afterward. Every G2 candidate receives swapped-layout closed-loop and recorded-action controls, paired with its saved original-layout results. This supplement does not change AS63-01's scores or criteria.

This source distinguishes the author's requirements, verified historical methods, and new designs. Registration does not establish completion, and apparatus consistency does not establish behavioral findings.

## 17 November 2026: Primary study completed; parent–offspring agreement without selection gain

AS63-01 completed 16 independent populations and 5,056 stages. Audits verified all 832 developed individuals, 4,224 complete-state assessment copies, 384 births, and 384 uninherited controls. No deaths or replacement materials occurred. Assessment measured the candidate's own complete state; only formal descendants underwent birth. This aligned the procedure with the newly specified scientific question.

Some existing regional tendencies continued in descendants. For region 0, 10/10 and 12/12 offspring of qualifying selected parents retained the tendency across the two transmissions; for region 1, the counts were 40/44 and 40/40. However, no population supplied qualifying selected G0 parents for both opposite directions. Consequently, 0/16 populations met both directions across both transmissions, below the registered 4/16 requirement. This is not a finding that all existing tendencies failed to persist.

The G2 gains over random selection were 0.042 and 0.127 percentage points. Progressive strengthening and the benefit of inherited states over matched blank-Core controls also missed their registered criteria. All eight mean-difference 95% upper limits were below 1 percentage point. Two populations with occupancy near 50% were continuously circling; their trajectories and behavior were retained. Parent–offspring agreement was observed, but the registered selection gains were not. Similarity alone did not establish a contribution from inherited Core states. The primary study ends as a valid negative result under its original criteria, without Core changes or additional generations. The separately registered layout control is still running and will be reported separately.
