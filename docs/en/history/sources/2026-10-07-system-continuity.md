## Clarification

> Continuity is for one consciousness plus one peripheral; different control groups may differ. Treat the experimental subject as a simulated robot: ThreeMind is its brain, and adapters including the Transformer motor center are its brain functions, nerves and limbs. They form one system. Training with one set and testing with another does not match. The motor center learns that individual's internal patterns; without training together they cannot be assumed to work together. Consult our histories and prior reports.

> Future experiments must not break dynamics, violate ThreeMind's assumptions or established system consistency. If our changes cause the system itself to cease to be the system under study, what are we testing? Continue.
