## Contemporary decision

E60 demonstrated internal-connection effects on eye/hand commands without reliable target control. The new question is whether usable bodily experience is missing before instruction. Inherited foundations, body calibration, stable skills and conceptual relations are training dependencies, not predefined semantic Core regions. [E60](../../../../lab/explorations/2026-10-07-eye-hand-learning/report.en.md)

E61 preserves the whole foveated view, eye position/velocity, visible hand and physical background. Four colored toys move, rebound and sound on contact; stereo bands, touch and mouth/self-sound feedback are added. Mechanics and acoustics are explicit reduced-order approximations. A fixed anonymous activity wiring and separately recorded aimless motor variation drive preschool exploration. Variation must not be described as Core intention.

Blank sources explore for180seconds before identical teaching, compared with equal-time quiet exposure and identical actuator replay with structural learning temporarily disabled. Measure coverage and real contacts before inferring a need for innate preferences. A later selection experiment could develop preferences in this body. Whole-body locomotion, NPC talk, the language adapter and energy rewards are independent future conditions, not hidden additions. ## Evidence boundaries

[Spontaneous movement and sensorimotor self-organization](https://www.nature.com/articles/nature01719), [staged robotic learning](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/robotics-and-ai/articles/10.3389/frobt.2018.00110/full), and [DAgger corrections in visited states](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v15/ross11a.html) inform the route; they do not validate ThreeMind's formulas. The particular parenting study recalled by the user has not been identified. The claim that absolute pitch necessarily requires infancy is not adopted as a prerequisite: [adult pitch-learning evidence](https://link.springer.com/article/10.3758/s13423-024-02620-2) requires a more qualified statement. Preserving the original analogy does not turn it into an established project result.

## Later training-method memorandum

Earlier in this phase, the user proposed separating environmental selection for inherited predispositions, energy rewards for conditioned behavior, and teacher-led training for goals and reasoning, explicitly deferring reward experiments. The [Chinese source](../../../zh/history/sources/2026-10-07-body-exploration.md) preserves the full original quotation.

Editorial assessment: these are separately testable training routes, not proof that each ability has one exclusive origin. Exploration and instruction can both contribute to body learning. Energy constraints, environmental selection and teacher feedback should have distinct registrations; a training method alone does not establish or exclude reasoning. E61 adds no rewards, starvation selection or Core preferences.
