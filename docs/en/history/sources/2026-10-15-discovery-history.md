## Commission and scope

On 15 October the user requested a fuller history of important conjectures, experiments, verification and discoveries, integrated into the chronological chapters and supplemented with concentrated thematic biographies. The exact Chinese request is preserved in the [Chinese source entry](../../../zh/history/sources/2026-10-15-discovery-history.md); this paragraph is a summary, not a quotation.

Volume V already mentioned the four criteria and their passage, but did not sufficiently develop the originating argument, preregistration and item-by-item evidence. Later discoveries were scattered through long appendices, while the main entry still listed nine chronological volumes. This supplement repairs both narrative coverage and discoverability. It does not claim that the old history never mentioned autonomy, nor treat one existing sentence as an adequate account.

15 October is the compilation date. Dates below belong to the original discussions or reports; revision dates associated with later storage migration are not new experiment dates. Original reports, scores and criteria remain intact. This work uses readable reports, designs, source excerpts and existing figure provenance; it does not recompute large trajectories or rerun individuals.

## Clarifications to earlier shorthand

1. **The autonomy verdict has a defined referent:** E15's four preregistered criteria and contemporary passing conclusion. This history does not introduce a new criterion or retroactively judge it by later harder tasks.
2. **Preference, ablation and transplantation form a cross-study research line.** E13 occupancy remained high with communication off; E15 removed an experience-dependent action difference; E21 exchanged order-response state; E30 transferred part of an action pattern. Volume V's old heading is retained with an adjacent dated clarification: E15 used reversible silencing, while transplantation came later.
3. **Parents, copies and questions are different units.** E15's four related parents/two wiring families, E30's related-source observations, E56's questions and E68's one complete individual per version remain distinct denominators.
4. **Development and causal copies retain their actual historical procedures.** E14/E15 had declared birth/intervention copies; E56 retains its calibration conditions; E68 preserves the complete individual across ordinary stages and questions. Later rules do not rewrite earlier events.
5. **Observations, interpretations and pending hypotheses remain distinct.** Turning, intake and descendant identity are recorded events; stable inherited pursuit skill is a further attribution. E69 waiting consolidation and emergent functional regions have no completed causal acceptance supplied by this supplement. These boundaries do not diminish the measured positive findings.
