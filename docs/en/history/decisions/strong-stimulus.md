## What is being discussed

The additional consolidation term is `consolidation_intensity_gain × max(0, frame_intensity - 1)`, with historical default coefficient 0.075. It contributes to an impulse affecting connection weight and stability. It does not rewrite text or favor a semantic class. The complete impulse also depends on effective drive, available-energy fraction and local plasticity and is bounded; this fragment is not the complete equation. It appears around lines 813–820 of `core/threemind/runtime.py` in root commit `97d8592`, alongside intensity tests. [Git evidence](../sources/git-register.md)

This is a human-chosen, content-neutral numerical rule. Content neutrality does not establish a physical derivation. Equal incident quantities factored differently can still consolidate differently, revealing an independent role for the source intensity parameter.

## Three judgments

**Retain it provisionally as an optional, disableable research rule.** The latest evidence does not establish equivalence after removal across relevant conditions. Some facilitation-enabled configurations without the term exceed other configurations, but that does not authorize deleting all historical paths. Old instances retain their identity. This history changes neither Core nor creation defaults and records a recommendation rather than a new implementation authorization.

**A better candidate could arise from local states on different timescales.** Activity accumulation, calcium-like variables, synaptic tags and slower stabilization may be compared with current drive, facilitation, depletion and inhibition. Candidates need isolated versions, equal-input controls, ablation and restoration. Removing one arbitrary threshold must not smuggle in a content-based judgment.

**Persistent effects of intense experience have real-world reference points.** Emotional-memory pharmacology, synaptic tagging and calcium-based plasticity provide different levels of evidence for state-dependent persistence. They do not validate ThreeMind's specific formula or guarantee that stronger input is always beneficial.

- Cahill et al. (1994) found that β-adrenergic blockade affected long-term memory for an emotional story differently from a neutral one. This supports modulation of retention, not direct equivalence with numerical input intensity. [Original abstract](https://www.nature.com/articles/371702a0)
- Frey and Morris (1997) examined weak/strong stimulation and protein-synthesis conditions, relating local tags, previous activity and sustained potentiation. [Original abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9020359/)
- Shouval, Bear and Cooper (2002) modeled several induction conditions for bidirectional plasticity using calcium-related local variables. This is a reference for producing different effects through simpler state interactions. [Original paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC125058/)

## 2026-10-01 continuation: retain the option, enabled by default

The researcher explicitly decided to retain the extra strong-input term as an optional kernel parameter, enabled by default. Any later default-off decision awaits experimental results and the researcher’s decision. The existing interface is `intensity_gain`: default 0.075, zero disables the extra term while ordinary contact consolidation remains. This task preserves values and dynamics and clarifies the Chinese/English configuration descriptions. Experimental switches affect new copies only; no second boolean state is introduced that could contradict the numeric parameter.

The earlier provisional recommendation is therefore now an explicit decision for this phase, not authorization to remove the term. Follow-up proceeds through readout calibration, retention, facilitation from creation, mechanism localization and order information, with prospective registrations and shared budgets.
