# ThreeMind trademark and brand policy

[简体中文](TRADEMARK.zh-CN.md) · 2026-11-24

## Scope

Dongxu Liu has applied to register the **ThreeMind** trademark. This policy covers the ThreeMind name and the logos that identify the official project. The project publishes its [public code preview on GitHub](https://github.com/nnrj/threemind-preview).

The [MIT License](LICENSE) permits use, modification and redistribution of the project’s original software, including commercial use, subject to its notice requirements. This trademark policy concerns how users identify the source of a product or service. All permissions granted under the applicable software or content license, and all uses permitted by law, remain available.

## Referring to ThreeMind

You may use the ThreeMind name without separate permission for the following purposes.

- Cite the project or its preprint, teach or discuss its methods, compare it with other work, and publish independent results, including criticism and unsuccessful replications. You may use the official logo to identify ThreeMind in reporting or teaching.
- Identify ThreeMind as the upstream project for a fork or a modified release. When you publish a modified version under a name that refers to ThreeMind, label it as an independent release and identify its maintainer.
- Describe software or services that use ThreeMind or are compatible with it, including commercial offerings. For example, an independent publisher can call an adapter “Example Adapter for ThreeMind” and identify itself as the publisher.

## Uses requiring written permission

Obtain written permission in advance to use the ThreeMind name or an official logo as the primary brand of your own product, service or merchandise, or to present an offering as approved, sponsored or certified by the project. This also applies to names and branding for domains, accounts, package publishers and software distributions that readers could reasonably mistake for an official ThreeMind channel. Identify the actual source and any affiliation accurately.
